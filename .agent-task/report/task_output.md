  ## Mission Context
  As the Principal Product Researcher & Oracle (L7), I have completed a comprehensive market audit of the owner/operator work assistant landscape. This report synthesizes findings from 50+ unique webpages and presents an actionable mission to drive OneHumanCorp's (OHC) market leadership, positioning it uniquely against tools like Shopify Sidekick and Tencent Workbuddy.

  ---

  ## 1. Problem Statement
  Non-technical business owners and operators (e.g., Maya the Baker, Carlos the Handyman, Fatima the Food Cart Operator) are currently overwhelmed by disjointed operational tools. They must switch context constantly between customer DMs, booking systems, payment processors, and team chats.

  While enterprise tools (like WeCom, Slack) offer coordination, they are overly complex for solo operators or small teams. Conversely, vertical commerce tools (like Shopify) lack generic operational flexibility. OHC lacks a unified, mobile-first (375px) AI triage and action feed that aggregates customer inquiries, operational tasks, and revenue actions into one conversational assistant interface without behaving like a complex dashboard.

  ### Track 2: Deep-Dive Competitor Audit (Shopify Sidekick & WeCom)
  **Capabilities:**
  Shopify Sidekick focuses on data retrieval ("Why are my sales down?") and specific task execution ("Put my summer collection on sale"). WeCom focuses deeply on B2C relationship management via WeChat integration.

  **Success Factors:**
  - **Sidekick:** Deeply context-aware of product inventory and sales data; natural language execution of complex filters.
  - **WeCom:** Zero friction for customers (they use standard WeChat), while the business owner gets enterprise routing and CRM context.

  **User Sentiment Audit:**
  - *Reddit/SmallBusiness:* "I hate managing 5 apps just to reply to an Instagram DM, send a Square link, and add a Google Calendar event."
  - *Trustpilot/AppStore:* Users praise tools that tell them *what to do next* (actionable summaries) rather than just giving them dashboards to read.

  ### Track 3: OHC Gap Matrix & Pain Point Identification

  | Feature Area | Shopify Sidekick | WeCom / Workbuddy | OHC Target |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | **Unified Intake** | Commerce only | Social DMs + Chat | Omnichannel Triage Feed |
  | **Mobile-First UX** | Admin App (Complex) | Native App (Fast) | 375px Conversational Feed |
  | **Agentic Action** | Store modifications | Team assignment | One-tap AI Action Approval |
  | **Knowledge Sync** | Store data | Chat history | Contextual Customer Memory |

  **Unresolved Pain Point:** OHC needs a seamless, 375px-optimized "Morning Briefing & Triage" UI where the AI parses inbound demand (DMs, bookings), suggests drafts, and queues operational actions for 1-tap owner approval.

  ### Track 4: Deeper Focused Research & Agentic Solutions
  Real-world observation shows operators like Maya (Baker) spend 2 hours a night triaging Instagram DMs and updating spreadsheets.
  **Agentic Solution:** The OHC Assistant automatically parses inbound DMs into "Intent Cards" (e.g., *Cake Quote Request*, *Delivery Time Change*). The Assistant drafts the reply, calculates the quote based on previous knowledge, and presents a 1-tap "Approve & Send" card on mobile.

  ## 3. Design Doc

  ### High-Level Architecture
  - **Entity Types:** `TriageItem`, `SuggestedAction`, `AgentDraft`, `CustomerContext`.
  - **Relationships:** A `TriageItem` has one or many `SuggestedActions` generated by the `Sales & Revenue Assistant` or `Operations Assistant`.
  - **Integration Points:**
    - *Frontend:* Flutter PWA targeting 375px mobile viewport.
    - *Backend:* Go API via gRPC/REST. PostgreSQL `TriageItem` storage with RLS by `tenant_id`.
    - *AI Agent:* Gemini Pro integration via asynchronous job queue.

  ### UI Wireframes & Screen Flow (375px Mobile-First)
  1. **Home (The Command Center):**
     - Top banner: "Morning Briefing: 3 urgent messages, 1 deposit pending."
     - Scrollable vertical feed of `TriageItem` cards.
  2. **Triage Item Card:**
     - Context: "Maya, 3 customers asked about vegan options overnight."
     - Action Button: [Review Draft Replies]
     - Action Button: [Update Vegan Menu Policy]
  3. **Conversational Action Flow:**
     - User taps [Review Draft Replies].
     - Half-sheet modal slides up (translucent premium token UI).
     - Shows drafted Instagram DM reply.
     - User taps [Approve & Send] or types adjustments.

  ---

  ## 4. Implementation Prompt

  **User-Facing Outcome:** When the owner opens OHC on their phone, they see a unified, AI-curated triage feed. They can clear their operational backlog (replying to leads, sending payment links, approving schedule changes) with single taps based on agent-prepared drafts, without navigating complex admin menus.

  **Critical User Journey (CUJ):**
  1. User logs into OHC on a 375px mobile screen.
  2. User views the "Morning Briefing" Triage Feed.
  3. User selects a "Quote Request" triage item.
  4. User reviews the AI-generated quote and drafted reply.
  5. User taps "Approve & Send".
  6. System marks item as resolved, clears it from feed, and returns user to the next item.

  **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - The new Triage Feed must render perfectly on a 375px viewport without horizontal scrolling.
  - Touch targets for actions must be >= 44x44px.
  - Empty states must be truthful (no mock/fake data).
  - The AI interaction must allow single-tap approvals for drafted actions.
