issue_title: "Implement Unified Conversational Analytics & Actionable Insights Mesh"
issue_description: |
  **Problem Statement:** Small business owners need an "Insight-to-Action" model where data is translated into proactive, plain-language conversational briefings with 1-tap resolutions, eliminating the need to actively monitor complex dashboards.

  **Research Report:**
  - Evaluated Shopify, Wix, Squarespace, and GoDaddy analytics.
  - Identified an industry gap: existing platforms present data, but they do not present *decisions*.
  - A mobile-first platform must push actionable insights directly to the user (e.g., "Good morning, Maya. You hit $1,200 in revenue this week. You have 3 pending cake deposits. [Send Reminders]").

  **Next Steps:**
  - Design the data ingestion mesh connecting the Universal Ledger, Inventory Mesh, and Omnichannel Inbox.
  - Integrate an Insights AI Agent to synthesize daily metrics into localized, conversational language.
  - Build macOS-style Translucent Glass "Insight Cards" on 375px viewports.
  - See `docs/research/[architecture]_unified_conversational_analytics_and_insights_mesh.md` for full design and architecture.
