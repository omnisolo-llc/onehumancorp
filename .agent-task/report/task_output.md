  # Research Report: Voice-Native Autonomous Agent for Missed Call & Lead Recovery

  ## Problem Statement
  Small business owners in field services (e.g., Carlos the handyman, 42) operate primarily from their trucks and job sites. They are often unable to answer their phones while working on a job. Every missed call is a potential lost lead to a competitor. Traditional platforms provide unified inboxes for text, but they fail to address the primary acquisition channel for service professionals: the phone call. A simple voicemail system doesn't convert leads or provide instant quotes.

  ## Research Report
  **Findings & Competitive Analysis:**
  - **Traditional Platforms (Housecall Pro, Jobber):** Offer good scheduling and invoicing but rely on standard voicemail or expensive human answering services for missed calls.
  - **AI Answering Services (Slang.ai, Goodcall):** Standalone services that answer calls but lack deep integration with the business owner's core scheduling, pricing, and CRM system.
  - **OHC Opportunity:** By leveraging the OHC unified context (tenant availability, service catalog, and past customer history), we can introduce a Voice-Native Operations Agent. When a call is missed, this agent can autonomously call back the lead or immediately intercept the call, converse in natural language, gather job details, provide an estimated quote using the service catalog, and propose a booking slot on the owner's calendar.

  ## Design Doc
  ### Architecture Diagram
  ```mermaid
  graph TD
      A[Inbound Call / Missed Call Event] -->|Twilio/Plivo Webhook| B(Voice Integration Gateway)
      B --> C[Speech-to-Text Stream]
      C --> D[Voice Agent Coordinator]
      D -->|Query Schedule/Pricing| E[OHC Unified Graph DB]
      D --> F[LLM Intent & Response Generation]
      F --> G[Text-to-Speech Stream]
      G -->|Audio Response| B
      D -->|Generate Lead/Quote| H[Action Required Queue]
      H --> I[Mobile App Feed 375px]
  ```

  ### Mobile UX Flow (375px First)
  - **Home Feed (Mobile):** Top card shows: "🚨 New Lead Captured: Sink Repair (Voice Agent)".
  - **Interaction:** Tapping the card reveals a summary of the call ("Customer called about a leaking sink, agent quoted $150-$200 based on standard pricing and offered Tuesday at 2 PM"). It includes a 30-second audio snippet of the crucial part of the conversation and a full transcript.
  - **Action:** Primary buttons: "Confirm Booking & Send Deposit Link" and "Call Customer Back".
  - **Visual Design:** Clean glassmorphism cards. Clear status indicators for AI-generated confidence scores.

  ### AI Agent Integration Points
  - **Voice Agent (The Receptionist):** Interfaces with telephony APIs (e.g., Twilio). Uses low-latency speech-to-text and text-to-speech. Contextually aware of the business's open calendar slots and service pricing constraints.
  - **Operations Agent (The Manager):** Receives the parsed intent from the Voice Agent and creates the pending booking and quote in the OHC system, placing the approval card in the owner's feed.

  ### Key Design Decisions
  - **Low Latency Voice:** Utilizing streaming LLM interactions to keep conversational latency below 500ms to maintain natural dialogue.
  - **Asynchronous Handoff:** The AI handles the synchronous phone call, but the interaction is converted into an asynchronous action card for the business owner, respecting their busy, offline nature.
  - **Seamless Context:** The Voice Agent must be injected with the current day's context (e.g., "Carlos is running 30 mins late today, so don't book anything before 3 PM").

  ## Implementation Prompt
  **User-Facing Outcome:** As a handyman, when I am under a sink and miss a call, my OHC assistant answers the call, understands the customer's plumbing issue, gives a ballpark estimate, and secures a tentative booking. When I check my phone 20 minutes later, I see a clear summary card and just need to tap "Confirm" to lock in the job and request a deposit.
  **CUJ & Acceptance Criteria:**
  1. A simulated inbound call webhook is processed by the Voice Integration Gateway.
  2. The Voice Agent Coordinator successfully retrieves availability and pricing context from the tenant DB.
  3. The system processes a simulated speech intent (e.g., "I need a leaky faucet fixed tomorrow").
  4. The Agent generates an appropriate conversational response and creates a pending Quote and Booking record.
  5. An Action Card is generated and surfaced in the tenant's Mobile App Feed.
  6. Provide Playwright E2E tests: A user logs in on a 375px viewport, views the new lead card generated by the voice agent, reviews the transcript/summary, and taps "Confirm Booking", which successfully updates the booking state in the backend.
