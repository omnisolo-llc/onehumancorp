issue_title: "AI-Powered Autonomous Service Estimator & Quoting Engine"
issue_description: |
  ## 1. Problem Statement
  Service-based small business owners (e.g., Carlos the Handyman, Nora the Agency Principal) lose hours every week driving to sites just to provide estimates, or manually drafting proposals for client intake. Existing quoting tools (like Jobber or basic invoicing apps) require the owner to manually input line items, guess material costs, and build the quote from scratch. They lack an integrated AI that can ingest a photo or a vague customer description (e.g., "my sink is leaking under the cabinet") and autonomously draft a professional, itemized quote.

  ## 2. Research Report
  - **Market Context**: Platforms like Jobber and Housecall Pro dominate field service, but their quoting tools are highly manual and require a steep learning curve. General CRM tools like HubSpot provide quote templates but lack contextual understanding of physical services. Emerging AI agents like 11x.ai focus on software sales rather than local service or physical quotes.
  - **The OHC Opportunity**: By integrating a multimodal quoting engine natively alongside our unified inbox and operations agent, OHC can eliminate the need for manual quote drafting. The Sales/Operations Agent can use image recognition and historical pricing data to generate accurate estimates instantly.
  - **Competitor Gaps**:
    - *Jobber/Housecall Pro*: Require manual line-item entry; poor multimodal (image-to-quote) capabilities.
    - *Square Invoices*: Good payment flow but relies entirely on manual input.
    - *Shopify*: Irrelevant for service-based quoting.

  ## 3. Design Doc
  ### Data Model (PostgreSQL)
  - `QuoteRequest`: Intake entity containing customer details, problem description, and attached images/media.
  - `QuoteLineItem`: Individual line items generated by AI (e.g., Labor, Materials) linked to a Service catalog.
  - `Estimate`: The formal document linking `QuoteRequest`, `QuoteLineItem`, and `Customer`, with states (Draft, Sent, Approved, Rejected).

  ### AI Integration
  - **Sales/Operations Agent**: Uses multimodal LLM (Gemini Vision) to analyze uploaded images of the workspace (e.g., broken pipe, empty room for painting) combined with the user's natural language description. Cross-references the owner's past estimates and standard service rates to draft an itemized estimate.
  - **Customer Success Agent**: Follows up on sent, unapproved estimates after 48 hours to negotiate or address customer concerns.

  ### Mobile UX Flow (375px)
  1. **Customer View (Intake)**: A mobile-friendly form where customers describe the issue and upload photos.
  2. **Owner View (Draft Review)**: The owner receives a push notification: "New Estimate Drafted for John Doe". Opening it shows a clean card UI with the AI-generated itemized list. The owner can tap to edit quantities, adjust pricing, or hit "Approve & Send".
  3. **Customer View (Approval & Deposit)**: Customer receives the approved quote via SMS/Email, reviews the professional estimate, and clicks a button to pay the deposit (via Stripe).
