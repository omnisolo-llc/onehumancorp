  # [Architecture] Autonomous Invisible Bookkeeping & Tax Engine

  ## Problem Statement
  Small business owners (like Maya the baker or Carlos the handyman) operate entirely on cash flow and lack the time, expertise, or desire to act as accountants. Traditional accounting platforms (like QuickBooks or Xero) fail the "grandmother test," forcing users to understand double-entry bookkeeping, chart of accounts, and manual reconciliation. Come tax time, they face a nightmare of lost receipts, scattered invoices, and unknown tax liabilities.

  They need an invisible financial engine that captures every transaction, maps it to a unified ledger, sets aside tax obligations, and generates plain-language financial health summaries automatically, without requiring them to ever see a spreadsheet.

  ## Research Report
  - **Market Context**: Platforms like Shopify and Wix offer basic financial reporting, but rely on third-party integrations for proper accounting. These integrations introduce complexity and steep learning curves.
  - **The Gap**: Users want a system that acts as a "silent co-founder" managing the finances in the background. Current solutions are tools; we need an agent.
  - **Key Use Cases**: Instant expense capture via multimodal vision (snapping a photo of a receipt), auto-categorization based on tax rules, and proactive tax reservation on every sale.

  ## Design Doc

  ### Architecture Diagram (Mermaid.js)
  ```mermaid
  sequenceDiagram
      participant User as Small Business Owner (Mobile)
      participant VisionAgent as AI Vision Agent
      participant Ledger as OHC Ledger (Postgres)
      participant TaxAgent as AI Tax & Finance Agent
      participant Bank as OHC Treasury Wallet

      User->>VisionAgent: Upload Receipt Image / Forward Email
      VisionAgent->>VisionAgent: Extract Vendor, Total, Date, Tax, Line Items
      VisionAgent->>TaxAgent: Pass Extracted Data
      TaxAgent->>Ledger: Match with Bank Feed & Categorize Expense
      TaxAgent->>Bank: Auto-Sweep Tax Reserve (on revenue)
      TaxAgent->>User: Weekly Plain-Language Financial Briefing
  ```

  ### UI Wireframes & Mobile UX Flow (375px)
  - **Expense Capture**: A single floating action button `[ 📸 Scan Receipt ]`. No manual entry forms by default.
  - **Financial Briefing**: A card in the Action Feed displaying a plain-language summary: "You made $500 this week. $100 was safely set aside for taxes. You spent $50 on supplies."
  - **Tax Toggle**: A simple switch in the settings: "Auto-Save for Taxes (e.g., 20%)" to enable automatic sweeps.

  ### Key Design Decisions
  - **Zero Manual Entry**: All expense inputs are vision-first. Manual entry is hidden behind an "Advanced Settings" toggle.
  - **Continuous Reconciliation**: The system continuously reconciles receipts against connected bank feeds or the internal OHC wallet without user intervention.
  - **Plain-Language Reporting**: Avoid accounting jargon. Use human-readable summaries generated by the LLM.

  ## Implementation Prompt
  To the Implementer:
  Design and implement the "Autonomous Invisible Bookkeeping & Tax Engine".
  - **Core Journey**: A user snaps a photo of a receipt. The system autonomously extracts the data, categorizes it for tax purposes, and updates the daily P&L.
  - **Requirements**: Provide a mobile-first UI for receipt capture. Ensure the AI Agent pipeline can process images and extract structured financial data. Implement a mechanism to simulate or perform automatic tax reservations from incoming revenue. The system must maintain strict multi-tenant data isolation.
