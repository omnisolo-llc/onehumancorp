# [RESEARCH] AI Agent Department Architecture

**Title**: AI Agent Department Architecture

**Problem Statement**:
Small business owners like Maya (baker) or Carlos (handyman) are overwhelmed by the sheer number of tasks required to run a business—from replying to customer DMs and processing orders, to creating marketing materials and tracking finances. They do not have the time, budget, or expertise to hire a full team (manager, marketer, salesperson, accountant). Existing platforms offer disconnected tools that still require the owner to do all the manual work. Business owners need a unified system where these tasks are handled invisibly by a "virtual team" that acts on their behalf, allowing them to focus on their actual craft.

**Research Report**:
- **Current Landscape**:
  - **Shopify**: Offers point solutions like Shopify Magic for text generation, but lacks autonomous, coordinated agents acting as a cohesive team. Requires heavy manual orchestration.
  - **Wix/Squarespace**: Provide basic AI website builders and some auto-responders, but no persistent "departments" that autonomously run marketing campaigns or proactively handle customer success.
  - **GoDaddy**: Focuses on simple templates and basic SEO tools; lacks true AI autonomy and cross-functional agent coordination.
- **Findings**:
  - Business owners understand traditional business roles (Manager, Accountant, Promoter) but are intimidated by terms like "AI Agents," "LLMs," or "RAG."
  - They want the ability to review AI actions before they are sent to customers (e.g., "draft-for-review") until they build trust, at which point they want "auto-execute."
  - Cross-department coordination is essential (e.g., when "The Manager" processes an order, "The Ambassador" should automatically send a personalized thank you note, and "The Accountant" should log the revenue).
- **References**:
  - *OHC Market Strategy*: Need for Hybrid RAG workflow for data privacy and scalable intelligence.

*Mobile UX Flow & UI Wireframes (375px first)*:
- **Home Dashboard**: A unified feed showing activity from all departments. E.g., a card saying "The Manager processed 3 orders today," and another saying "The Promoter drafted an Instagram post."
- **Department Drill-down**: Tapping a department (e.g., "The Promoter") opens a chat-like interface or activity log specific to that role. The user can see past actions, current drafts awaiting approval, and performance metrics.
- **Approval Queue**: A swipeable interface (Tinder-style or simple list) for pending AI actions. E.g., "The Ambassador drafted a reply to Maya's DM. Swipe right to send, swipe left to edit."
- **Settings/Trust Levels**: A simple toggle for each department: "Draft for Review" vs. "Auto-Execute."

*AI Agent Integration Points*:
- **Triggers**:
  - *On Event*: A new order comes in, triggering Operations.
  - *On Schedule*: Weekly financial summary generated by Finance every Friday at 5 PM.
  - *On Demand*: User asks The Promoter to "create a Mother's Day sale campaign."
- **Coordination**: Agents communicate via a shared event stream. When Operations updates an order status to "shipped," an event is emitted that Customer Success listens to, triggering a shipping confirmation message.
- **Memory/Context**: All agents read from and write to a shared "Swarm Memory" (the persistent context), ensuring that if Customer Success talks to a customer, Operations knows their order history, and Sales knows their previous inquiries.
- **Budgeting & Throttling**: Each tenant has an AI action budget based on their tier (e.g., Free tier = 100 actions/mo). Before an agent executes an action, it checks the budget. If the limit is reached, the action is paused and the user is prompted to upgrade.

*Key Design Decisions & Why*:
- **Role-Based Personas**: Using friendly names like "The Manager" and "The Promoter" bridges the gap for non-technical users, making the system approachable.
- **Configurable Autonomy (Draft vs. Auto)**: Builds user trust over time. Users can start with drafts and move to full autonomy once they are comfortable with the AI's output.
- **Shared Swarm Memory**: Crucial for a cohesive experience. Prevents different "departments" from contradicting each other or asking the user/customer for the same information twice.
- **Tier-Based Throttling at the Action Level**: Simplifies billing and ensures fair usage across the multi-tenant architecture without exposing underlying token complexities to the user.
