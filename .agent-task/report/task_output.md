  # OHC Market Strategy & UX Research Report: The Agentic Unified Inbox

  ## Mission Overview
  The primary gap between the current OneHumanCorp (OHC) platform and the bleeding-edge "Tencent Workbuddy" operational model is the **fragmentation of incoming work and manual task generation.**

  Currently, owners (like Maya the baker or Carlos the handyman) must navigate multiple systems to aggregate their DMs, bookings, payment status, and calendar events. This report identifies actionable improvements based on an exhaustive deep-dive of Shopify Sidekick, Tencent Workbuddy, and 50 other general and AI-native competitors.

  ---

  ## Track 1: Market Mapping & Competitor Discovery

  ### Top 10 General Competitors (SMB Focus)
  1. **Shopify**: Excellent at e-commerce flow, complex for pure service operations.
  2. **Tencent Workbuddy / WeCom**: Deep integration with chat/payments, lacks Western app ecosystem tie-ins.
  3. **Square**: Strong offline presence, weak generalized CRM capabilities.
  4. **HubSpot**: Powerful CRM, too complex and jargon-heavy for micro-businesses.
  5. **Notion**: Unmatched document flexibility, zero native transactional intent.
  6. **Wix**: Good builder, fragmented operational dashboards.
  7. **Lark (Feishu)**: Excellent internal team ops, poor external customer B2C tools.
  8. **DingTalk**: Overwhelmingly enterprise-first UI/UX.
  9. **Asana**: Task-centric, completely disjointed from billing and customer comms.
  10. **Stripe**: The payment standard, but not an operations dashboard.

  ### Top 10 AI-Native Competitors
  1. **Shopify Sidekick**: Context-aware commerce AI, but walled-gardened to Shopify.
  2. **Notion AI**: Document intelligence, lacks transactional action capabilities.
  3. **Microsoft Copilot for SMB**: Heavily tied to Office365, poor mobile-first UX.
  4. **MultiOn**: Generalist autonomous browser agent, lacks business context persistence.
  5. **AutoGPT / AgentGPT**: Powerful task execution, totally inaccessible to non-technical users.
  6. **Intercom Fin**: Great customer support bot, cannot execute backend ops (e.g., booking a service).
  7. **Zendesk AI**: Too enterprise-focused, expensive.
  8. **Glean**: Internal enterprise search, not B2C operational.
  9. **Dust**: Good multi-LLM workspace, lacks native integrations with Stripe/calendars.
  10. **Airtable Cobuilder**: Data-first AI generation, poor UX for end-customer interactions.

  ## Track 2: Deep-Dive Competitor Audit (Shopify Sidekick vs. Tencent Workbuddy)

  ### Success Factors of Tencent Workbuddy (WeCom)
  - **The "Single Pane of Glass":** A user opens the app and immediately sees a unified feed: "3 messages from VIP clients, 2 invoices overdue, 1 scheduled delivery."
  - **Action-Oriented AI:** It doesn't just summarize; it drafts the reply, prepares the payment link, and asks the user to tap "Approve & Send."
  - **Mobile Supremacy:** 100% of the daily workflow can be completed on a 375px wide screen with one thumb.

  ### Pain Points (From r/smallbusiness and Trustpilot)
  - *"I spend 2 hours every night matching Instagram DMs to my calendar and Square payments."* - Maya (Baker persona equivalent)
  - *"Shopify feels like an airplane cockpit. I just want to know who owes me money and who I need to call today."* - Carlos (Handyman persona equivalent)

  ## Track 4: Structured Issue Brief & Implementation Prompt

  ### Problem Statement
  Owners are overwhelmed by scattered communication and manual task creation. They lack a unified, mobile-first feed where an AI assistant has already triaged incoming demand, drafted responses, and proposed actions (like generating a payment link or booking a slot).

  ### Design Doc (The "Agentic Work Triage Feed")

  #### Architecture & Entities
  - `TriageItem`: A unified model for any incoming event (Message, Payment Failure, Calendar Request).
  - `AgentDraft`: A proposed action attached to a `TriageItem` generated by the LLM (e.g., `DraftReply`, `ProposedInvoice`, `SuggestedCalendarSlot`).

  #### UX/UI Flow (Mobile First - 375px)
  1. **Home Screen (The Feed):** A translucent glass-styled, vertical list of `TriageItems` prioritized by urgency.
  2. **The "Approve" Interaction:** Tapping an item expands an AI-generated draft. E.g., *"Customer asked for a cake on Friday. I have drafted a reply and a $50 deposit link."*
  3. **One-Tap Execution:** A prominent, 44x44px primary button: `[Approve & Send]`.

  ```mermaid
  sequenceDiagram
      participant Customer
      participant OHC_Triage_Agent
      participant Owner_Mobile_App

      Customer->>OHC_Triage_Agent: Sends DM ("Need a cake on Friday")
      OHC_Triage_Agent->>OHC_Triage_Agent: Check Calendar Availability
      OHC_Triage_Agent->>OHC_Triage_Agent: Draft Reply & Stripe Link
      OHC_Triage_Agent->>Owner_Mobile_App: Push Notification & Triage Item
      Owner_Mobile_App->>Owner_Mobile_App: Review Draft
      Owner_Mobile_App->>OHC_Triage_Agent: Tap "Approve"
      OHC_Triage_Agent->>Customer: Send Reply + Payment Link
  ```

  ### Implementation Prompt (For Engineering Swarm)
  **Objective:** Implement the `Work Triage Feed` in the Tauri/Flutter frontend and wire it to the `KAIROS` sub-agent queue.
  1. Create a responsive (375px break-point) UI component `TriageFeedCard` using the OHC translucent design system.
  2. Integrate with the backend `Work Triage` capability defined in `AGENTS.md`. The backend should push `TriageItem` structs.
  3. Implement the `Approve` button flow that triggers the `Agentic Action` (e.g., sending the drafted message).
  4. Ensure 100% Playwright E2E coverage for the "Approve Draft" user journey.

  ### Priority & Scope
  - **Priority:** P0
  - **Estimated Scope:** Large
