  # Predictive Cashflow & Autonomous Capital Engine Research Report

  ## Findings & Proposed Next Steps
  Small business owners face frequent "cashflow crunches" due to delayed payments and upfront inventory costs. Existing market solutions (like Shopify Capital or Square Loans) require manual application or navigating complex dashboards, lacking integration with future business events (e.g. upcoming bookings or inventory depletion).

  This architectural deep-dive proposes a "Predictive Cashflow Oracle" that leverages the existing OHC Event Mesh, analyzing unified booking calendars, universal inventory, and payment ledgers. It predicts capital shortfalls and proactively offers a "1-tap micro-advance" on the user's mobile lock screen, automatically recovering the advance through a revenue split on future transactions.
