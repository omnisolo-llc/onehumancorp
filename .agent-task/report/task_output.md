issue_title: "[architecture] Invisible Omnichannel Customer Identity Graph"
issue_description: |
  **Research Report & Proposed Architecture:**
  We researched the gap in current small business platforms regarding unified customer identity across disparate channels (Instagram, WhatsApp, Tap-to-Pay, Web).

  Unlike Shopify or Wix which silo these interactions or require complex third-party tools, OneHumanCorp will introduce an *Invisible Omnichannel Customer Identity Graph*. This system will automatically stitch together customer aliases and interactions securely using SPIFFE/SPIRE for identity validation.

  **Next Steps:**
  The engineering swarm needs to implement the background identity resolution service and the mobile-first (375px) translucent glass UI to display the unified timeline of customer touchpoints. Detailed design and instructions are documented in `docs/research/[architecture]_invisible_omnichannel_customer_identity_graph.md`.
