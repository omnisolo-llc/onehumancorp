  # Research Report: Multi-Tenant Universal Agent Feed and AI Decision Engine

  ## Problem Statement
  Business owners like Maya (baker), Carlos (handyman), Priya (boutique owner), Leo (music tutor), and Fatima (food cart) currently face a fragmented workflow. They receive inquiries via Instagram DMs, website forms, SMS, and emails. Existing platforms treat these as separate silos (e.g., a "messages" app, an "orders" app) requiring the owner to manually route tasks. They have to read a message, switch to the calendar to check availability, switch to inventory to check stock, and then draft a reply. They need a centralized "Agent Feed" that acts as a single pane of glass, where AI agents proactively triage demand, consult business context, and propose actions.

  ## Research Report
  - **Market Context**: Traditional platforms (Shopify Inbox, Wix Inbox) aggregate messages but lack proactive AI decision-making; they are merely reactive chat interfaces. Tencent Workbuddy and DingTalk excel at centralizing team operations but are too complex for micro-SMEs.
  - **Competitive Gaps**:
    - *Shopify*: Relies on third-party apps for deep AI integration, creating a disjointed UX and additional subscription costs.
    - *HubSpot*: Powerful CRM but overkill and too expensive for a food cart or solo baker.
    - *Notion AI*: Great for knowledge management but not wired for live transactional operations (payments, bookings).
  - **The OHC Opportunity**: By integrating a multi-tenant Agent Feed deeply tied to the business ledger, inventory, and calendar, OHC can shift the owner from "doing the work" to "approving the work". This creates a truly autonomous assistant that respects the owner's ultimate authority.

  ## Design Doc
  ### Data Model (PostgreSQL)
  - `FeedEvent`: A unified event stream (message received, order placed, booking requested) with `tenant_id` for row-level security.
  - `AgentActionDraft`: A proposed action generated by an AI agent (e.g., a drafted reply, a quote to approve) linked to a `FeedEvent`.
  - `ActionApproval`: Tracks the owner's decision (approved, edited, rejected) for audit and RLHF (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback) loop.

  ### Architecture Diagram
  ```mermaid
  graph TD
      A[Webhooks / APIs] --> B[Event Bus]
      B --> C[Work Triage Agent]
      C --> D[Specialized Agents]
      D --> E[Redis/PG Context]
      E --> F[Draft Generation]
      F --> G[Flutter Mobile UI]
  ```

  ### AI Agent Integration
  - **Work Triage Agent**: Subscribes to the event bus, normalizes incoming data from disparate sources (Instagram, SMS, Web), and routes to specialized agents.
  - **Specialized Agents (Customer, Operations, Sales)**: Query business context (Redis/PostgreSQL) and generate `AgentActionDraft` records.
  - **Context Memory**: Each tenant has a dedicated memory partition (vector store) containing policies, past interactions, and preferences.

  ### Mobile UX Flow (375px)
  1. **The Feed (Home)**: A single scrolling list of prioritized cards. High-priority items (e.g., "Urgent: 3 Cake Inquiries") are pinned at the top.
  2. **Action Card**: Each card contains context (e.g., the customer's DM) and a large, touch-friendly proposed action (e.g., a drafted reply with a payment link).
  3. **Interaction**: The owner can tap "Approve & Send" (one tap), "Edit" (opens native keyboard), or "Dismiss" (swipe gesture).
