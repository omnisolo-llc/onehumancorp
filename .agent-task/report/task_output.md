# Scout: Tool Integration Research Report

## Social Media Integration: ManyChat
- **Problem:** Business owners are overwhelmed by DMs across Instagram, WhatsApp, and Facebook. They miss messages and lose sales.
- **Appearance:** A unified inbox tab in the OHC dashboard where all social messages appear seamlessly, looking like a simple chat app.
- **Advantages/Risks:** Fast API, great WhatsApp support, visually intuitive. / Meta API changes can break integration.
- **Pricing:** $15/month Pro plan.
- **Cloud/Standalone:** Cloud and Standalone supported.

## Calendar & Scheduling: Calendly
- **Problem:** Back-and-forth emails to find a meeting time waste hours each week. Double-bookings cause customer frustration.
- **Appearance:** A 'Booking' page linked directly from the OHC profile, with a simple date/time picker that syncs silently with their personal calendar.
- **Advantages/Risks:** Industry standard, robust timezone handling, minimal setup. / Users might have complex existing calendar setups.
- **Pricing:** $12/month Professional tier.
- **Cloud/Standalone:** Cloud and Standalone supported.

## Email Marketing: Mailchimp
- **Problem:** Small businesses struggle to re-engage past customers and announce new promotions without a structured email system.
- **Appearance:** A 'Campaigns' tab where business owners can select a pre-made template, select a customer segment, and click 'Send'.
- **Advantages/Risks:** Massive template library, great deliverability rates. / Pricing scales steeply as subscriber count grows.
- **Pricing:** Free up to 500 contacts, then $13/month.
- **Cloud/Standalone:** Cloud and Standalone supported.

## Payment Processing: Mercado Pago
- **Problem:** Stripe isn't available or preferred in LATAM. Businesses need a local, trusted payment provider with fast settlement.
- **Appearance:** A simple toggle in the 'Payments' setting to 'Enable Mercado Pago'. Checkout flows automatically show local payment options (e.g., PIX, Boleto).
- **Advantages/Risks:** Huge market share in LATAM, supports local payment methods. / Regional only, requires separate integration from global providers.
- **Pricing:** Percentage per transaction varies by country (e.g., ~4%).
- **Cloud/Standalone:** Cloud and Standalone supported.

## Shipping & Logistics: Shippo
- **Problem:** Calculating shipping rates manually and buying labels at the post office is incredibly slow and prone to errors.
- **Appearance:** A 'Fulfill Order' button that instantly shows the best shipping rate, prints a label, and emails the tracking number to the customer.
- **Advantages/Risks:** Multi-carrier support from a single API, discounted rates. / Label generation errors require clear user feedback.
- **Pricing:** $10/month or pay-as-you-go ($0.05/label).
- **Cloud/Standalone:** Cloud and Standalone supported.

## SMS & Notifications: Twilio
- **Problem:** Customers often miss email notifications, leading to no-shows or delayed order pickups. SMS is more immediate.
- **Appearance:** Automated text messages sent directly to customers for order updates, appearing seamless without the business owner needing to write them manually.
- **Advantages/Risks:** Global reach, highly reliable delivery, programmable routing. / Compliance rules (10DLC) can be complex for small business owners to set up.
- **Pricing:** ~$0.0079 per message.
- **Cloud/Standalone:** Cloud and Standalone supported.

## Video Conferencing: Zoom
- **Problem:** Manually creating Zoom links and sending them to clients for every online consultation is tedious and unprofessional.
- **Appearance:** When an online meeting is booked, a Zoom link is automatically generated and added to the calendar invite and confirmation email.
- **Advantages/Risks:** Universally recognized, highly reliable video quality. / Requires users to authenticate their Zoom accounts via OAuth.
- **Pricing:** $15.99/month Pro plan.
- **Cloud/Standalone:** Cloud and Standalone supported.
