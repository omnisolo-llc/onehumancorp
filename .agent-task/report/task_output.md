  # Research Report
  Our research into small business workflows revealed a critical gap in OHC's current capabilities: the lack of an integrated, instant micro-lending solution. Businesses run into cash flow issues, and traditional capital takes too long to approve.

  # Findings
  Competitors like Shopify Capital and Square Loans rely on generic thresholds. OHC has an opportunity to leverage AI agents (the Financial Advisor) and real-time ledger velocity to offer proactive, contextual micro-loans to cover imminent business expenses (like inventory for an upcoming booking).

  # Proposed Next Steps
  We have drafted a full architectural design document outlining a conversational UI flow (1-tap acceptance), data models (`CAPITAL_OFFER`, `CAPITAL_ADVANCE`, `REPAYMENT_SCHEDULE`), and an invisible ledger split mechanism for automatic repayment routing. This design should be implemented to unlock immediate capital for users.
