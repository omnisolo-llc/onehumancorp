issue_title: Implement Omnichannel AI Inbox & Proactive Customer Assistance
issue_description: "# OHC Market Research & Issue Brief: Omnichannel AI Inbox & Proactive Customer Assistance\n\n## Problem Statement\nSmall business owners, like Maya (Baker) and Carlos (Handyman), manage customer inquiries across fragmented channels (Instagram DMs, WhatsApp, Email, Web Widget). They struggle with manually triaging messages, remembering customer context, scheduling, and quoting. The absence of an integrated, mobile-first AI assistant results in missed leads, delayed responses, and lost revenue. Existing solutions (like Shopify Inbox or HubSpot) are too complex for solopreneurs, while basic tools (like WhatsApp Business) lack unified context and agentic capabilities.\n\n## Research Report\n\n### 1. Market Mapping & Competitor Discovery\n\n**Chatwoot Source Code Audit & Feature Benchmarking**\n- **Architecture**: Rails monolith with Vue/Nuxt frontend, Redis for Sidekiq background jobs, ActionCable for WebSockets. \n- **Core Features**: \n  - Shared Omnichannel Inbox (Email, WhatsApp, FB Messenger, IG, Web Widget, API, SMS).\n  - Agent routing & Assignment.\n  - Custom Attributes & Contact Management.\n  - Macros, Canned Responses, Automation Rules.\n  - SLA Policies, CSAT Surveys.\n- **Takeaway for OHC**: Chatwoot relies heavily on traditional webhooks and manual agent actions. OHC's Rust implementation should modernize this by integrating an AI triage layer directly into the event stream, automatically appending context, drafting responses, and triggering downstream tasks (e.g., quoting, booking) without requiring a human routing step.\n\n**Top 10 General Competitors**\n1. **Tencent Workbuddy**: High integration, complex setup. Strong in enterprise, weak in solopreneur agility.\n2. **WeCom (Tencent)**: Deep WeChat integration, pervasive in Asia, highly operational.\n3. **DingTalk (Alibaba)**: Extremely broad (HR, scheduling, approval flows), can feel oppressive and admin-heavy.\n4. **Feishu/Lark (ByteDance)**: Excellent document collaboration, less focused on physical service operations.\n5. **Shopify Inbox**: Great for e-commerce, terrible for service businesses.\n6. **Square Team & Customer**: Strong POS integration, fragmented messaging.\n7. **HubSpot**: Powerful CRM, too complex and expensive for micro-businesses.\n8. **Notion**: Excellent knowledge management, poor realtime omnichannel messaging.\n9. **Microsoft Copilot**: Generalized AI, lacks out-of-the-box vertical workflows for small biz.\n10. **Wix Inbox**: Good basic unification, lacks proactive agentic scheduling.\n\n**Top 10 AI-Native Competitors**\n1. **Sidekick (Shopify)**: Specialized for store owners, deep e-com context.\n2. **Harvey AI**: Legal focus, highly accurate text synthesis.\n3. **Fin (Intercom)**: Great customer facing bot, high cost.\n4. **Kustomer AI**: Support-oriented, strong NLP routing.\n5. **Glean**: Internal knowledge search, not customer facing.\n6. **Sierra**: Conversational AI for brands, less for solopreneurs.\n7. **PolyAI**: Voice-focused AI for restaurants/services.\n8. **Bland AI**: Phone calling AI, complex setup.\n9. **DevRev**: Developer focus, connects support to product.\n10. **Lindy.ai**: AI employee builder, highly customizable, slightly technical.\n\n### 2. Deep-Dive Competitor Audit: Intercom (with Fin AI) vs WeCom\n\n*Focusing on Intercom given its push into AI-first support and Inbox UI.*\n\n**Capabilities**: \n- Omnichannel inbox with unified customer timeline.\n- \"Fin\" AI answers questions using help center docs.\n- Proactive messages and product tours.\n\n**Success Factors**:\n- Time-to-value for end customers is fast if help docs exist.\n- Excellent UI/UX on desktop.\n\n**User Sentiment Audit (Intercom/Shopify Inbox/HubSpot)**:\n- *Positive*: \"I love having all chats in one place.\"\n- *Negative (Reddit r/smallbusiness)*: \"Intercom is way too expensive for a 2-person team ($74+/mo plus AI fees).\"\n- *Negative (Trustpilot)*: \"The mobile app is clunky. I can't easily send a payment link from my phone while chatting.\"\n- *Negative (App Store)*: \"Shopify Inbox doesn't let me manage my in-person service bookings.\"\n\n### 3. OHC Gap & Pain Point Identification\n\n**OHC Feature Audit vs Competitors**\n- **Current OHC**: Lacks a native, highly responsive, AI-first omnichannel inbox.\n- **The Gap**: We need an inbox that doesn't just display messages, but *acts* on them. When Carlos gets a WhatsApp message asking for a quote, the AI should immediately draft the quote based on historical pricing and propose it to Carlos for approval.\n- **Pain Point**: Solopreneurs are paralyzed by context-switching between chat, calendar, and quoting tools on a 375px screen.\n\n### 4. Deeper Focused Research & Agentic Solutions\n\n**Agentic Solution Design**:\n- **Work Triage AI**: Intercepts inbound webhook events (IG, WA, Email).\n- **Context Injection**: Queries OHC Knowledge & Documents (past bookings, pricing lists).\n- **Action Proposal**: Generates a rich UI card within the chat feed (e.g., `[Approve Quote for $150]`, `[Suggest Tuesday at 2 PM]`).\n- **Owner Flow**: Owner receives a push notification, opens the OHC app (375px optimized), sees the drafted response + action card, and taps \"Approve & Send\".\n\n### 5. Visual Evidence & Charts\n\n```mermaid\ngraph TD\n    A[Inbound DM/Email] --> B(OHC Rust Event Stream)\n    B --> C{Work Triage Agent}\n    C -->|Classify & Enrich| D[Database/Redis Lock]\n    D --> E[Generate Draft & Action Card]\n    E --> F[Owner Push Notification]\n    F --> G[Owner Approves (1 Tap)]\n    G --> H[Dispatch Reply + Update State]\n```\n\n**Feature Gap Heatmap**\n```mermaid\npie title \"Small Biz Needs Addressed by Incumbents\"\n    \"Omnichannel Chat\" : 40\n    \"AI Triage & Context\" : 10\n    \"Integrated Billing/Scheduling\" : 20\n    \"Mobile-First UX\" : 30\n```\n\n### 6. Design Doc\n\n**High-Level Architecture**:\n- **Ingestion**: Native Rust microservice (`ohc-ingest`) handling high-throughput webhooks (Stripe, Twilio, Meta Graph API).\n- **Processing**: PostgreSQL `SKIP LOCKED` job queue.\n- **Agent Integration**: `ohc-triage-worker` runs the Gemini Pro structured prompt, yielding JSON with `{draft_reply, action_type, action_payload}`.\n- **Frontend**: Flutter app using OHC Premium Tokens. The Inbox view is a unified list sorted by AI-determined urgency, not just chronological.\n\n**Mobile UX Flow (375px)**:\n1. **Home Screen**: \"2 Urgent Leads\" displayed at top.\n2. **Tap Lead**: Opens chat timeline. Bottom sheet peeks with \"AI Suggested Action: Send $50 Deposit Link\".\n3. **Action Tap**: Translucent glass modal confirms amount. Tap \"Send\".\n4. **Resolution**: Chat shows inline payment request block.\n\n### 7. Implementation Prompt\n\n**User-Facing Outcome**:\nThe owner opens the OHC app and sees all customer messages from Instagram, Email, and WhatsApp in one unified feed. Every message from a potential lead already has a context-aware drafted reply and a suggested next step (e.g., send quote, book meeting) waiting for 1-tap approval.\n\n**Critical User Journey (CUJ)**:\n1. System receives a mock Instagram DM via API.\n2. AI Triage job processes the DM, identifies it as a pricing request, and drafts a reply.\n3. Owner navigates to the Inbox on mobile UI.\n4. Owner sees the draft and the \"Create Quote\" action chip.\n5. Owner taps \"Approve & Send\".\n6. System dispatches the reply and transitions the conversation state.\n\n**Acceptance Criteria**:\n- Rust ingestion endpoint responds in < 50ms.\n- PostgreSQL job queue successfully routes the message to the AI worker.\n- Flutter UI displays the chat timeline with the AI draft and action chip without horizontal scrolling on 375px.\n- Playwright E2E test validates the full flow from incoming webhook to UI approval and outbound message dispatch.\n- Zero mock data in the UI; all state flows from the database.\n\n**Priority**: P0\n**Estimated Scope**: Large\n\n### 8.
