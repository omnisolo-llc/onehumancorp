  # Research Report: Autonomous Revenue-Based Capital Engine

  ## Findings
  Small business owners face severe cash flow bottlenecks for micro-loans ($500 - $5,000) that traditional banks cannot serve. Existing platforms (Shopify Capital, Stripe Capital) offer revenue-based financing but disconnected from operational triggers.

  ## Next Steps
  Implement an Autonomous Revenue-Based Capital Engine that:
  1. Monitors the `TENANT_LEDGER` to calculate dynamic, pre-approved capital limits.
  2. Correlates with `BOOKING_EVENT`s to proactively offer capital exactly when needed.
  3. Uses a 1-tap approval mobile UI (375px first) without financial jargon.
  4. Recovers funds invisibly via a flat fee and a percentage split of future daily sales.

  Please refer to `docs/research/[architecture]_autonomous_revenue_based_capital_engine.md` for the full design doc.
