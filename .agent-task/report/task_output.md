  # Research Report & Proposed Next Steps

  ## Gap Discovered
  Through research into leading small business platforms (Shopify, Square, Stripe), we identified a critical feature missing in OHC: the ability for SMB owners (like Maya the baker or Fatima the food cart owner) to instantly access revenue-based capital directly through the platform. Currently, businesses face cash-flow crunches when buying new equipment and struggle with traditional banking loans.

  ## Proposed Solution
  We proposed the **Autonomous Revenue-Based Capital Engine**. This system will leverage the existing OHC Universal Ledger and autonomous Finance AI to:
  1. Pre-approve businesses based on real-time ledger revenue and bookings.
  2. Offer 1-tap capital advances directly into their OHC Treasury Wallet.
  3. Invisibly auto-repay the advance as a fixed percentage of daily sales.
