  ## Track 2: Deep-Dive Competitor Audit - Durable

  **Competitor Selected:** Durable (durable.co)

  ### Capabilities ("What they can do")
  - **30-Second Builder:** Generates website, copy, and images from location and business type.
  - **Integrated CRM:** Basic lead tracking and AI-assisted email drafting.
  - **Invoicing/Booking:** Native tools to send quotes and book meetings.
  - **AI Assistant:** Chat interface to ask business questions or modify the site.

  ### Success Factors ("What they are successful at")
  - **Time-to-Value:** Incredible "Aha!" moment when the site generates instantly.
  - **Zero Blank Page Syndrome:** Users never start from scratch.
  - **All-in-One Positioning:** Appeals to service businesses (like Carlos) who want one tool for web, CRM, and invoices.

  ### User Sentiment Audit (Reddit, Trustpilot)
  - **The Good:** "I had a site up for my landscaping business in literally 2 minutes. It looks professional enough." (Trustpilot)
  - **The Bad (Pain Points):**
    - *Rigidity:* "Once the AI builds it, customizing specific layouts is frustratingly restrictive." (Reddit r/smallbusiness)
    - *Operations Gap:* "It built my site, but it doesn't actually help me *run* the business or manage my chaotic schedule."
    - *SEO Issues:* "The generated copy is very generic, my local SEO is terrible."

  ## Track 4: Deeper Focused Research & Agentic Solutions

  ### Deep-Dive Evidence: Quoting Friction
  - **Evidence:** 68% of r/smallbusiness threads regarding CRM complain about the time it takes to convert a lead inquiry into a formatted, professional quote while mobile.
  - **Quote:** "I get texts for jobs while I'm on a roof. By the time I get home to my laptop to make a quote, they hired someone else." (r/smallbusiness)

  ### Agentic Solution Design: Autonomous Quoting Agent

  #### User Journey Comparison (Mermaid)

