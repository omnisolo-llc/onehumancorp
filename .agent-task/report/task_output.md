issue_title: "Implement AI-Driven Autonomous Subscription Replenishment Engine"
issue_description: |
  ## Problem Statement
  Small business owners and operators (like Priya the Boutique Operator or Maya the Home Baker) struggle with customer retention and recurring revenue predictability. Currently, if a customer buys a consumable product (e.g., coffee beans, skincare, custom monthly cake boxes), the burden is entirely on the owner to remember to follow up or on the customer to manually re-order.
  Traditional platforms (Shopify, Wix) require expensive third-party apps (like ReCharge) which are highly complex to configure, involving rigid billing schedules, complex discount logic, and confusing customer portals.
  The OHC owner needs an *invisible* AI agent that autonomously tracks customer purchase history, predicts when a customer is likely running low on a consumable item, and proactively drafts a personalized, 1-click replenishment offer (via SMS, WhatsApp, or email) that the owner simply approves.

  ## Research Report
  - **Market Context**: Subscription commerce is a major growth area for SMBs. The "Subscribe & Save" model guarantees revenue but is technically prohibitive for micro-SMEs to set up.
  - **Competitor Analysis**:
    - **Shopify**: Requires apps like ReCharge or Skio. High monthly fees ($99+/mo) + transaction fees. Setup is notoriously complex, requiring managing separate customer portals and rigid cron-job billing cycles.
    - **Wix / GoDaddy**: Offer basic recurring payments, but no predictive or AI-driven follow-ups.
  - **The OHC Opportunity**: We can leapfrog the market by shifting from "rigid cron-based subscriptions" to "AI-predicted conversational replenishment." The AI Sales Agent observes a purchase (e.g., a 30-day supply of vitamins), waits 25 days, and drafts a message: "Hi [Customer], you might be running low on your daily vitamins! Want me to send another batch for 10% off? Just reply YES." The owner approves this draft in a 375px mobile feed.

  ## Design Doc
  ### Architecture
  1.  **Replenishment Prediction Pipeline (Background Worker)**:
      - A daily PostgreSQL cron job (or AI Job Queue `SKIP LOCKED` worker) scans recent `Orders`.
      - Identifies items tagged as `consumable` with an `estimated_duration_days`.
      - Identifies customers approaching the replenishment window.
  2.  **AI Department Orchestration (Sales Agent)**:
      - Triggered by the pipeline.
      - Uses the `SalesAgent` (or a specialized `RetentionAgent`) to draft a personalized, conversational message based on the customer's previous order history and tone preferences.
  3.  **Owner Triage Feed (Frontend - Flutter/Tauri)**:
      - The drafted message appears in the owner's "Today's Priorities" feed.
      - Mobile-first (375px) card: Shows the customer, previous item, drafted message, and a large "Approve & Send" button.
  4.  **1-Click Payment Intent (Stripe)**:
      - Upon customer reply ("YES"), the agent generates a Stripe Payment Link with the exact cart and discount applied, sending it directly in the chat.

  ### Mobile UX Flow (375px)
  1.  Owner opens OHC app. The top card in the feed reads: "3 customers are running low on Coffee Beans."
  2.  Owner taps the card to expand.
  3.  Card displays a summary: "Agent drafted 3 personalized SMS reminders with a 10% loyalty discount."
  4.  Owner can swipe to preview the drafts or tap a single large, primary action button: "Approve All".

  ### AI Agent Integration Points
  - The feature extends the existing Orchestration framework.
  - The `OperationsAgent` or `SalesAgent` must gain a new tool/capability to query `Order` history and generate `ReplenishmentDrafts`.
  - Handoff from AI draft -> Human Approval -> AI execution (sending the message).
