  ## Title
  Autonomous AI Dispute & Chargeback Defense Engine

  ## Problem Statement
  Small business owners like Maya (who takes deposit-based custom orders) and Carlos (who performs physical services) face devastating setbacks when hit by fraudulent chargebacks or payment disputes. Fighting a dispute typically requires manually logging into a payment gateway, assembling proof of service, screenshots of communications, and uploading PDFs before a rigid deadline. A single lost chargeback can cost a business owner their weekly profit, and the technical anxiety of navigating Stripe/PayPal dispute dashboards leads many to just accept the loss. They need an automated, invisible defense system that compiles evidence from their unified OHC ecosystem and fights the dispute on their behalf.

  ## Research Report
  - **Competitive Benchmark:** Shopify offers some chargeback protection (Shopify Protect) but only for physical goods shipped via specific carriers. Wix and Squarespace simply pass the dispute to the merchant via Stripe, offering no integrated defense.
  - **Market Gap:** Current platforms lack context. They do not connect the conversational history (DMs, emails), the signed contracts/deposits, and the fulfillment/booking logs into a cohesive evidence packet automatically.
  - **Pain Point:** "I lost $300 because a customer claimed they never received the custom cake, but I have their Instagram DM saying 'It looks beautiful, thanks!' I didn't know how to submit the DM screenshot to the payment processor in time."
  - **Opportunity:** Since OHC owns the Omnichannel Inbox, the Booking Calendar, and the Payment Ledger, an AI agent can instantly pull all relevant context (messages, booking completion, photo uploads, contract signatures) to construct and submit a compelling dispute response document the moment a chargeback is initiated.

  ## Design Doc

  ### High-Level Architecture
  ```mermaid
  graph TD
      Webhook[Payment Gateway Webhook: Dispute Created] --> EventBus[OHC Event Bus]
      EventBus --> Trigger[Finance Agent Department Trigger]

      subgraph OHC Memory Layer
          Trigger --> Inbox[Omnichannel Inbox History]
          Trigger --> Contracts[Signed Contracts & Invoices]
          Trigger --> Fulfillment[Booking Logs & Fulfillment Data]
      end

      Inbox --> Evidence[Evidence Compiler Agent]
      Contracts --> Evidence
      Fulfillment --> Evidence

      Evidence -->|Generates Formal Response| Draft[Dispute Response Document]
      Draft --> MobileUI[Mobile Approval Push Notification]
      MobileUI -->|User Taps 'Submit Defense'| Gateway[Payment Gateway API: Submit Evidence]
  ```

  ### Mobile UX Flow (375px First)
  1. **Push Notification:** "Urgent: $150 Chargeback initiated by John Doe. We prepared a defense."
  2. **Alert Screen:** A clean card showing the disputed amount, the reason code (e.g., "Product Not Received"), and a countdown timer for the response deadline.
  3. **Evidence Review (The "Grandmother Test"):** A scrollable summary generated by AI: "We found John's text confirming receipt and the signed deposit agreement. Tap to preview the evidence packet." No JSON, no API keys, just human-readable proof.
  4. **One-Tap Action:** A large primary button: "Submit AI Defense".
  5. **Confirmation State:** "Defense submitted successfully. We will notify you when the bank decides."

  ### AI Agent Integration Points
  - **The Finance & Legal Agent:** A specialized sub-agent triggered by chargeback webhooks. It acts as a paralegal, querying the Memory Layer for interactions linked to the specific Customer ID and Order ID.
  - **The Operations Agent:** Synthesizes the timeline of events (booking created -> deposit paid -> service completed -> feedback received) into a chronological narrative for the bank.

  ### Key Design Decisions
  - **Zero-Config Integration:** The user does not need to connect their inbox to their payment gateway; OHC's unified architecture provides this inherently.
  - **Human-in-the-Loop:** While the evidence compilation is autonomous, the final submission requires a 1-tap approval from the business owner to ensure they are aware of the financial dispute.
  - **macOS-style Translucent UI:** The alert cards will use premium glassmorphism to make a stressful financial event feel controlled, professional, and manageable.

  ## Implementation Prompt
