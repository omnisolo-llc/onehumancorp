  # Title: Autonomous Dynamic Pricing and Yield Engine

  ## Problem Statement
  Small business owners like Leo (music tutor) and Priya (boutique owner) leave money on the table because they lack the time and data to manually adjust pricing based on demand, seasonality, or customer booking density. Traditional platforms like Shopify or Acuity require manual intervention or complex third-party tools to implement basic yield management. They need an invisible AI capability that automatically optimizes pricing to maximize revenue without requiring them to become data analysts or revenue managers.

  ## Research Report
  - **Market Gap:** While airlines and hotels have sophisticated yield management (e.g., dynamic pricing based on availability), small businesses lack access to these tools. Existing SMB platforms only support static pricing.
  - **Competitor Landscape:**
      - *Shopify:* Requires paid apps (e.g., "Dynamic Pricing") which are often complex and require manual rule creation.
      - *Wix/Squarespace:* No native dynamic pricing features.
      - *Acuity/Calendly:* Booking slots have fixed prices; no ability to charge more for peak times automatically.
  - **OHC AI Advantage:** OHC's Financial and Advisory AI agents can monitor real-time signals (booking velocity, inventory levels, competitor pricing, seasonality) and autonomously adjust prices within pre-approved boundaries set by the business owner.

  ## Design Doc
  ### Architecture Diagram
  ```mermaid
  graph TD
      A[Market/Internal Signals: Booking Velocity, Inventory, Time] --> B[Business Advisory Agent]
      B -->|Analyzes Trends & Predicts Demand| C[Finance & Payments Agent]
      C -->|Calculates Optimal Price| D[Dynamic Pricing Engine]
      D -->|Updates Pricing Tier/Slot| E[Global Inventory/Ledger]
      E --> F[Storefront/Booking UI]
      B -.->|Notifies Owner of Adjustments| G[Plain-Language Weekly Report]
  ```

  ### Mobile UX Flow (375px First)
  1. **Opt-in/Configuration:** In the "Finance" settings, a simple toggle: "Enable Smart Pricing".
  2. **Boundary Setting:** Two simple sliders/inputs: "Minimum Price" and "Maximum Price" (e.g., Leo sets his lesson price to normally $50, with a min of $40 for slow weeks and a max of $70 for high demand).
  3. **Display:** When a customer views Leo's calendar, peak times (e.g., Saturday morning) might show as $60, while off-peak (e.g., Tuesday afternoon) show as $45.
  4. **Reporting:** The Business Advisory Agent sends a weekly SMS: "Smart Pricing earned you an extra $120 this week by charging slightly more for your most popular Saturday slots."

  ### Key Design Decisions
  - **Opt-in & Boundaries:** To maintain trust, dynamic pricing must be strictly opt-in and bound by min/max limits set by the owner.
  - **Invisible Operation:** The actual yield calculation (e.g., analyzing remaining capacity vs. time to event) happens entirely in the background via the AI departments.
  - **Transparent Reporting:** The owner must clearly see the value generated by the AI in their weekly business brief.

  ## Implementation Prompt
  Implement the "Autonomous Dynamic Pricing and Yield Engine". The system should allow business owners to enable "Smart Pricing" on specific products or services, setting strict minimum and maximum price boundaries. The Finance & Advisory AI agents should then autonomously adjust the public-facing price based on demand signals (e.g., remaining inventory, booking velocity, or time of day) without further user intervention. Ensure the UI for setting these boundaries is radically simple and mobile-first, and integrate the resulting revenue lift into the weekly plain-language business reporting flow. Focus on the end-to-end user journey and automated adjustment logic.
