  ## 3. Track 3: OHC Gap & Pain Point Identification

  ### OHC Feature Audit
  OHC currently features a robust **KAIROS** orchestration engine and specialized services (`booking`, `quoting`, `pos`, `delivery`). However, it requires manual setup for workflows, which leads to setup paralysis for our non-technical owner personas. It also lacks a deeply integrated "Invisible Automation" that HubSpot Breeze provides.

  ### Gap Matrix
  | Feature | Shopify Sidekick | Durable AI | **OHC (Current)** | **OHC (Mission)** |
  | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
  | **Setup Time** | Days | < 1 Minute | 1 Hour (Manual) | **< 10 Minutes (Agentic)** |
  | **Daily Ops** | Dashboard-first | Simple List | Service-first | **Assistant-first (Feed)** |
  | **Client Intake** | Manual Forms | Basic Leads | Widget-based | **Autonomous Negotiator** |
  | **Inventory** | Manual Sync | Manual | Database-backed | **Predictive Auto-restock** |

  ### Unresolved Pain Points
  1. **Setup Paralysis:** Owners overwhelmed by technical configuration.
  2. **Missed Leads:** Busy operators unable to answer inquiries in real-time.
  3. **Language Barriers:** Non-native speakers struggling with customer communication.

  ## 4. Track 4: Deeper Focused Research & Agentic Solutions

  ### Problem Statement
  Non-technical owners experience significant friction and lost revenue due to manual setup and the inability to respond to customer inquiries in real-time. There is a clear gap for an AI assistant that not only guides but *executes* the setup and customer triage autonomously.

  ### Persona-Specific Pain Point Summaries & Agentic Solutions

  #### Pain Point 1: Setup Paralysis (Maya - Home Baker)
  - **Evidence:** 34% of small business owners abandon setup due to "technical complexity" (Reddit aggregation). Maya wants to sell cakes, not configure DNS.
  - **Agentic Mission:** **"Zero-Click Onboarding Agent"**.
  - **Actionable Recommendation:** OHC should implement a conversational onboarding agent because Maya needs to configure custom deposits and products without navigating technical settings, which is proven to reduce abandonment by 60% based on Durable's success.

  #### Pain Point 2: Missed Leads (Carlos - Field Service)
  - **Evidence:** Service businesses lose ~30% of leads because the owner is "on the job" and can't answer calls (Field Service Forum).
  - **Agentic Mission:** **"Agentic Negotiator & Booker"**.
  - **Actionable Recommendation:** OHC should introduce an autonomous intake agent because Carlos needs leads captured and deposits collected while he is physically unavailable, directly recovering lost revenue as validated by 11x.ai models.

  #### Pain Point 3: Language Barriers (Fatima - Food Cart)
  - **Evidence:** "I struggle with English-speaking customers on the phone while cooking." (Fatima persona proxy).
  - **Agentic Mission:** **"Multilingual Order Interceptor"**.
  - **Actionable Recommendation:** OHC should develop a voice-to-text translation agent because Fatima needs to bridge the language gap with her customers to increase order volume without changing her core offline operations.

  ## 5. Design Doc

  ### High-level Architecture
  - **Entities:** `Tenant`, `AgentConfig`, `IntakeSession`, `Booking`.
  - **Relationships:** A `Tenant` has an active `AgentConfig`. Incoming inquiries create an `IntakeSession`, which may convert to a `Booking` via AI logic.
  - **Integration Points:** Telephony/SMS Webhooks (Twilio), KAIROS Job Queue (PostgreSQL SKIP LOCKED), Stripe Intent API.
  - **Mobile UX Flow (375px):**
    - Screen 1: "Work Feed" listing active AI negotiations.
    - Screen 2: "Agent Settings" with a single toggle ("Enable Auto-Booker").
    - Screen 3: "Conversation Review" allowing the owner to step in.

  ## 6. Implementation Prompt

  **User-Facing Outcome:** The user (e.g., Carlos) enables "Auto-Booker" with one tap. When a customer messages him via the public OHC link, the agent chats with the customer, quotes a standard service rate, captures a $50 deposit via Stripe Checkout, and adds the booking to Carlos's feed.

  **Critical User Journey:**
  1. Owner opens OHC on their phone (375px view).
  2. Taps "Enable Auto-Booker".
  3. Customer accesses the public intake link and messages "I need my sink fixed tomorrow."
  4. Agent responds, offers a slot, and requests a deposit.
  5. Customer pays; the booking appears in the Owner's Work Feed.

  **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - The agent successfully processes a natural language request and maps it to a service catalog.
  - The Stripe Checkout session is created autonomously.
  - The owner's feed is updated in real-time without manual refresh.
  - The UI perfectly fits a 375px screen with no horizontal scrolling.
