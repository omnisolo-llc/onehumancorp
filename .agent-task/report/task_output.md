issue_title: "Research and Design Autonomous Micro-Capital and Cash Flow Engine"
issue_description: |
  We have researched the architectural gap regarding proactive cash flow management for small businesses. Competitors like Shopify and Square offer capital, but it is heavily dashboard-dependent and reactive. OHC merchants face cash flow crunches before events/jobs (buying supplies). The proposed architecture is an Autonomous Micro-Capital Engine driven by the unified ledger and AI Finance Agent. It will proactively identify cash crunches (e.g. expenses logged exceeding balance) and instantly offer revenue-based financing (RBF) with zero paperwork via a mobile-first, zero-trust flow. Repayments will be automatically routed from future incoming transactions.
