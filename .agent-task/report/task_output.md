  ## 2. Deep-Dive Competitor Audit: Shopify
  - **Capabilities**: Full-stack e-commerce, endless app store, complex dashboard.
  - **Success Factors**: Unmatched ecosystem, scalability.
  - **Pain Points**: "Too complex for a side hustle", "App subscriptions eat margins", "No mobile-first AI help".
  - **User Sentiment**: HackerNews discussions show advanced users love the power; beginners (like Maya) find it paralyzing (e.g. HN item 37756182).

  ## 3. OHC Gap & Pain Point Identification
  - **OHC Feature Audit**: OHC currently has a basic drag-and-drop builder and manual settings configuration.
  - **Gap**: OHC lacks an invisible, proactive AI management layer. Users still have to *build* rather than *direct*.
  - **Unresolved Pain Point**: Users like Fatima and Carlos have no time to navigate a dashboard. They need to manage their business via simple text commands.

  ## 4. Agentic Solution Design: Conversational Store Manager
  - **Concept**: Replace the traditional dashboard with a Chat/Voice AI interface.
  - **Mechanism**: The user says "Update my holiday hours to 9-5" or "Create a 20% off discount for returning customers." The Agent executes it invisibly.
