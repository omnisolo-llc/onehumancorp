issue_title: "[Architecture] Autonomous Cross-Platform Migration Engine"
issue_description: |
  Research report and architectural design for the Autonomous Cross-Platform Migration Engine.
  This feature provides small business owners (like boutique owners) a "1-click magic wand" to instantly clone their existing digital business into the OHC platform, seamlessly migrating inventory, mapping variants, and recreating storefronts optimized for mobile from just a target URL, bypassing the "Migration Nightmare" of APIs or CSVs.

  The design involves an Autonomous Web/Social Scraping Agent that feeds raw data to an LLM Data Structuring Engine, which normalizes the data into OHC's Universal Capacity & Inventory Ledger. The system operates entirely within ephemeral sandboxes to ensure Zero Trust and multi-tenancy.
