# OHC Core Integration Research Report

## Overview
This research covers evaluations across 7 key integration categories critical for small business owners: Social Media Integration, Calendar & Scheduling, Email Marketing, Payment Processing, Shipping & Logistics, SMS & Notifications, and Video Conferencing.

## Findings by Category

### 1. Social Media Integration
- **Tool Evaluated**: WhatsApp Business API
- **Evaluation**: Deep integration for global communication. Essential for reaching customers where they are.
- **Action**: Addressed in `docs/research/[social_media]_whatsapp.md`

### 2. Calendar & Scheduling
- **Tool Evaluated**: Cal.com
- **Evaluation**: Open-source, flexible, self-hostable. Ideal for both cloud and standalone OHC deployments.
- **Action**: Addressed in `docs/research/[calendar]_cal_com.md`

### 3. Email Marketing
- **Tool Evaluated**: Listmonk
- **Evaluation**: High-performance, self-hosted newsletter and mailing list manager. Perfect for privacy-focused standalone modes.
- **Action**: Addressed in `docs/research/[email_marketing]_listmonk.md`

### 4. Payment Processing
- **Tool Evaluated**: Mercado Pago
- **Evaluation**: Crucial for LATAM markets. Supports local payment methods and fast settlements.
- **Action**: Addressed in `docs/research/[payment]_mercado_pago_q3.md`

### 5. Shipping & Logistics
- **Tool Evaluated**: EasyPost
- **Evaluation**: Unified API for multiple carriers, simple integration, good reliability.
- **Action**: Addressed in `docs/research/[shipping]_easypost_q3.md`

### 6. SMS & Notifications
- **Tool Evaluated**: Twilio
- **Evaluation**: Industry standard for SMS. Essential for low-English proficiency users and offline notifications.
- **Action**: Addressed in `docs/research/[sms]_twilio_q3.md`

### 7. Video Conferencing
- **Tool Evaluated**: Jitsi Meet
- **Evaluation**: Open-source, easily embeddable, no-account required for users. Great for standalone mode.
- **Action**: Addressed in `docs/research/[video]_jitsi_q3.md`
