  #### Pain Point 1: Invisible to AI Search (Priya - Boutique Operator)\n**Title:**\
  \ Agentic AEO Tracker\n**Problem Statement:** Priya needs her business to be visible\
  \ in AI searches.\n**Research Report:** With the rise of ChatGPT and Google's Universal\
  \ Commerce Protocol, traditional SEO is failing. Owners don't know if AI bots are\
  \ recommending their products.\n**Design Doc:** Add a new AEO Tracker widget to\
  \ the dashboard. The backend will use existing promoter agent logic, hitting the\
  \ LLM providers periodically. The UI will show a trend line and specific recommendations.\
  \ Mobile-first design: simple card with a big score.\n**Implementation Prompt:**\
  \ With the rise of ChatGPT and Google's Universal Commerce Protocol, traditional\
  \ SEO is failing. Owners don't know if AI bots are recommending their products.\n\
  **Agentic Mission:** **\"Agentic AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) Tracker\"**.\n\
  - **Outcome:** OHC's Promoter Agent autonomously queries top LLMs (Gemini, ChatGPT)\
  \ for Priya's product categories, tracks her brand's mention rate, and automatically\
  \ updates her site content to improve her AI search ranking.\n- **Acceptance Criteria:**\
  \ A user can view an \"AI Visibility Score\" on their dashboard, generated by the\
  \ agent without manual configuration.\n- **Priority:** P1\n- **Estimated Scope:**\
  \ Medium\n\n#### Pain Point 2: Inbox Overwhelm on Mobile (Maya - Home Baker)\n**Title:**\
  \ SMS-First Unified Executive Assistant\n**Problem Statement:** Maya needs to manage\
  \ DMs and emails without opening a complex app.\n**Research Report:** Managing business\
  \ across Instagram DMs, email, and texts leads to missed orders. Complex CRMs are\
  \ too slow for mobile.\n**Design Doc:** Integrate a new webhook for inbound SMS/iMessage\
  \ (e.g. Twilio). Route messages to a new 'unified triage' agent. Agent parses intent\
  \ and drafts replies. DB schema requires linking phone numbers to user accounts.\n\
  **Implementation Prompt:** Managing business across Instagram DMs, email, and texts\
  \ leads to missed orders. Complex CRMs are too slow for mobile.\n**Agentic Mission:**\
  \ **\"SMS-First Unified Executive Assistant\"**.\n- **Outcome:** Maya receives a\
  \ single SMS from OHC: \"You have 3 cake requests. I drafted replies and set up\
  \ payment links. Reply 'Send' to approve all.\"\n- **Acceptance Criteria:** Core\
  \ triage and drafting actions can be completely executed via an SMS/iMessage interface\
  \ without opening an app.\n- **Priority:** P0\n- **Estimated Scope:** Large\n\n\
  #### Pain Point 3: Setup Paralysis for Service Providers (Carlos - Field Service)\n\
  **Title:** Zero-Click Business Generator\n**Problem Statement:** Carlos needs to\
  \ setup a website and booking flow instantly.\n**Research Report:** Carlos needs\
  \ a website and booking flow but has zero time to configure it.\n**Design Doc:**\

  \ and create initial service offerings.\n**Implementation Prompt:** Carlos needs\
