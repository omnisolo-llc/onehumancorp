issue_title: "Growth Feature - Customer Acquisition Blocked"
issue_description: |
  # Research Report

  **Issue**: The implementation of new customer acquisition and virality loops (e.g., referral widgets, soft paywalls, milestone alerts) is currently blocked.

  **Problem Statement**: The legacy product strategy mandated the creation of viral loops and fixed-segment acquisition funnels. However, the current `RESEARCH.md` explicitly suspends the earlier fixed-price, exclusive agency segment, and fixed cohort hypotheses. The active requirement for "Acquire customers" is to measure "Qualified inquiries and attributable paid work, not impressions or generated copy alone."

  **Findings**:
  1. We currently lack an evidenced baseline for customer revenue vs OHC subscription conversion.
  2. The integration of accurate proposals and usable payment requests is still an open research uncertainty (see finding F08 in remediation ledger).
  3. We must establish tracking for denominators, consent, and attribution limitations before introducing acquisition campaigns, as generating unverified or non-attributable leads would violate the evidence-first policy.

  **Conclusion (No-Work)**: We are returning a no-work finding. We will not fabricate a viral feature or paywall without a proven baseline of attributable paid work.
