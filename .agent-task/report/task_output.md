  # Universal Autonomous Grant and Funding Engine

  Small business owners like Maya (baker), Carlos (handyman), and Priya (boutique owner) constantly struggle with cash flow and scaling capital. There are billions of dollars in local, state, federal, and private small business grants available annually. However, finding these grants, checking eligibility, and writing complex proposals is practically a full-time job. Most non-technical SMB owners don't have the time or expertise to apply, leaving free capital on the table.

  Current Platforms (Shopify Capital, Stripe Capital) provide revenue-based loans and cash advances, but they are *loans* that must be repaid. Grant Aggregators (Grants.gov, HelloAlice) require manual searching, profile creation, and essay writing.

  OHC already possesses the business's entire context—revenue history, location, industry, owner demographics, and growth trajectory. By leveraging this data, the OHC AI Swarm can cross-reference live grant databases, autonomously verify eligibility, and draft highly personalized, compelling grant proposals for the business owner to review and submit with one tap.

  This research brief proposes the backend schema, background orchestration workers, and AI agent integration points needed to support an autonomous funding matching and proposal generation engine.
