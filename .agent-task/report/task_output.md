  # Research Report: Cash Flow Forecasting
  Small business owners suffer from "Financial Fog," making spending decisions based on raw bank balances rather than predicted cash flow.

  **Gap Identified:** A real-time, event-driven Cash Flow Forecasting Engine that integrates deeply with OHC’s Omni-Channel AI Inbox, Booking/Quoting Engine, and unified Ledger to provide a true, forward-looking "Safe to Spend" balance.

  **Proposed Solution:** We propose building a forecasting engine that calculates a "Safe to Spend" metric by analyzing the ledger, recurring expenses, and open quotes. The AI Finance Agent will use this engine to proactively notify users of cash crunches and answer queries in plain language (e.g., "Can I afford this mixer?").
