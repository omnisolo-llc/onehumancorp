  # Automated Compliance & AI Contract Drafting Engine

  ## Problem Statement
  Small business owners like Carlos (Handyman) and Maya (Baker) often face significant liability risks but cannot afford lawyers. They rely on verbal agreements or generic templates, leaving them exposed to disputes, chargebacks, or liability claims (e.g., allergies for Maya, property damage for Carlos).

  They need an invisible way to generate airtight, situation-specific contracts and policies without understanding legalese.

  ## Research Report
  - **Shopify / Wix / Squarespace:** Offer generic templates for Terms of Service and Privacy Policies, but nothing dynamic or tailored to specific transactions (e.g., custom cake deposit agreement).
  - **GoDaddy:** Basic legal templates, not integrated into the checkout flow.
  - **Opportunity:** OHC can differentiate by deeply integrating the **Legal & Compliance Department** ("The Protector" AI) into every transaction. For example, if Carlos books a plumbing job, the AI automatically drafts a "Water Damage Liability Waiver" and attaches it to the deposit invoice for digital signature.

  ## Design Doc

  ### Architecture Overview (Mermaid.js)
  ```mermaid
  graph TD
      A[Customer Books Service/Order] --> B[Operations Engine]
      B --> C{Legal AI Agent 'The Protector'}
      C -->|Analyzes Job Context| D[Drafts Custom Contract/Waiver]
      D --> E[Stripe Payment Intent + Digital Signature Request]
      E --> F[Customer Signs & Pays]
      F --> G[Contract Secured in Vault - pgvector]
  ```

  ### Mobile UX Flow (375px)
  1. **Business Owner View:** Maya turns on "Require Custom Order Agreement" in Settings > Legal.
  2. **Customer View:** During checkout for a custom order, customer sees a clean, glassmorphic card: "Order Agreement."
  3. **Interaction:** Customer taps to expand, reads plain-language summary generated by AI (e.g., "Non-refundable deposit. Must report issues within 24 hours."), and signs with their finger on the native mobile screen.
  4. **Post-Checkout:** Both parties receive a PDF copy via the Omnichannel Inbox.

  ### AI Agent Integration
  - **Trigger:** Webhook from booking/order creation.
  - **Context:** Feeds the specific order details (item, dates, custom requests) and the tenant's business type to the LLM.
  - **Action:** Generates a legally sound (disclaimer attached) PDF and a plain-text summary.

  ### Key Design Decisions
  - Use serverless PDF generation (e.g., headless Chrome via Puppeteer or native Go PDF library).
  - Store signed hashes on a simple immutable ledger table to prove non-repudiation.
  - Fallback to generic templates if the AI generation fails or times out to not block checkout.

  ## Implementation Prompt
  **User Facing Outcome:** Implement the backend service and frontend UI for an automated contract generation system. A small business owner should be able to toggle "AI Contracts" on. When a customer books a specific service (like a custom cake or a home repair), the system must intercept the checkout flow, generate a contextual agreement, and require a digital signature before payment succeeds.

  **Acceptance Criteria:**
  1. Toggle in settings UI for "Enable AI Contracts".
  2. Intercept checkout flow to display the signature pad.
  3. Backend service that prompts the Legal AI with order context to generate the text.
  4. PDF generation and storage of the signed document.
  5. 100% test coverage and Playwright E2E test verifying a customer can sign and checkout.

  **Estimated Scope:** Medium
