## [research] Unified Social Inbox for Instagram, WhatsApp, Facebook, and TikTok

**Category:** Social Media Integration

### Problem Statement
Small business owners struggle to manage customer inquiries across multiple platforms. A customer might DM on Instagram, comment on a TikTok video, and follow up on WhatsApp. Trying to keep track of these fragmented conversations means lost leads, slow response times, and overwhelming context-switching for non-technical users like Fatima, who just wants to see all customer messages in one place without logging into four different apps.

### Research Report
The market for unified social inboxes is dominated by enterprise tools, leaving small businesses underserved.
Most tools charge per agent or per channel, making it cost-prohibitive for a solo entrepreneur or a small team.

When evaluating APIs, Meta's Graph API covers Instagram and Facebook but requires business verification, which can be a hurdle for the smallest merchants.
WhatsApp Business API requires a BSP (Business Solution Provider) or direct integration via Meta Cloud API, which is now more accessible but still requires technical setup.
TikTok's integration is relatively new and focuses heavily on comment moderation.

**Ease of Use for Small Business Owners:**
The ideal solution must completely abstract away the concept of 'webhooks' or 'API keys'. The user should just click 'Connect Instagram', authorize via a popup, and immediately see messages flow into OHC.

**Pricing Estimates:**
Tools in this space typically cost $20-$100/month. If OHC provides a basic unified inbox natively, it adds massive value and stickiness to our platform.

**OHC Mode Compatibility:**
- **Cloud Mode:** Easy to support. We can use central webhooks to receive messages and route them to the correct tenant.
- **Standalone Mode:** Tricky because local machines don't have public IP addresses for webhooks. We would need a cloud-relay service that receives webhooks and pushes them to the standalone client via WebSockets or long-polling, or simply use local polling where the API permits (though most rely on webhooks).

**Deep Dive on WhatsApp:**
WhatsApp is critical in regions like LATAM, India, and parts of Europe/Africa. Fatima runs her bakery entirely via WhatsApp. We must ensure WhatsApp integration allows rich media (images of cakes) and quick replies.

## [research] Frictionless Booking & Calendar Sync (Google & Outlook)

**Category:** Calendar & Scheduling

### Problem Statement
Small business owners lose hours every week playing phone tag or email ping-pong to schedule appointments. They need a simple, self-serve booking link to give to clients, but they also need it to sync perfectly with their personal Google or Outlook calendar so they don't get double-booked.

### Research Report
Scheduling is often the first workflow a service-based business digitizes.
The main friction point is calendar sync reliability. If a user gets double-booked once, they lose trust in the system forever.

**Ease of Use for Small Business Owners:**
The setup must be minimal. 'Connect Google Calendar', set working hours (e.g., 9 AM - 5 PM), and instantly get a shareable link (`ohc.com/book/fatimas-bakery`).

**Pricing Estimates:**
Standalone scheduling tools cost $10-$15/month. By bundling this into OHC, we save the user money and keep their customer data inside our ecosystem.

**OHC Mode Compatibility:**
- **Cloud Mode:** Perfect fit. We can manage OAuth tokens securely and handle background syncs and webhooks from Google/Microsoft.
- **Standalone Mode:** The booking page needs to be hosted somewhere public. For standalone users, we might need a lightweight OHC-hosted proxy that serves the booking page and securely tunnels the booking request down to the local app, or syncs via the user's connected cloud calendar as the source of truth.

**Timezone Handling:**
Crucial for online businesses. The booking page must auto-detect the client's timezone, but display appointments to the business owner in their local timezone.
