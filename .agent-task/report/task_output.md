# One Human Corp - Tool Integration Research Report

## Category: Calendar & Scheduling
### Problem Statement
Small business owners often struggle with manual scheduling, double-booking, and timezone confusion. They need a simple, automated way to sync their Google Calendar or Outlook and let clients book time directly without back-and-forth emails.

### Research Report
**Tools Evaluated:** Calendly, Cal.com, Google Calendar Sync
**Findings:** Cal.com offers the most developer-friendly API and a seamless white-label experience for both Cloud (multi-tenant) and Standalone (local) setups. Calendly is popular but its integration is more rigid and costlier per user. Google Calendar Sync is essential but requires complex OAuth flows.
**Pricing:** Cal.com provides a free tier for basic scheduling and paid tiers for team scheduling.
**Ease of Use:** High for end-users, simple booking page.

### Design Doc
- **Trigger:** A customer clicks "Book Consultation" on the business owner's OHC public page or inside an email.
- **Action:** OHC opens the integrated booking widget. Upon booking, it syncs to the owner's Google/Outlook calendar and OHC's internal calendar.
- **User View:** The business owner sees a unified schedule in their OHC dashboard and can set availability rules via a simple "Simple Mode" interface.

### Implementation Prompt
Implement a calendar synchronization feature that allows business owners to connect their existing calendars. Provide a unified view of upcoming appointments and allow them to set their availability. Ensure that when a client books a slot, the time is blocked across all connected calendars.

### Priority
P1 (High)

### Estimated Scope
Medium

## Category: Payment Processing
### Problem Statement
Business owners outside the US, particularly in LATAM and Asia, need localized payment options to receive funds. Stripe is not always supported or preferred in these regions.

### Research Report
**Tools Evaluated:** Mercado Pago (LATAM), Paytm (India), Alipay (China)
**Findings:** Mercado Pago dominates LATAM with easy integration and support for local payment methods (Pix, OXXO). Paytm is critical for the Indian market but requires strict KYC.
**Pricing:** Standard transaction fees apply (e.g., 2.9% + $0.30), varying by region.
**Ease of Use:** Mercado Pago provides simple checkout components suitable for non-technical owners.

### Design Doc
- **Trigger:** A customer proceeds to checkout on an invoice generated by OHC.
- **Action:** OHC routes the payment to the appropriate regional provider based on the business owner's configured preferences.
- **User View:** The business owner selects their preferred regional payment gateway in the OHC "Finances" tab and views a consolidated transaction history, regardless of the underlying processor.

### Implementation Prompt
Add support for Mercado Pago for business owners operating in Latin America. The business owner should be able to connect their Mercado Pago account via a simple button click in the settings. Invoices should display Mercado Pago as a payment option when enabled, and successful transactions should reflect in the unified OHC dashboard.

### Priority
P1 (High)

### Estimated Scope
Large

## Category: Video Conferencing
### Problem Statement
Online tutors, consultants, and service providers need to automatically generate meeting links when an appointment is booked. Manually creating and emailing Zoom/Meet links is error-prone and time-consuming.

### Research Report
**Tools Evaluated:** Zoom API, Google Meet API, Jitsi (Self-hosted)
**Findings:** Zoom and Meet are ubiquitous but require OAuth. Jitsi provides a zero-setup alternative but lacks brand trust. Zoom has complex rate limits, while Meet is tightly coupled with Google Calendar.
**Pricing:** Zoom API requires a Pro account. Google Meet is included with Workspace.
**Ease of Use:** High for end-users; automatic link generation is practically invisible.

### Design Doc
- **Trigger:** An appointment is successfully scheduled in the OHC Calendar.
- **Action:** OHC automatically provisions a meeting link using the connected Zoom/Meet account and attaches it to the calendar event.
- **User View:** The business owner sees the meeting link in their dashboard, and the client receives it automatically in their confirmation email.

### Implementation Prompt
Integrate automatic Zoom link generation for scheduled appointments. When a business owner connects their Zoom account, any new booking should automatically create a Zoom meeting and include the link in the notification sent to the client. The business owner should see a "Join Meeting" button directly in their OHC schedule view.

### Priority
P2 (Medium)

### Estimated Scope
Medium
