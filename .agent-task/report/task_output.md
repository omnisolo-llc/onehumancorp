issue_description: "# [Architecture] Real-Time Predictive Analytics & Mobile Reporting Engine\n\n## Problem Statement\nSmall business owners like Priya (boutique owner) and Carlos (handyman) need to make fast, data-driven decisions on the go. Currently, accessing business metrics requires logging into complex web dashboards that are not optimized for a 375px mobile viewport, leaving them flying blind regarding daily sales trends, inventory alerts, and cash flow projections. They need a simple, zero-configuration daily briefing and real-time dashboard right on their phone, telling them exactly what happened yesterday, what's happening now, and what to expect tomorrow\u2014without having to ask or dig for it.\n\n## Research Report\n### Current Landscape & Gap Analysis\n- **Shopify:** Offers comprehensive analytics but the mobile app experience is often cluttered. Advanced reports and predictive metrics require higher-tier plans or third-party apps.\n- **Wix / Squarespace:** Provide basic traffic and sales reporting. Predictive analytics (e.g., forecasting inventory depletion) are largely absent or rudimentary.\n- **Stripe:** Excellent financial reporting, but lacks deep integration with physical/digital inventory and operational metrics out-of-the-box for a non-technical user.\n\n### The OHC Opportunity\nOHC can differentiate by offering an **invisible, predictive reporting engine** that feels like a native iOS/Android widget. Instead of a \"reporting suite,\" the user gets a \"Daily Briefing\"\u2014a plain-language summary of operations, auto-generated by the AI Finance/Operations departments, backed by a real-time event-driven ledger. \n\n**Key Findings:**\n1. **Mobile-First is Mandatory:** 90%+ of our target personas run their business primarily from their phone.\n2. **Predictive > Retrospective:** Telling a baker they sold out of flour yesterday is less valuable than warning them they *will* sell out of flour by Thursday based on current order volume.\n3. **Plain Language:** Metrics like \"Conversion Rate\" or \"ARPU\" mean nothing to Maya or Fatima. They need: \"You made $400 today, which is 20% better than last Tuesday.\"\n\n## Design Doc\n\n### Architecture Diagram (Mermaid.js)\n```mermaid\ngraph TD\n    subgraph OHC Event Mesh\n        A[Sales Ledger] -->|Event| E(Event Bus / NATS)\n        B[Inventory Ledger] -->|Event| E\n        C[Booking Ledger] -->|Event| E\n    end\n\n    subgraph Analytics & Predictive Engine\n        E --> F[Real-Time Aggregator]\n        F --> G[(Time-Series DB / Cache)]\n        G --> H{AI Finance & Ops Dept}\n        H -->|Generates Briefing| I[Daily Briefing Cache]\n        H -->|Generates Alerts| J[Push Notification Service]\n    end\n\n    subgraph Mobile Client 375px\n        K[Mobile Dashboard Widget] -->|Fetches| I\n        L[Real-time Pulse View] -->|WebSockets| F\n        M[Push Alerts] <--> J\n    end\n```\n\n### UI Wireframes / Mobile UX Flow (375px)\n1. **The Morning Briefing (Push Notification):**\n   - *Lock screen:* \"Good morning Maya! \U0001F31E You have 3 custom cake orders due today and expected revenue is $450. Tap for your daily brief.\"\n2. **The Dashboard (Home Screen):**\n   - Translucent Glass styling, clean Unifi-style cards.\n   - **Top Card (The Pulse):** Big number: \"$1,240 Total Sales Today\". A simple, smooth sparkline chart indicating hourly trend.\n   - **Middle Card (Predictive Alerts):** \"\u26A0\uFE0F Vegan Frosting running low (est. empty by tomorrow). [1-Tap Reorder]\"\n   - **Bottom Card (Recent Activity):** Live feed of incoming transactions/bookings.\n3. **Deep Dive (Swipe Right):**\n   - Clean breakdown of Top Selling Items.\n   - Cash flow projection for the next 7 days based on scheduled bookings and historical data.\n\n### AI Agent Integration Points\n- **AI Data Analyst (Finance Dept):** Continuously monitors the time-series datastore. Runs anomaly detection (e.g., \"Sales are 50% lower than usual for a Friday\").\n- **AI Operations Agent (Ops Dept):** Correlates sales velocity with inventory levels to generate predictive stockout alerts.\n- **AI Copywriter (CS Dept):** Translates raw JSON data from the aggregators into the plain-language \"Daily Briefing\" tailored to the specific business context (e.g., uses food terms for Fatima, service terms for Carlos).\n\n### Key Design Decisions\n- **Event-Driven Aggregation:** We will not run heavy analytical `GROUP BY` queries on the primary transactional database. Instead, an event listener will maintain real-time materialized views/counters in a fast cache (e.g., Redis).\n- **Push vs. Pull:** The daily briefing is pre-computed and cached by AI agents during off-peak hours (e.g., 4 AM local time), ensuring zero latency when the user opens the app in the morning.\n- **Strict Multi-Tenancy:** The time-series data and aggregations must be strictly keyed by `tenant_id` to ensure absolute data isolation.\n\n## Implementation Prompt\n**Objective:** Implement the backend event aggregator and the mobile-first \"Daily Briefing\" API for the Real-Time Predictive Analytics Engine.\n\n**User Journey (CUJ):**\nAs a small business owner (like Priya), I want to open my OHC app and instantly see a plain-language summary of yesterday's performance and today's forecast, along with real-time sales numbers, so I can make quick decisions without digging through complex charts.\n\n**Acceptance Criteria:**\n1. Create an event consumer that listens to sales/booking events and updates real-time counters (daily revenue, order count) in a fast access store, segregated by `tenant_id`.\n2. Implement a background worker (simulating the AI Finance Dept) that runs daily, fetching the previous day's metrics and generating a structured JSON \"Briefing Object\" (containing plain-text summaries and predictive alerts).\n3. Expose a low-latency API endpoint that returns the pre-computed briefing and real-time pulse data, optimized for rendering on a 375px mobile UI.\n4. Ensure the design strictly adheres to Zero-Trust multi-tenant isolation principles.\n5. All outputs must support localization (e.g., formatting for different currencies and languages, like Arabic for Fatima).\n\n**Note:** Do not worry about the exact AI prompt generation in this step; focus on the data pipeline, caching, and API delivery.\n\n## Estimated Scope\nMedium\n"
