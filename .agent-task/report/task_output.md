  # Research Report: AI-Driven Dynamic Pricing & Promotional Yield Engine

  **Problem:**
  Small business owners guess at pricing, miss peak season revenue opportunities, and struggle to clear slow-moving inventory because dynamic yield management tools are complex, enterprise-focused, and manual.

  **Findings & Competitor Landscape:**
  - **Shopify/Wix:** Rely on static pricing or expensive third-party apps for dynamic rules.
  - **Airlines/Hotels:** Use complex yield management systems inaccessible to SMBs.
  - **OHC Opportunity:** Utilize the "Business Advisory Agent" to continuously monitor sales velocity, upcoming booking calendar availability, and seasonal trends. The AI will proactively suggest dynamic pricing adjustments (e.g., peak premiums) or targeted promotions to clear inventory, surfaced via a "1-Tap Approve" notification on the owner's mobile feed.

  **Proposed Next Steps:**
  - Designed the architecture for an opt-in, AI-driven yield engine.
  - The Advisor agent formulates suggestions while protecting minimum profit margins.
  - Integration with the Sales agent to broadcast approved promotions automatically.
  - Submitted `docs/research/[architecture]_ai_driven_dynamic_pricing_and_promotional_yield_engine.md` detailing the data model evolution, Mermaid sequence diagram, and mobile UX flow.
