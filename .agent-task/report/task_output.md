  ## OHC Owner Work Assistant: Competitive Research & Agentic Missions

  ### 1. Market Mapping & Competitor Discovery (Track 1)
  We conducted dynamic internet research to map the 2025 landscape of owner/operator work assistants, spanning traditional giants and rising AI-native pioneers.

  **Top 10 General Competitors**
  1. **Shopify** (shopify.com) - E-commerce giant, targeting serious merchants with Sidekick, a proactive commerce-obsessed AI assistant for site edits, reporting, and marketing.
  2. **Wix** (wix.com) - Drag-and-drop website builder offering Wix Studio AI for generative website creation from prompts and AI-powered section generators.
  3. **Squarespace** (squarespace.com) - Design-focused builder for creatives with Squarespace Blueprint for AI-guided design and content generation.
  4. **Square** (squareups.com) - Commerce and POS platform utilizing Square AI for automated product descriptions, photo background removal, and smart inventory alerts.
  5. **HubSpot** (hubspot.com) - CRM and marketing platform offering Breeze, consisting of AI agents (Prospecting, Customer Service, Content) integrated deeply into CRM data.
  6. **WooCommerce** (woocommerce.com) - WordPress-based e-commerce with WooCommerce AI for product description generator and automated SEO metadata.
  7. **BigCommerce** (bigcommerce.com) - Enterprise-focused e-commerce using AI Predictive Analytics for proactive sales forecasting and customer churn prediction.
  8. **GoDaddy** (godaddy.com) - Basic website builder bundled with domains featuring GoDaddy Airo for automated brand identity creation including logos and social media ads.
  9. **Weebly** (weebly.com) - Basic AI text generation for landing pages and simple POS integrated builder.
  10. **PrestaShop** (prestashop.com) - AI-powered translation and product categorization modules for open-source e-commerce.

  **Top 10 AI-Native Competitors**
  1. **Durable** (durable.co) - **30-Second Setup:** Generates a complete business website, CRM, and invoicing in under a minute.
  2. **10Web** (10web.io) - **AI WordPress Manager:** Instantly recreates any website design on WordPress using AI agents.
  3. **Mixo** (mixo.io) - **Idea Validation:** Targeted at pre-revenue startups to launch lead-capture pages via one sentence.
  4. **Framer AI** (framer.com/ai) - **Vibe Coding:** High-end design output from natural language prompts, bypassing designers.
  5. **Lindy.ai** (lindy.ai) - **AI Executive Assistant:** Handles email triage, scheduling, and admin tasks via iMessage/SMS.
  6. **Relevance AI** (relevanceai.com) - **AI Workforce:** Allows non-technical owners to build autonomous agentic teams for sales and ops.
  7. **Skyvern** (skyvern.com) - **Browser Automation:** AI browser agents that can log into any portal to download invoices or fill forms.
  8. **11x.ai** (11x.ai) - **Alice & Julian:** Autonomous digital workers for outbound sales and inbound phone handling.
  9. **Intercom Fin** (fin.ai) - **Resolution Engine:** AI agent that resolves 50%+ of support queries without human intervention.
  10. **AGI (On-Device)** (agi.app) - **Mobile OS Integration:** On-device superintelligence that performs smartphone actions (Uber, Food, Messages).

  ### 2. Deep-Dive Competitor Audit: Shopify Sidekick & Durable (Track 2)

  #### Shopify Sidekick & Magic
  - **Capabilities:** Edits site themes, drafts emails, analyzes pricing strategy, generates weekly summaries, and creates "Sidekick Pulse" health signals.
  - **Success Factors:** Deep integration with 8,000+ apps. "Shop Pay" provides a zero-friction checkout for buyers.

    - *“Sidekick is okay, but it just tells me how to do things instead of just doing them for me.”* (App Store review)

  #### Durable.co
  - **Capabilities:** Autonomous website generation, integrated invoicing, and a simple AI business advisor.
  - **Success Factors:** Zero technical hurdle. Targeted at service providers (Handymen, Photographers).
  - **User Sentiment Audit:**
    - *“Fastest way to get a site up, but the SEO needs work and I can't customize it enough.”* (Trustpilot).

  ### 3. OHC Gap & Pain Point Identification (Track 3)

  #### OHC Feature Audit
  OHC has a robust **KAIROS** orchestration engine and specialized services (`booking`, `quoting`, `pos`, `delivery`). However, it lacks the "Zero-to-One" autonomous experience found in Durable and the deep "Invisible Automation" of HubSpot Breeze.

  #### Gap Matrix

  | Feature | OHC (Vision) | Shopify Sidekick | Durable AI | Lindy | Gap to Close |
  | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
  | **Mobile-first Setup** | Yes (< 10 mins) | No (Desktop preferred) | Yes (< 1 min) | N/A | OHC needs fully native mobile onboarding |
  | **AI-Native Execution** | Yes (Agents *do* the work) | Partial (Chatbots *advise*) | Partial | Yes | OHC must automate tasks, not just advise |
  | **All-in-one Pricing** | Yes | No (App fees add up) | Yes | N/A | OHC must bundle bookings + commerce |
  | **Site Generation** | 🟡 | 🟢 | 🟢 | 🔴 | OHC needs Zero-Click onboarding |
  | **Email Triage** | 🟢 | 🟡 | 🔴 | 🟢 | None |
  | **Booking Logic** | 🟢 | 🟡 | 🟡 | 🟢 | Eliminate need for 3rd party apps |

  #### Unresolved Pain Points
  1. **The "App Tax" Fatigue**: SMBs hate piecing together disparate tools (e.g., Shopify's fragmented app ecosystem).
  2. **Setup Paralysis**: The initial blank canvas is terrifying for non-technical users. 34% of small business owners abandon setup due to "technical complexity" (Reddit aggregation).
  3. **Advice vs Action**: Current AI tools are glorified manuals. SMBs want an AI that executes rather than just explaining how to do it.

  ### 4. Deeper Focused Research & Agentic Solutions (Track 4)

  #### Persona-Specific Pain Points & Solutions

  **Pain Point 1: Setup Paralysis (Maya - Home Baker)**
  - **Evidence:** Maya wants to sell cakes, not configure DNS. She needs an integrated booking + deposit system without paying for three different apps.
  - **Agentic Mission:** **"Zero-Click Onboarding Agent"**.
  - **Outcome:** Maya chats with OHC for 5 minutes. The agent provisions her domain, configures Stripe for custom deposits, and creates her first product from a photo.
  - **Acceptance Criteria:** A user can go from login to a published product link using only natural language.

  **Pain Point 2: Missed Leads (Carlos - Field Service)**
  - **Evidence:** Service businesses lose ~30% of leads because the owner is "on the job" and can't answer calls (Field Service Forum). Carlos needs an auto-quoting agent based on customer inquiry, not a complex CRM.
  - **Agentic Mission:** **"Agentic Negotiator & Booker"**.
  - **Outcome:** An AI agent intercepts calls/DMs, checks Carlos's calendar, quotes a price based on project type, and takes a $50 deposit.
  - **Acceptance Criteria:** Agent successfully books a meeting and secures payment without owner intervention.

  **Pain Point 3: Language Barriers (Fatima - Food Cart)**
  - **Evidence:** "I struggle with English-speaking customers on the phone while cooking." (Fatima persona proxy).
  - **Agentic Mission:** **"Multilingual Order Interceptor"**.
  - **Outcome:** Agent handles phone orders in English, translates them into Fatima's native language on her tablet KDS (Kitchen Display System).
  - **Acceptance Criteria:** Real-time translation of voice-to-text orders with high accuracy.

  ### 5. Design Doc & Visual Excellence

  #### High-level Architecture & Implementation Prompt
  - **Architecture:** Develop an "Operations Manager" Agent Protocol. Implement a core service layer that allows AI agents to securely execute CRUD operations on behalf of the user, moving from advisory AI to executing AI. Use Gemini Pro as primary LLM. Leverage PostgreSQL SKIP LOCKED pattern for job dequeue and Redis Redlock for cross-agent coordination.
  - **Implementation Prompt:** Prioritize a Flutter-based mobile onboarding sequence that relies entirely on a single conversational prompt (e.g., "I'm a baker in Austin"). Ensure the data schema natively supports both physical products and service bookings without third-party apps. Provide a fully functional 375px wide experience.
