  # OHC Research & Mission Brief: The Agent Action Feed

  ## 1. Problem Statement
  Business owners (like Maya the baker and Carlos the handyman) are overwhelmed by fragmented systems and dashboards designed for desktop. They need to manage their entire operation from a 375px mobile screen. When an inquiry comes in, a booking changes, or inventory drops, legacy tools force owners to click through multiple tabs. There is a profound gap for an "Invisible AI Assistant" that turns fragmented signals into a unified, actionable feed where agents propose drafts, actions, and summaries, and the owner simply taps "Approve" or "Reject".

  ### Track 2: Deep-Dive Competitor Audit (Shopify Sidekick)
  **Capabilities:** Sidekick acts as an embedded chat interface inside the Shopify admin panel. It answers "How do I do X", writes product descriptions, sets up discounts, and summarizes sales performance.
  **Success Factors:** It eliminates the need for merchants to hunt through complex settings menus. It understands the store's context.
  **User Sentiment Audit:** Users on r/ecommerce praise Sidekick for saving time on repetitive tasks (like bulk product edits). However, a major complaint is its desktop-centric nature; merchants running their business from their phones find the chat interface cramped and disconnected from real-time customer DMs or operational alerts.
  *Evidence Quote:* "I love Sidekick for writing product descriptions, but when I'm at my pop-up shop, I don't have time to chat with an AI. I need it to just tell me what to do and give me a button to press."

  ### Track 3: OHC Gap & Pain Point Identification
  **OHC Feature Audit:** OHC currently has robust backend agent routing and job queues, but the mobile UI lacks a unified presentation layer for proactive agent suggestions.
  **Gap Matrix:**
  | Capability | Shopify Sidekick | Legacy Dashboard | OHC Target Vision |
  | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
  | **Proactive Suggestions** | Moderate (chat based) | Low | High (Feed-based) |
  | **Mobile-First UX** | Low | Low | High (Native 375px) |
  | **Multi-Channel Inbox** | Low | Moderate | High (Unified) |

  **Unresolved Pain Points:**
  - **Persona (Maya, 28, Baker):** Managing DMs across Instagram and WhatsApp while baking. Needs pre-drafted replies and deposit links ready to send with one tap.
  - **Persona (Carlos, 42, Handyman):** Misses leads when on a ladder. Needs an agent to automatically reply to missed calls with a booking link and surface the new lead in his feed.

  ### Track 4: Deeper Focused Research & Agentic Solutions
  To solve the "complex actions on small screens" problem, the traditional dashboard must be replaced by a **Unified Agent Feed**.
  Instead of navigating to a "Discounts" page, the owner sees an Action Card generated by the Marketing Agent: "Traffic is slow today. Want to offer a 10% flash sale to your top 50 customers?" -> [Approve] [Edit] [Dismiss].

  ## 3. Design Doc

  **Architecture & Entities:**
  - `AgentAction`: An entity representing a proposed task (e.g., drafted reply, suggested booking, inventory alert).
  - `ActionFeed`: The presentation layer aggregating `AgentAction`s for a specific `tenant_id`.

  **UI Flow (Mobile 375px First):**
  1. User opens the app.
  2. The Home Screen is a vertical feed (The Agent Feed).
  3. Cards are styled with OHC Premium Tokens (translucent glass, strong hierarchy).
  4. Each card displays:
     - Context (e.g., "New inquiry from Sarah")
     - Drafted AI Response/Action (e.g., "Hi Sarah, yes I am available next Tuesday...")
     - Action Buttons: `Send`, `Edit`, `Dismiss`.

  ## 4. Implementation Prompt
  **User-Facing Outcome:** Build a mobile-first (375px) "Unified Agent Feed" page. When an owner logs in, they see a clean, scrollable list of actionable cards proposed by their AI assistants.
  **Critical User Journey:**
  1. Log into the OHC app.
  2. Navigate to the Home/Feed page.
  3. View at least one mock (or real) Action Card with a drafted response.
  4. Tap "Approve" and see a success state.
  **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - Layout must be flawless on a 375px width.
  - Implement using OHC design tokens (glassmorphism, clear typography).
  - Fully functional E2E test verifying the card approval flow.
  - Zero mock data in production; pull from a real (or seeded) database endpoint.
