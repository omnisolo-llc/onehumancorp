  # [architecture] Autonomous AI Voice Receptionist Research Report

  ## Problem Statement
  Small business owners like Carlos (the handyman) and Fatima (the food cart operator) often miss phone calls while they are busy serving customers or working on jobs. A missed call often means lost revenue, as impatient customers will simply call the next business on Google Maps. While the Unified Omnichannel AI Ambassador handles text-based inquiries (SMS, WhatsApp, Instagram), voice calls remain a critical channel that goes unanswered or straight to a generic, non-interactive voicemail. Small businesses need an invisible, real-time AI voice agent that can answer calls, understand context (e.g., booking calendar, inventory, pricing), negotiate schedules, take messages, and route complex issues seamlessly.

  ## Research Report
  *   **Current State:** Most small businesses rely on basic voicemail or expensive human answering services.
  *   **Competitor Analysis:** Platforms like Shopify and Wix focus almost exclusively on text/web chat. Dedicated voice AI solutions (like Bland AI or Vapi) are complex to integrate, requiring API keys and webhook configurations that are beyond the technical expertise of our target personas.
  *   **OHC Differentiation - "Zero-Config Voice":** The Autonomous AI Voice Receptionist is tightly integrated with the OHC ecosystem. When a user connects their business phone number, the voice agent instantly inherits knowledge of the business's catalog, calendar, and active orders. It dynamically generates responses using real-time text-to-speech (TTS) and speech-to-text (STT) without any developer configuration.

  ### UI Wireframes & 375px Baseline
  **Core Layout: macOS-style Translucent Glass + Ubiquiti UniFi Modular Dashboard Cards**
  *   **Global Viewport:** 375px width (Mobile First). No horizontal scrolling.
  *   **App Bar:** Blurred glass top nav with the business logo and a quick toggle: `[AI Voice: Active / Voicemail]`.
  *   **Inbox Feed Integration:** Call logs appear natively in the Unified Inbox.
      *   A call card has a frosted glass background (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(10px)`).
      *   **Badging:** A phone icon indicates a voice call.
      *   **Status Indicators:** A green checkmark indicates "Issue Resolved" (e.g., appointment booked). A red pulse indicates "Action Required" (e.g., call dropped or complex issue needing a callback).
  *   **Call Details View:**
      *   Displays a structured summary of the call generated by the AI (e.g., "Customer wanted to know if we fix leaky pipes. I quoted $100/hr and booked them for tomorrow at 2 PM.").
      *   Includes a full transcript in a scrollable, translucent container.
      *   Provides an embedded audio player with a waveform visualization for the recording.
      *   Quick action buttons: `[Call Back]`, `[Send SMS Quote]`.

  ### Mobile UX Flow
  1. **The Call:** A customer calls Carlos's business number while he is driving.
  2. **AI Intercept:** The AI Receptionist answers: "Hi, you've reached Carlos's Handyman Services. How can I help you today?"
  3. **Resolution:** The AI answers pricing questions and books an appointment.
  4. **Notification:** Carlos receives a push notification: "📞 AI Voice booked a plumbing repair for tomorrow at 2 PM."
  5. **Review:** Carlos opens the app, sees the summary card in his inbox, and taps to view the transcript or listen to the audio if he wants to verify.

  ### AI Agent Integration Points
  *   **Reception Department:** Manages the real-time conversation flow, handling interruptions and maintaining a natural conversational tone.
  *   **Operations & Booking Department:** Consulted during the call to check calendar availability (e.g., "Are you free Tuesday?") or inventory (e.g., "Do you have the vegan cake available today?").
  *   **Finance Department:** Engaged if the customer asks for an estimate or needs to pay a deposit over the phone (triggering a follow-up SMS with a payment link).

  ### Key Design Decisions (Why, not How)
  *   **Integrated into Unified Inbox:** Small business owners shouldn't have to check a separate app or tab for phone calls. Voice interactions are just another type of conversation and must be normalized into the same `UNIFIED_THREAD` model as SMS and Instagram DMs.
  *   **Real-time & Low Latency:** The architecture must prioritize ultra-low latency for STT and TTS. Awkward pauses break the illusion of a helpful receptionist. The `VOICE_GATEWAY` must be highly optimized.
  *   **Zero-Trust Isolation:** Phone calls contain sensitive PII. The `VOICE_GATEWAY` must enforce strict multi-tenant isolation, ensuring audio streams and transcripts are strictly bound to the correct `organization_id`.

  ## Implementation Prompt
  **To the Implementer Swarm:**
  Your goal is to build the backend infrastructure and UI to support the Autonomous AI Voice Receptionist, allowing OHC users to have an AI answer their business calls and log the interactions in their Unified Inbox.

  **Customer User Journey (CUJ):**
  1. User activates the "AI Receptionist" feature in their OHC settings.
  2. A customer calls the provided business phone number.
  3. The real-time speech engine streams the audio, translates it to text, processes it through the Ambassador Agent, and generates a spoken response with low latency.
  4. After the call concludes, a summary, full transcript, and audio recording are seamlessly added to the user's Unified Inbox.

  **Acceptance Criteria:**
  *   **Mobile Parity:** The UI for viewing call summaries and transcripts must be implemented for a 375px viewport using the Translucent Glass aesthetics.
  *   **Voice Gateway Mock:** Implement a robust mock of the `VOICE_GATEWAY` that simulates an incoming call, audio streaming, and interaction with the AI backend.
  *   **Inbox Integration:** Ensure that completed call logs appear natively within the existing Unified Inbox feed, alongside text-based messages.
  *   **Isolation Guarantee:** Enforce strict multi-tenant boundaries for call recordings and transcripts based on `organization_id`.
