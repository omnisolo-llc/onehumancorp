  # Research Report: AI-Automated Quoting & Invoicing Architecture

  ## Problem Statement
  Service-based owners and agency principals (e.g., Carlos the handyman, Nora the agency principal) lose hours every week manually translating customer conversations into formal quotes, generating invoices, and chasing down payments. Traditional tools (like QuickBooks or standalone Stripe Invoicing) force the owner to leave their communication context, manually enter line items, and configure reminder schedules. This context-switching is a massive friction point for non-technical users running their business primarily from a smartphone.

  ## Research Report
  - **Codebase & Market Gap:** Currently, OHC lacks a unified Quote-to-Cash data model and the agentic workflow required to turn conversational agreements into actionable, localized invoices.
  - **Competitor Analysis:**
    - *Shopify/Wix:* E-commerce first, very clunky for custom service quotes.
    - *Stripe Invoicing / QuickBooks:* Powerful, but highly manual and requires dedicated administrative time. Not conversational or agent-driven.
    - *Square:* Good mobile POS, but quoting is still a manual form-filling exercise.
  - **The OHC Opportunity:** By leveraging the Sales and Finance AI Agents, OHC can read a conversational agreement (e.g., "I can fix that tomorrow for $200 labor and $50 parts"), autonomously draft a structured quote/invoice, and present a 1-tap "Approve & Send" card to the owner on their mobile device.

  ## Design Doc

  ### Architecture Diagram
  ```mermaid
  graph TD
      A[Customer Chat / Email] --> B[Unified Inbox]
      B --> C[Sales AI Agent]
      C -->|Extracts Pricing & Scope| D[Draft Quote/Invoice]
      D --> E[Owner Mobile Feed - Action Required]
      E -->|1-Tap Approve| F[Finance AI Agent]
      F -->|Create Stripe Payment Link| G[Dispatch to Customer]
      G --> H[Stripe Webhook]
      H -->|payment_intent.succeeded| I[Ledger & Invoice Update]
      I --> J[Operations AI Agent - Schedule Work]
  ```

  ### Data Model & Invariants
  - **`Invoice`**: `id`, `tenant_id`, `customer_id`, `status` (draft, sent, partially_paid, paid, overdue), `currency`, `total_amount`, `due_date`.
  - **`InvoiceLineItem`**: `id`, `invoice_id`, `description`, `quantity`, `unit_price`, `tax_rate`.
  - **`PaymentEvent`**: `id`, `invoice_id`, `amount`, `stripe_payment_id`, `timestamp`.
  - **Multi-Tenant Isolation**: Enforced via PostgreSQL `ENABLE ROW LEVEL SECURITY` on `tenant_id` for all tables.
  - **Invariants**: An invoice cannot transition to `paid` unless the sum of `PaymentEvent`s equals `total_amount`.

  ### Mobile UX Flow (375px First)
  1. **The Trigger**: The owner is chatting with a customer in the OHC unified inbox.
  2. **AI Action**: A translucent, frosted-glass card pops into the feed: "Draft Quote Ready: Sink Repair ($250)".
  3. **Review Screen**: Tapping the card opens a clean, bottom-sheet summary showing line items ($200 Labor, $50 Parts).
  4. **Execution**: A large, 44x44px primary button "Send Payment Link".
  5. **Follow-up**: If unpaid after 48 hours, the Finance Agent surfaces a new card: "Send friendly reminder to [Customer] for $250?"

  ### AI Agent Integration Points
  - **Sales Agent (The Promoter):** Listens to text/voice inputs from the owner to extract line items and draft the initial quote.
  - **Finance Agent (The Accountant):** Monitors the invoice due dates, drafts culturally localized payment reminders, and reconciles Stripe webhooks with the PostgreSQL ledger.
  - **Operations Agent (The Manager):** Once the invoice or deposit is paid, it automatically unblocks the associated task or booking on the calendar.

  ## Implementation Prompt
  **Task**: Implement the end-to-end AI-Automated Quoting & Invoicing capability.
  **User Journey**:
  1. As an owner (Carlos), I receive a customer request.
  2. The AI reads my conversational response containing price details and generates a Draft Invoice in the database.
  3. I see a Draft Invoice card in my mobile feed (375px optimized).
  4. I tap "Approve", which finalizes the invoice, generates a payment link via Stripe, and updates the invoice status to `sent`.
  **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - Create the PostgreSQL schema for Invoices and Line Items with RLS enabled.
  - Implement the AI service logic to extract line items from a text prompt and construct a draft invoice.
  - Build the mobile-first UI components (Draft Card, Invoice Detail Bottom Sheet) using OHC Premium Tokens (translucent glass, clean typography).
  - Ensure 100% unit test coverage for the invoice state machine and E2E Playwright coverage for the owner approval flow.
  - DO NOT use any mock data; the UI must render real draft invoices generated by the backend.
