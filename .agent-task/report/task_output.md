# Scout: Tool Integration Research Q4 Final Report

## Executive Summary
This report details the evaluation of seven critical tool categories to expand OHC's capabilities for small business owners in both Cloud and Standalone environments. The focus is strictly on tools that solve real pain points for non-technical users, prioritizing ease of use, cost-effectiveness, and reliability.

## Evaluated Categories and Recommended Tools

### 1. Social Media Integration
- **Recommended Tool**: ManyChat
- **Pain Point**: Fragmented communication across Meta platforms and WhatsApp.
- **Solution**: Unified inbox within OHC.
- **Priority**: P1

### 2. Calendar & Scheduling
- **Recommended Tool**: Cal.com
- **Pain Point**: "Email ping-pong" for booking appointments and timezone confusion.
- **Solution**: Automated booking links with calendar sync.
- **Priority**: P0

### 3. Email Marketing
- **Recommended Tool**: Mailchimp
- **Pain Point**: Manual, unscalable customer re-engagement.
- **Solution**: Drag-and-drop broadcast emails synced with OHC customer lists.
- **Priority**: P2

### 4. Payment Processing (Regional Focus: LATAM)
- **Recommended Tool**: Mercado Pago
- **Pain Point**: High failure rates and lack of local payment methods (PIX, OXXO) with standard gateways.
- **Solution**: Localized checkout options for LATAM customers.
- **Priority**: P1

### 5. Shipping & Logistics
- **Recommended Tool**: EasyPost
- **Pain Point**: Manual rate calculation and copy-pasting addresses for label generation.
- **Solution**: Automated rate fetching and one-click label printing.
- **Priority**: P1

### 6. SMS & Notifications
- **Recommended Tool**: Twilio
- **Pain Point**: Customers missing critical email updates (e.g., order ready, appointment reminders).
- **Solution**: Automated transactional SMS alerts.
- **Priority**: P1

### 7. Video Conferencing
- **Recommended Tool**: Zoom
- **Pain Point**: Manual creation and distribution of meeting links for virtual appointments.
- **Solution**: Auto-generated meeting URLs attached to bookings.
- **Priority**: P2

## Next Steps
Detailed issue briefs for each of the recommended tools have been generated in the `docs/research/` directory. These briefs contain problem statements, design docs, and implementation prompts tailored for the engineering team.
