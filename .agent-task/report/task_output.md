  Research and architectural design for the Invisible AI Intake and Diagnostic Engine. This feature addresses the pain point of manual back-and-forth communication required to gather requirements for service-based and custom-order businesses. The design uses Conversational and Vision AI to dynamically gather details from the customer (e.g., photos of a repair issue or custom requests) and compiles a Diagnostic Brief for the merchant to easily generate a quote from their mobile device. The design document includes the problem statement, competitive analysis, Mermaid.js architecture, UI wireframes adhering to the design system, and an implementation prompt.
