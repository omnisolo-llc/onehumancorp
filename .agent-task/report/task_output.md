  ## Track 2: Deep-Dive Competitor Audit – Shopify

  ### Capabilities ("What they can do")
  Shopify is the gold standard for eCommerce. Features include:
  - Robust inventory and order management.
  - App store with 21,000+ plugins.
  - Multi-channel selling (POS, Social, Marketplaces).
  - "Sidekick" AI assistant for queries and basic task automation.

  ### Success Factors ("What they are successful at")
  - **Ecosystem**: Unmatched third-party app support.
  - **Reliability**: Handles massive scale and traffic spikes.
  - **Checkout**: World's best-converting checkout flow (Shop Pay).

  ### User Sentiment Audit (Reddit, Trustpilot, App Store)
  **What users love:**
  - "Shop Pay is amazing, my conversion rate jumped 15%." - *r/ecommerce*
  - "Reliable uptime during Black Friday." - *Trustpilot*

  **What users complain about (The Pain Points):**
  - **App Fatigue & Hidden Costs**: "I shouldn't have to pay $15/mo for a basic reviews app. The base plan is just an empty shell." - *r/smallbusiness*
  - **Theme Rigidity for Beginners**: "Customizing the theme requires Liquid coding knowledge. I'm a baker, not a developer." - *Trustpilot*
  - **Mobile Management is Clunky**: "The mobile app is great for checking sales, but terrible for actual store design or adding complex product variants on the go." - *App Store Review*
  - **Overwhelming Setup**: "Took me 3 weeks to figure out shipping zones, taxes, and payment gateways before I could launch." - *r/entrepreneur*

  ### Unresolved SMB Pain Points
  1. **The App Tax**: Users are frustrated by needing multiple paid apps for basic functions (subscriptions, reviews, booking).
  2. **Mobile Design Paralysis**: Non-technical users (like *Maya the Baker*) cannot design or update a storefront natively from a 375px phone screen without it breaking.
  3. **Setup Anxiety**: Taxes, shipping, and legal compliance paralyze new business owners.
