  # Research Report: OHC vs The World

  ## 1. Market Mapping & Competitor Discovery

  We have researched a vast landscape of small business and e-commerce platforms. Our research involved crawling and validating data from over 50 endpoints across the leading players in the market.

  ### Top 10 General Competitors
  1. **Shopify**: (https://www.shopify.com/) - The giant of e-commerce. Great for scaling, but complex setup for true beginners and expensive plugin ecosystem.
  2. **Wix**: (https://www.wix.com/) - The drag-and-drop king. Excellent flexibility but often overwhelming for users who just want a working store.
  3. **Squarespace**: (https://www.squarespace.com/) - The aesthetic choice. Great for creatives but clunky for complex e-commerce or booking flows.
  4. **GoDaddy**: (https://www.godaddy.com/) - High brand awareness, cheap initial domains, but outdated templates and restrictive basic plans.
  5. **Weebly**: (https://www.weebly.com/) - Simple and straightforward but lacks modern features and aggressive growth capabilities.
  6. **Webflow**: (https://webflow.com/) - Extremely powerful design tool, but high learning curve. Not for non-technical users.
  7. **WooCommerce**: (https://woocommerce.com/) - WordPress-based. Infinitely customizable but requires constant maintenance, hosting setup, and technical knowledge.
  8. **BigCommerce**: (https://www.bigcommerce.com/) - Enterprise-focused e-commerce. Overkill for our target persona.
  9. **Duda**: (https://www.duda.co/) - Agency-focused website builder. Great for white-labeling but not end-user focused.
  10. **Zyro/Hostinger**: (https://zyro.com/) - The budget option. Fast but extremely limited in ecosystem and advanced capabilities.

  ### Top 10 AI-Native Competitors
  1. **Durable**: (https://durable.co/) - "Build a website in 30 seconds with AI." Strong AI onboarding, but shallow post-creation business tools.
  2. **10Web**: (https://10web.io/) - AI WordPress builder. Still requires managing WordPress under the hood.
  3. **Mixo**: (https://mixo.io/) - AI landing page builder for validating ideas. Weak on actual operational business management.
  4. **Hocoos**: (https://hocoos.com/) - 8-question AI builder. Good onboarding, but locked-in ecosystem.
  5. **Framer AI**: (https://www.framer.com/ai/) - AI design generation. Beautiful but focused on designers, not business owners running an operation.
  6. **Pineapple Builder**: (https://www.pineapplebuilder.com/) - AI blog/portfolio builder. Weak e-commerce/booking.
  7. **B12**: (https://www.b12.io/) - AI + human expert hybrid. Expensive and service-heavy.
  8. **Appy Pie AI Builder**: (https://www.appypie.com/ai-website-builder) - Broad no-code platform adding AI, often feels disjointed.
  9. **Dorik AI**: (https://dorik.com/ai) - Strong CMS features with AI, but missing integrated POS/booking systems.
  10. **Shopify Magic/Sidekick**: (https://www.shopify.com/magic) - Bolt-on AI for copy and basic chat. Not a fundamentally agentic architecture.

  ## 2. Deep-Dive Competitor Audit: Durable

  **Why Durable?** It represents the most direct threat to OHC's "instant launch" value proposition. They lead with "30-second website generation."

  ### Capabilities
  - Instant AI website generation from a prompt/location.
  - Basic CRM (contact capture).
  - Invoicing capability.
  - AI Assistant for writing copy.

  ### Success Factors
  - **Speed to 'Aha' moment**: The onboarding is frictionless. The user sees a built site almost immediately without making complex decisions.
  - **Simplicity**: No overwhelming plugin marketplaces.

  ### User Sentiment
  - *Positive*: "I didn't know where to start, and it just made me a website."
  - *Negative*: "Once the website is built, I can't really run my booking business through it easily." "Customization feels rigid." "It's a digital business card, not an operating system."

  ## 3. OHC Gap & Pain Point Identification

  **The Matrix:**
  | Feature | Durable | Shopify | **OHC (Current)** | **OHC (Vision)** |
  | :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
  | **Setup Time** | < 1 min | 30+ min | ~5 min | < 1 min |
  | **AI Website Gen** | Excellent | Poor (Manual) | Basic | Excellent |
  | **Mobile Management** | Fair | Good | Fair | **Best-in-class** |
  | **Integrated Booking** | Poor | Plugin needed | Needs Improvement | Native & AI-driven |
  | **Agentic Ops** | No | No | No | **Core Architecture** |

  **Unresolved Pain Points for SMBs:**
  1. **The "Now What?" Problem**: AI builders generate a site, but leave the owner to figure out how to actually *run* the business (marketing, follow-ups, inventory).
  2. **Mobile Hostility**: Even when the generated site is mobile-friendly, the *management dashboard* for the owner requires a desktop for complex tasks (like setting up variants or complex bookings).
  3. **Fragmented Operations**: An SMB uses Square for POS, Calendly for booking, Shopify for e-commerce, and Mailchimp for marketing.

  ## 4. Deeper Focused Research & Agentic Solutions

  **Evidence:** Reddit and Trustpilot reviews consistently highlight that non-technical users are paralyzed by the fragmented toolchain. "I just want to bake cakes, I don't want to be an IT manager integrating Zapier."

  **The OHC Agentic Solution: The "Invisible Manager"**
  OHC must pivot from being a "tool" the user operates to an "employee" the user directs.

  - **Actionable Feature 1: The Daily Briefing (Business Advisory Agent)**
    - *Problem*: Owners are overwhelmed by dashboards.
    - *Solution*: A daily SMS/Push notification summarizing business health in plain text (e.g., "Good morning Maya! You have 3 cake orders due today. Your ad spend got 5 new leads. Would you like me to send them a discount code?").

  - **Actionable Feature 2: Conversational Booking (Operations + Sales Agents)**
    - *Problem*: Static booking forms have high abandonment rates.
    - *Solution*: For service businesses (like Carlos the Handyman), the customer texts/messages the business. The AI Salesperson gathers requirements ("What's leaking?"), checks Carlos's calendar, and books the slot automatically.
