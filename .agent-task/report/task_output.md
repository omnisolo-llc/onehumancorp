issue_title: "[Architecture] Zero-Touch Dynamic Pricing & Yield Management Engine"
issue_description: |
  **Research Report & Findings:**
  Small business owners—particularly those in services, food, and perishables—struggle to maximize revenue from expiring inventory or unbooked time slots. Currently, adjusting prices manually across menus or calendars is time-consuming and often forgotten.

  Competitor platforms (Shopify, Wix, Squarespace) rely on static pricing models, making flash sales difficult without manual intervention or expensive plugins. Enterprise yield management solutions (like those used by airlines or Uber) are too complex for SMBs.

  **Proposed Next Steps:**
  Implement a "Zero-Touch Dynamic Pricing & Yield Management Engine" that operates silently in the background. It will use the autonomous agent framework (Operations, Finance, Marketing) to monitor the Capacity and Inventory Mesh and automatically adjust prices down to ensure 100% sell-through (or up during peak demand). This engine will update edge caches dynamically.

  The user experience should be extremely simple: a "Smart Pricing" toggle and a "Maximum Discount" slider, hiding all algorithmic complexity from the small business owner.
