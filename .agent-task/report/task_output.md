  # Research Report: Autonomous Dynamic Pricing & Yield Management System

  ## 1. Problem Statement
  Small business owners with expiring inventory or time-sensitive availability (e.g., Fatima the Food Cart Operator with unsold daily meals, or Leo the Music Tutor with unbooked afternoon slots) struggle to maximize their revenue and minimize waste. They lack the time and analytical tools to monitor demand in real-time and dynamically adjust prices to stimulate sales before the "asset" expires or the window closes. Existing platforms require manual price adjustments and flash sale setups, which are too time-consuming for active operators.

  ## 2. Research Report
  - **Market Context**: Large enterprises (airlines, hotels, Amazon) use sophisticated yield management and dynamic pricing algorithms to maximize revenue per available unit. In the SMB space, platforms like Shopify or Wix allow for manual discount codes or scheduled sales, but this requires active intervention. There is no built-in, autonomous system that monitors expiring assets and acts on them.
  - **The OHC Opportunity**: By leveraging the Operations and Sales AI Agents, OHC can democratize yield management. The system can autonomously detect when an item is nearing expiration (e.g., end of day for food, or 2 hours before an unbooked appointment) and automatically deploy localized flash sales or dynamic discounts to clear the inventory.
  - **Competitor Gaps**:
    - *Shopify/Wix*: Require manual creation of discount codes and manual marketing broadcasts.
    - *UberEats/DoorDash*: Do offer dynamic pricing, but take large cuts and control the customer relationship, disintermediating the owner.

  ## 3. Design Doc
  ### Architecture & Data Model (PostgreSQL)
  - `YieldProfile`: Configuration attached to a Product or Service (e.g., "Perishable", "Time-Slot"). Defines the decay curve and minimum acceptable price floor.
  - `DynamicPriceOverride`: A temporary price applied to a product, generated by the agent, valid for a specific duration.
  - `YieldEvent`: Records the trigger (e.g., "2 hours to close, 10 meals left") and the agent's action.

  ### Architectural Diagram
  ```mermaid
  graph TD
      A[Operations Agent] -->|Monitors Inventory & Time| B(YieldProfile)
      B -->|Trigger Condition Met| C{Yield Event Generated}
      C --> D[Generate DynamicPriceOverride]
      C --> E[Sales/Marketing Agent]
      D --> F[Database PostgreSQL]
      F --> G[Cache Invalidator Service]
      G --> H[Purge CDN Edge Cache]
      E --> I[Draft & Send Flash Sale Notification SMS/Email]
      I --> J[Customer Receives Alert]
      J --> K[Customer Checks out at new Dynamic Price]
  ```

  ### AI Agent Coordination
  - **Operations Agent**: Continuously monitors inventory levels against time (using a time-series approach or scheduled checks) based on the `YieldProfile`.
  - **Sales/Marketing Agent**: When the Operations Agent flags excess expiring inventory, the Sales Agent drafts a flash sale notification (Push, SMS, or Email) to a segmented list of local/past customers and applies the `DynamicPriceOverride`.

  ### Mobile UX Flow (375px)
  1. **Owner View (Dashboard)**: A "Yield Management" card appears in the feed. "You have 10 Halal Platters left and 2 hours until close. Approve a 20% flash sale?" (1-tap approve). Or, if fully autonomous mode is enabled, it simply reports: "Cleared 8 expiring meals via automated 15% flash sale."
  2. **Customer View**: Receives a push/SMS: "Flash Sale: 20% off Halal Platters for the next hour!" Tapping links directly to a fast-checkout storefront with the dynamic price applied.

  ### Edge & CDN Considerations
  - When the `DynamicPriceOverride` is applied, the cache invalidator must immediately purge the product's edge cache tag so the new price is reflected instantly on the storefront.

  ## 4. Implementation Prompt
  **Feature Name**: Autonomous Dynamic Pricing & Yield Management Engine
  **Target Persona**: Fatima the Food Cart Operator
  **Outcome**: Fatima no longer throws away unsold food at the end of the day. The system detects surplus inventory near closing time, automatically discounts it to her preset floor, and notifies nearby past customers, turning potential waste into recovered revenue.

  **Next Actions**:
  1. Implement `YieldProfile` and `DynamicPriceOverride` models with multi-tenant isolation.
  2. Create a background worker (or integrate with existing scheduler) that evaluates inventory against yield profiles.
  3. Extend the Sales/Marketing Agent to generate and dispatch flash-sale notifications based on yield events.
  4. Build the mobile-first "1-Tap Approve" UI card for the owner's feed, and ensure the edge cache invalidation correctly picks up dynamic price changes.
