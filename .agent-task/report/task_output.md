# 📊 Global SMB Market & Competitor Research Report

## Executive Summary
OneHumanCorp (OHC) is positioned to disrupt the SMB platform market by transitioning from a "tool-based" paradigm (Shopify, Wix) to an "agent-based" paradigm. Current platforms are too complex for the average non-technical user (e.g., Maya, 28, Baker) and require hours of configuration. OHC's unique advantage is utilizing AI as an autonomous employee rather than just a setup wizard.

## Target Personas & Core Pain Points
1. **Maya (Baker, 28)** - Sells via Instagram DMs.
   *Pain:* Overwhelmed by Shopify's setup; loses sales due to slow DM responses.
2. **Carlos (Handyman, 42)** - Word-of-mouth only.
   *Pain:* Manual quoting and back-and-forth scheduling via text.
3. **Priya (Boutique Owner, 35)** - In-store + online ambition.
   *Pain:* Inventory sync and complex email marketing.
4. **Leo (Music Tutor, 22)** - Online + in-person.
   *Pain:* Manual booking and tracking subscription payments.
5. **Fatima (Food Cart, 50)** - Pre-orders for pickup, limited English.
   *Pain:* Existing tools are too complex and English-centric.

## Market Sizing & Go-To-Market
- **TAM:** 27M+ non-employer businesses in the US; 330M+ globally.
- **Beachhead:** The "Social Seller" (e.g., Maya). Focus heavily on Instagram/WhatsApp DM auto-replies to capture immediate value.
- **Expansion:** Move towards service providers (smart scheduling) and local food (POS integration).

## Actionable Feature Missions
### Mission 1: AI Auto-Reply System for Social Channels
**Problem Statement:** Small business owners spend hours manually replying to the same questions via DMs and lose sales when they can't reply instantly.
**Design Doc:** OHC Message Bus connects via Webhooks to IG/WhatsApp. AI Agent queries Inventory/FAQs and autonomously generates replies.
**Implementation Prompt:** Implement a unified message ingestion pipeline that connects to Instagram and WhatsApp webhooks, routing to an AI agent capable of parsing inquiries and matching inventory/custom FAQ context. (Priority P0, Scope Large)

### Mission 2: Zero-Config One-Click Storefront Generation
**Problem Statement:** Non-technical owners find website builders too complicated and need an instant, shareable URL.
**Design Doc:** User input routes to LLM Store Generator, which creates entities in OHC DB and deploys a PWA to a CDN.
**Implementation Prompt:** Create a zero-configuration onboarding flow that parses a simple text business description to automatically provision a tenant, catalog, default theme, and live URL. (Priority P0, Scope Medium)

### Mission 3: AI-Powered Smart Booking System
**Problem Statement:** Service providers struggle with text-based scheduling ping-pong and manual calendar tracking.
**Design Doc:** AI Agent interacts directly with Client over SMS, queries OHC Calendar for availability, reserves slots, and triggers billing/deposit flows.
**Implementation Prompt:** Implement a smart booking engine integrated with the messaging bus and billing system, supporting natural-language availability queries, time-slot reservation, and pre-payment capture. (Priority P1, Scope Large)

We have identified significant gaps in the market where legacy players fail to support true "mobile-first, zero-config" workflows. Four actionable feature missions have been drafted to drive the next iteration of the engineering roadmap.
