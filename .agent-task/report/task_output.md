issue_title: "[architecture] The 'Invisible Local Delivery & Inventory Mesh' for Predictive Omnichannel Restock"
issue_description: |
  **Problem Statement:** Maya (baker) and Priya (boutique owner) suffer from "Omnichannel Sync Nightmare." Physical inventory and digital storefronts desynchronize easily. When stock runs low, they find out reactively. Existing platforms (Shopify) require manual stock taking and app combinations. Furthermore, managing local ad-hoc deliveries is chaotic and manual.

  **Research Report:** Competitors provide static ledgers that merely report numbers. Shopify requires 3rd party apps to handle dynamic routing or predictive reordering. OHC will build an "Invisible Local Delivery & Inventory Mesh." This proactively monitors sales velocity across online and POS. When stock drops below dynamic thresholds, the Operations Agent automatically drafts a reorder to the supplier and presents it for 1-tap approval. For local deliveries, it automatically optimizes drop-off routes and texts customers ETAs.
