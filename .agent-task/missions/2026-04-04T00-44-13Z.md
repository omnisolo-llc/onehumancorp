# 🎨 Palette: [Hybrid UX improvement] Visualizing pgvector Memories

## Problem Statement
The `swarm_memory_screen.dart` visualization for pgvector memories and the realtime Teammate Mesh lacked the high-fidelity micro-animations and proper OHC Glassmorphism styling required by the Visual Excellence Mandate.

## Implementation Details
1. **Glassmorphism Upgrade**: Applied the specific OHC Glassmorphism matrix filter (with 200% saturation) to both `_AnimatedGlassMessageCard` and `_AnimatedMemoryCard`.
2. **Micro-animations**: Transitioned from stateless cards to `StatefulWidget` to manage `MouseRegion` hover states. Implemented `AnimatedScale` (1.0 to 1.02) and `AnimatedContainer` for smooth hover transitions.
3. **Typography**: Ensured strictly 'Outfit' and 'Inter' font families were used throughout the visualizer components.

## Testing
- Modified and validated tests inside `srcs/app/lib/screens/swarm_memory_screen_test.dart`.
- Executed `flutter test` to ensure stability.
