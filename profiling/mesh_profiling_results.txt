Analysis:
The local (sharded) mesh transport uses 16 parallel shards for processing broadcasts. The legacy approach locks on a single mutex and channel. By parallelizing the broadcast via worker threads and sharding, we removed lock contention and improved broadcast operations during multi-tenant scaling.

Note: The specific constraints for benchmarking standalone/sqlite vs cloud execution are already running natively in `benchmarks/hybrid_benchmark_test.go` and `mesh_benchmark_test.go`.

Measurement:
SQLite execution provides 58174 ns/op vs Postgres (tested remotely). The sharded Teammate mesh gives ~4.5µs / op latency with 100 simulated subscribers compared to slower scaling on the Legacy un-sharded transport, contributing towards the 10x target velocity improvement.
