# KAIROS AI OS Orchestration Walkthrough

The KAIROS Orchestration layer is the foundation of the OneHumanCorp autonomous workforce. It ensures that your business runs smoothly, efficiently, and with perfect memory.

## The KAIROS Triad

### 1. The Brain: Shared Task List
The **Shared Task List** is where all business logic and agent assignments live. KAIROS intelligently prioritizes tasks based on your business goals, ensuring that the most important work always gets done first.

### 2. The Nerves: Teammate Mesh
The **Teammate Mesh** is a high-speed communication layer that connects all agents in your swarm. It allows for real-time coordination, instant updates, and seamless handoffs between specialized agents.

### 3. The Memory: AutoDream
**AutoDream** provides your autonomous team with infinite long-term memory. Every interaction, customer preference, and business decision is stored and indexed, allowing your agents to learn and grow alongside your business.
