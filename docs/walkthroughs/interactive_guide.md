# Interactive Walkthroughs

If you prefer to be guided step-by-step while you use the app, we have Interactive Walkthroughs!

Instead of reading a long page, the app will actually highlight the buttons you need to press and tell you exactly what to do next. It is like having a helpful friend standing right next to you.

## How to start a Walkthrough

1. Go to the page where you want help (for example, the **Products** page).
2. Tap the `?` icon in the top corner.
3. Tap **Start Walkthrough**.

A small blue highlight will appear on the first button you need to press, along with a little speech bubble explaining what to do. Just follow the bubbles!

## Available Walkthroughs

Try these popular walkthroughs to learn the basics quickly:

*   **"Add a Product in 60 Seconds"**: Found on the Products page.
*   **"Set Up Bank Transfers"**: Found on the Settings > Payments page.
*   **"Send Your First Email Newsletter"**: Found on the Marketing page.
*   **"Hire Your First AI Agent"**: Found on the My Team page.

Do not worry about messing anything up while you learn. The walkthroughs will guide you safely through the whole process.
