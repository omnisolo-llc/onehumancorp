# Guide 38: Essential Strategies for Non-Technical Founders

## Introduction
Welcome to this essential guide designed exclusively for non-technical small business owners using One Human Corp (OHC).
In this guide, we will cover strategies to maximize your efficiency without requiring any coding knowledge.

### Section 1: Setting up your workspace
1. Navigate to the dashboard.
2. Click on the Settings icon.
3. Choose your preferred layout.

### Section 2: Managing AI Agents
Your AI agents are your employees. Treat them with clear instructions:
- Provide plain language goals.
- Do not use jargon.
- Trust the orchestration layer.

### Section 3: Payments and Invoicing
1. Connect your bank account via the Integration tab.
2. Generate an invoice by clicking "New Invoice".
3. Send it directly to your client via the platform.

### Conclusion
By following these steps, you will save hours of manual work every week. If you need more help, return to the Help Center.
