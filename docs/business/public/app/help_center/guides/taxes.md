# Managing Taxes

Taxes are complicated, but they don't have to be scary. OneHumanCorp helps you collect the right amount of sales tax from your customers so you can pay the government at the end of the year.

*Disclaimer: OneHumanCorp does not provide legal or tax advice. You should always consult with a professional accountant to ensure you are meeting all local laws.*

## Automatic Tax Calculation

The easiest way to handle taxes is to let OHC do it for you. We partner with secure tax databases to know exactly what the sales tax is in every city and state.

1. Go to **My Store > Settings > Taxes**.
2. Click **Enable Automatic Taxes**.
3. You will need to tell us where your business is physically located (your "Nexus"). If you have a shop in California, you must collect California sales tax.
4. Once enabled, when a customer checks out, OHC will look at their address and automatically add the correct tax percentage to their total bill.

## Manual Tax Calculation

If you prefer to set the tax rates yourself, or if you live in a region not supported by automatic taxes, you can set manual rules.

1. Go to **My Store > Settings > Taxes**.
2. Select **Manual Tax Configuration**.
3. Click **Add Tax Rule**.
4. Choose a region (e.g., "New York").
5. Enter the tax percentage (e.g., "8.875%").
6. Check the box if tax should also be applied to the shipping cost (this depends on local laws).
7. Click **Save Rule**.

## Tax-Exempt Customers

Sometimes you will sell to another business or a non-profit organization that does not have to pay sales tax.

1. Go to the **Customers** tab.
2. Find the customer and click on their profile.
3. Click **Edit Customer Details**.
4. Check the box that says **"This customer is tax-exempt."**
5. (Optional) Enter their official Tax Exemption ID number for your records.
6. Click **Save**.

The next time this customer logs in and buys something, they will not be charged any tax.

## Generating Tax Reports for Your Accountant

At the end of the year (or quarter), you need to give the tax money you collected to the government. We make it easy to see exactly how much you owe.

1. Go to the **Analytics** tab.
2. Click on **Financial Reports**.
3. Select **Sales Tax Collected**.
4. Choose the dates you want to look at (e.g., January 1st to March 31st).
5. Click **Export as CSV**.

You can email this file directly to your accountant. It will show them exactly how much tax was collected in every city and state.
