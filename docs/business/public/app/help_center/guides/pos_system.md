# Using the Point of Sale (POS) System

If you have a physical storefront or sell at farmers markets, you can use OneHumanCorp's Point of Sale (POS) system to ring up customers in person.

## Getting Started with POS

The OHC POS runs on any modern iPad or Android tablet.

1. Download the "OneHumanCorp POS" app from the App Store or Google Play.
2. Log in using your normal OHC email and password.
3. The app will automatically sync all your products, prices, and inventory levels from your online store.

## Ringing Up a Sale

1. On the main POS screen, you will see a grid of your products. Tap a product to add it to the cart.
2. Alternatively, you can use the search bar at the top to find an item quickly.
3. If you have a Bluetooth barcode scanner connected, simply scan the item's barcode.
4. To apply a discount, tap the "Add Discount" button below the cart and enter the percentage or dollar amount.
5. Tap the big green **Charge** button.

## Accepting Payments

You can accept several types of payment through the POS.

- **Cash:** Tap "Cash", enter the amount the customer handed you, and the app will tell you how much change to give back.
- **Credit Card (Manual Entry):** You can type the credit card numbers in manually. Note: This charges a slightly higher processing fee.
- **Credit Card (Reader):** If you purchased the OHC Card Reader, ensure it is turned on and paired via Bluetooth. Tap "Card Reader" and have the customer tap, dip, or swipe their card.

## Printing or Emailing Receipts

After the payment is successful, you will see the receipt screen.

1. **Email/Text:** Ask the customer for their email address or phone number and type it in. They will instantly receive a digital receipt.
2. **Print:** If you have a compatible receipt printer connected to your network, tap "Print Receipt."
3. **No Receipt:** If the customer doesn't want one, just tap "Done."

## End of Day Register Close

At the end of your shift, you should close your register to ensure your cash drawer matches your sales data.

1. Tap the menu icon in the top left.
2. Select **Register Details**.
3. Tap **Close Register**.
4. Count the physical cash in your drawer and enter the total amount.
5. The system will tell you if you are over or under based on the day's recorded cash sales.
6. Click **Confirm Close** to generate your daily sales summary report.
