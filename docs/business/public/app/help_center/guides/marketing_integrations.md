# Marketing Integrations

To supercharge your sales, OneHumanCorp integrates directly with major marketing platforms. This guide explains how to connect your store to Facebook, Instagram, and Google.

## Connecting to Facebook and Instagram

By connecting your store to Facebook, you can automatically sync your products and run targeted ad campaigns.

1. Go to **My Store > Settings > Marketing**.
2. Find the "Facebook & Instagram" integration and click **Connect**.
3. You will be prompted to log in to your Facebook account.
4. Select the Facebook Page and Instagram Account associated with your business.
5. Choose your "Facebook Pixel" (or create a new one). The pixel tracks what visitors do on your website, which helps Facebook show your ads to the right people.
6. Click **Finish Setup**.

Once connected, your OHC products will automatically appear in your Facebook Shop and Instagram Shop.

## Running Google Shopping Ads

Google Shopping ads show pictures of your products directly at the top of Google search results. This is one of the most effective ways to find new customers.

1. Go to **My Store > Settings > Marketing**.
2. Find the "Google Shopping" integration and click **Connect**.
3. Log in with your Google account.
4. You will need to link your "Google Merchant Center" account. If you don't have one, OHC can help you create it automatically.
5. Set your daily budget (e.g., \$10/day).
6. Click **Launch Campaign**.

Our integration automatically updates Google whenever your prices change or an item goes out of stock, so you never pay for clicks on sold-out items.

## Setting Up Email Marketing (Mailchimp/Klaviyo)

While OHC has basic email tools built-in, you might want to use a dedicated email platform for advanced campaigns (like abandoned cart emails).

1. Go to **My Store > Settings > Integrations**.
2. Find your email provider (e.g., Mailchimp) and click **Connect**.
3. Enter your API Key from that provider.
4. Map your OHC customer lists to your email lists.
5. Click **Sync Now**.

This ensures that whenever a customer signs up for your newsletter during checkout, their email is automatically sent to your marketing tool.
