# Adding Team Members

As your business grows, you might need help. OneHumanCorp allows you to invite your employees, accountants, or partners to access your dashboard. You have full control over what they can see and do.

## How to Invite a New Team Member

1. Click on your profile picture in the top right corner.
2. Select **Account Settings**.
3. Go to the **Team Members** tab.
4. Click **Invite Member**.
5. Enter their email address.
6. Choose their Role (see below for explanations).
7. Click **Send Invitation**.

They will receive an email with a link to create their own password. They will not share your password.

## Understanding Roles and Permissions

You shouldn't give everyone the keys to the castle. We provide three standard roles to keep your data safe.

### 1. The Administrator
- **What they can do:** Everything. They can change billing details, delete the store, add new team members, and view all financial data.
- **Who gets this:** Only you (the owner) or a trusted business partner.

### 2. The Manager
- **What they can do:** They can add products, process refunds, create marketing campaigns, and edit the website design.
- **What they CANNOT do:** They cannot change your bank details, upgrade your subscription plan, or delete the store.
- **Who gets this:** Your store manager or main employee.

### 3. The Analyst (Or Accountant)
- **What they can do:** They can view all sales data, export tax reports, and see the analytics dashboard.
- **What they CANNOT do:** They cannot change the website, add products, or issue refunds. They can only look, not touch.
- **Who gets this:** Your accountant or bookkeeper.

## Removing a Team Member

If an employee leaves your company, you must remove their access immediately.

1. Go to the **Team Members** tab.
2. Find their name in the list.
3. Click the three dots next to their name.
4. Select **Remove Access**.
5. Confirm your choice.

They will be logged out instantly and will not be able to log back in.
