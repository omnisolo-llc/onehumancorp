# Search Engine Optimization (SEO) Basics

Search Engine Optimization (SEO) is the process of making your store show up higher on Google when people search for things you sell. This guide covers the easy steps you can take to improve your ranking.

## Writing Good Product Titles

Your product title is the most important piece of SEO information. Think about what a customer would actually type into Google.

*   **Bad Title:** "The Sparkle 5000"
*   **Good Title:** "Silver Diamond Engagement Ring - The Sparkle 5000"

Always include the descriptive words (material, color, type of item) before your creative name.

## Writing Detailed Descriptions

Google's robots read your descriptions to understand what you sell.

1. Write at least two full paragraphs for every product.
2. Naturally include words related to your item (e.g., if selling a mug, use words like "coffee," "tea," "ceramic," "handmade," "microwave safe").
3. Do not just paste a list of keywords. Google will penalize you for "keyword stuffing." Write for humans first, robots second.

## Editing Meta Titles and Descriptions

When you search on Google, the blue link is the "Meta Title" and the gray text below it is the "Meta Description." You can customize these in OHC.

1. Edit a product and scroll down to the "Search Engine Preview" section.
2. Click **Edit Website SEO**.
3. **Page Title:** Keep it under 60 characters so it doesn't get cut off. (e.g., `Buy Handmade Ceramic Coffee Mugs | YourStoreName`)
4. **Description:** Keep it under 160 characters. Write a compelling sentence that makes people want to click. (e.g., `Shop our collection of microwave-safe, artisan-crafted ceramic mugs. Perfect for your morning coffee. Free shipping over $50!`)

## Optimizing Images

Google cannot "see" images, it can only read the text attached to them.

1. Before uploading an image, rename the file on your computer from `IMG_9942.jpg` to something descriptive like `red_leather_handbag.jpg`.
2. After uploading the image in OHC, click on it and add "Alt Text". Alt Text should be a literal description of the image (e.g., "A woman holding a red leather handbag standing on a city street"). This also helps visually impaired users who use screen readers.

## Getting Reviews

Google loves active, trustworthy businesses. Getting your customers to leave reviews on your OHC store or your Google Business profile is one of the strongest signals you can send to search engines that your store is legitimate.
