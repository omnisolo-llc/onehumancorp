# Growing Your Business (Marketing)

Having a great store is only half the battle. The other half is getting people to visit it! The Marketing tab gives you simple tools to reach out to customers and offer them deals.

## How to Create a Discount Code

Everybody loves a sale. You can create special codes that give customers money off their purchase.

1. Go to the **Marketing** tab.
2. Click **Create Promo Code**.
3. **Name the Code:** Type what the customer will type at checkout. Make it easy to remember, like `SUMMER20` or `FREESHIP`.
4. **Choose the Discount:**
   - *Percentage:* Take 10% or 20% off the total price.
   - *Fixed Amount:* Take exactly \$5.00 off the total.
5. Click **Save Code**.

Now, you can post that code on your social media or email it to your friends!

## Sending Email Updates to Customers

When people buy from you, they can choose to join your email list. Sending them an email is a great way to tell them about new products or special sales.

1. In the **Marketing** tab, click **Send an Email Update**.
2. **Subject Line:** Write something catchy. (Example: "Our new winter coats are here!")
3. **The Message:** Keep it short and friendly. Add a picture of your new product if you have one.
4. Click **Send Email**.

*Important Note: Please do not send emails every single day. People might get annoyed and unsubscribe. Once a week or twice a month is usually best!*

## Understanding Your Store Visitors (Analytics)

It helps to know how many people are looking at your store.

At the top of the **Marketing** screen, you will see a simple chart showing "Store Visits."

- **Visits:** How many times someone looked at your website today.
- **Sales:** How many of those people actually bought something.

If you have 100 visits but 0 sales, it might mean your prices are too high, or your photos aren't clear enough. If you have 5 visits and 2 sales, you have a great product, you just need to get more people to look at your site!
