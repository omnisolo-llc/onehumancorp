# What's New?

We are always updating OneHumanCorp to make it easier for you to run your business. Here is a plain-language list of the newest features and fixes.

---

## April 2026 Update

### 🚀 Better Mobile Store Previews
You asked, we listened! It used to be hard to see exactly what your store looked like on a customer's phone before you hit "Publish". Now, when you design your store, there is a dedicated "Phone Preview" button that perfectly matches what your customers see.

### 🤖 AI Agent "Tone" Settings
Your AI agent can now match your personality. When setting up your AI Support Agent, you can now choose if it should sound "Professional and Polished" or "Friendly and Casual."

### 🐛 Bug Fixes
- Fixed an issue where some users couldn't upload photos larger than 5MB. You can now upload large, high-quality photos.
- Fixed a confusing error message that showed up when trying to refund a payment that had already been refunded.

---

## March 2026 Update

### 🛍️ Digital Products
You can now sell things that aren't physical objects! If you write an eBook, create a music track, or sell a digital sewing pattern, you can upload the file to OHC. When a customer buys it, the system automatically emails them the download link. No shipping required!

### 📊 Simpler Analytics
We redesigned the "Store Visits" chart on the Marketing page. It now clearly shows you exactly which days of the week you get the most traffic, so you know the best time to run your sales.
