# Getting Paid

Getting paid should be the easiest part of running a business. This page explains how money moves from your customer's credit card into your bank account.

## How to Accept Credit Cards

Before a customer can buy anything, you must connect your bank. We use a secure system so we never actually see your bank password.

1. Go to the **Payments** tab.
2. Click **Connect Bank**.
3. Follow the instructions on the screen to log into your bank safely.
4. Once connected, your store will automatically let customers pay using Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Apple Pay.

---

## When Do I Get My Money? (Payouts)

When a customer buys something, the money doesn't instantly appear in your bank. It takes a little bit of time for the banks to talk to each other. This process is called a "Payout."

- **First Payout:** Your very first payout might take up to 7 days. The banks need extra time to make sure you are a real business.
- **Normal Payouts:** After your first payout, money usually arrives in your bank account in **2 business days**.

You can check when your next payout is coming by looking at the top of the **Payments** screen.

---

## What Are the Fees?

We believe in simple, transparent pricing. We do not charge monthly fees just to use the basic payments system. We only make money when you make money.

- **Standard Fee:** For every successful credit card charge, the fee is **2.9% + 30 cents**.
- *Example:* If you sell a shirt for \$10.00, the fee is \$0.59. You will receive \$9.41 in your bank account.

There are no hidden fees, no setup fees, and no cancellation fees.

---

## How to Refund a Customer

Sometimes things go wrong, or a customer changes their mind. Refunding them is easy.

1. Go to the **Payments** tab.
2. Look at the list of "Recent Transactions."
3. Click on the customer's name or the order you want to refund.
4. Click the red **Refund** button.
5. You can choose to refund the full amount, or just a part of it.
6. Click **Confirm Refund**.

*Note: It usually takes 5 to 10 days for the refunded money to show up on the customer's credit card statement. Please tell them to be patient!*
