# Getting Started with OneHumanCorp

Welcome to OneHumanCorp! We built this app to help you run your small business without needing a computer science degree. Whether you are selling handmade crafts or running a local service, this guide will help you get set up in just a few minutes.

## Your First 3 Steps

To get the most out of OHC, we recommend doing these three things first:

1. **Set up your Store profile.**
2. **Connect your bank account to take payments.**
3. **Turn on your AI Support Agent.**

---

### Step 1: Set Up Your Store Profile

Your store profile is what your customers will see.

1. Click on the **My Store** button on the main menu (it looks like a little shop building).
2. Click **Store Settings**.
3. Type in your business name. Make sure it's the name your customers know you by.
4. Upload a logo or a nice photo of your business.
5. Write a short description of what you do. Keep it simple and friendly!
6. Click **Save Changes**.

*Tip: You can always come back and change these details later.*

---

### Step 2: Connect Your Bank Account

To get paid, you need to tell us where to send the money. We use secure connections, so your bank details are always safe.

1. Click on the **Payments** button on the left side of your screen.
2. Click **Connect Bank**.
3. A safe window will pop up asking you to log into your bank. Follow the steps on the screen.
4. Once you finish, you will see a green checkmark. You are now ready to accept credit cards!

[Read more about Payments and Fees](payments.md)

---

### Step 3: Turn On Your AI Support Agent

You can't be awake 24/7, but your AI agent can! It can answer common questions from your customers while you sleep.

1. Click on the **AI Agents** button.
2. Find the "Customer Support Agent" and click the switch to turn it **On**.
3. It will ask you a few simple questions about your business, like your return policy or opening hours. Answer them honestly.
4. Click **Activate**.

That's it! If a customer asks a question on your website, your AI agent will try to help them first.

[Learn more about what AI Agents can do](ai_agents.md)

---

## Need More Help?

If you ever feel lost, look for the little **"?"** button floating in the bottom corner of your screen. You can search for answers there, or chat with our automated help guide.
