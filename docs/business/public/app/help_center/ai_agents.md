# Using AI Support Agents

As a small business owner, you are doing everything: making the product, shipping it, and answering customer emails. Our AI Support Agents are designed to handle the repetitive questions so you can focus on growing your business.

## What is an AI Support Agent?

An AI Support Agent is a smart, automated assistant that lives on your website. When a customer has a question (like "What are your opening hours?" or "Do you ship to Canada?"), the agent types back an answer immediately.

It is like having a helpful employee who never sleeps and knows exactly what you tell them.

---

## How to Turn On Your AI Agent

1. Click on the **AI Agents** button on the left menu.
2. You will see the "Customer Support Agent" card. Click the switch to turn it **On**.
3. A screen will pop up asking you for "Business Rules." This is where you teach the agent what to say.

---

## Teaching Your Agent (Business Rules)

Your AI Agent only knows what you tell it. If you don't tell it your return policy, it won't know how to answer questions about returns.

In the **Business Rules** section, write down simple facts about your business. Talk exactly like you would talk to a new employee.

**Good examples to type in:**
*   "We are open Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM."
*   "We only ship within the United States."
*   "We offer full refunds within 30 days if the item is broken."
*   "Custom orders take 2 weeks to make."

**Bad examples (too complicated):**
*   "In accordance with section 4, paragraph B, international logistics are prohibited." *(Just say "We don't ship internationally.")*

---

## How Does the Agent Know When to Ask You for Help?

The AI Agent is smart enough to know when it is confused.

If a customer asks a very complicated question, or is very angry, the AI Agent will politely say:
*"I am just a virtual assistant and I want to make sure you get the right help. Let me send a message to the owner."*

The agent will then send you a notification, and you can take over the chat yourself.

---

## Reviewing What the Agent Said

You can always check to see if the AI Agent is doing a good job.

1. Go to the **AI Agents** tab.
2. Click on **Chat History**.
3. You will see a list of all the conversations the agent had with your customers.
4. If the agent gave a wrong answer, you can click on the message and correct it. The agent will learn from your correction and won't make that mistake again!
