# Account Settings and Billing

This section covers the boring (but important) stuff, like changing your password and paying for your OneHumanCorp subscription.

## How to Change Your Password

It is a good idea to change your password if you think someone else might know it.

1. Click on your profile picture in the top right corner.
2. Click **Account Settings**.
3. Under the "Security" section, click **Change Password**.
4. Type in your old password once, and your new password twice to confirm it.
5. Click **Update Password**.

---

## Understanding Your Subscription

We try to keep our pricing as simple as possible. You only pay for what you use.

1. Click on your profile picture.
2. Click **Billing**.

Here, you will see what plan you are currently on.
- **The Starter Plan** is free forever, but you pay a slightly higher fee when you sell something.
- **The Pro Plan** costs a flat monthly fee, but the fees for selling things are much lower. It also gives you access to advanced AI Agents.

If you are selling a lot of items every month, upgrading to the Pro Plan will usually save you money!

## How to Change or Cancel Your Plan

You are never locked into a contract. You can change your plan at any time.

1. Go to the **Billing** screen.
2. Click **Change Plan**.
3. Select the new plan you want.
4. If you are upgrading, you will be asked for a credit card to pay the monthly fee.

**To Cancel Completely:**
If you decide to close your business, we are sad to see you go! At the bottom of the Billing screen, click the small text that says "Close my account." Your store will be taken offline immediately.
