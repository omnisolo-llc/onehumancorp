# Managing Your Store

Your store is your online home. This guide explains how to add the things you sell (products or services) and how to organize them so customers can easily buy from you.

## How to Add a New Product or Service

Whether you sell a physical item (like a handmade mug) or a service (like a 1-hour plumbing consultation), you add them the same way.

1. Go to the **My Store** tab.
2. Click the big green button that says **+ Add New Item**.
3. **Name it:** Give your item a clear name (e.g., "Handmade Coffee Mug").
4. **Price it:** Type in how much it costs.
5. **Describe it:** Write a sentence or two explaining what the customer gets. Be honest and clear!
6. **Add a Photo:** This is very important! Click the camera icon to upload a picture. Clear, brightly lit photos help items sell faster.
7. Click **Save to Store**.

Your item is now live and people can buy it!

## Organizing Your Store with Categories

If you only sell three things, you don't need categories. But if you sell 20 things, it helps to group them so customers aren't confused.

1. In the **My Store** tab, click **Organize Categories**.
2. Click **Create New Category** (for example, "Summer Clothing" or "Consultation Services").
3. Now, when you add or edit an item, you can select that category from a dropdown menu.

---

## Tracking Your Inventory (Stock Levels)

If you make physical goods, you don't want someone to buy something you don't have. You can tell OHC to keep track of how many items you have left.

1. When editing an item, look for the section called **Inventory**.
2. Check the box that says **"Track how many I have."**
3. Type in the number of items you currently have sitting on your shelf.
4. Every time someone buys one, OHC will lower that number by one.
5. When the number reaches zero, OHC will automatically show the item as "Sold Out" so nobody gets angry.

*Tip: If you sell a service (like a haircut), do not check the inventory box. Leave it blank so people can keep booking!*

## Changing Your Store Design

You don't need to hire a designer to make your store look good. We built simple, clean layouts that work well on phones and computers.

1. In **My Store**, click **Design & Colors**.
2. Choose one of our pre-made themes. We recommend picking colors that match your logo.
3. Click **Preview** to see what it looks like to a customer.
4. Click **Publish** when you are happy with the look.
