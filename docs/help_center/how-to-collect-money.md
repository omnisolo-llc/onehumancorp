# How to Collect and Check Your Money

## Getting Paid Made Simple

As a business owner, getting paid should be the easy part. Here is how your AI team helps you send bills and track your cash.

### 1. Sending a Bill

When you finish work for a client, your AI assistant can automatically create a bill.
- It uses your set prices to calculate the total.
- It creates a clear request and sends it to your client.
- The bill includes a secure link for them to pay online.

### 2. Following Up

If a client forgets to pay, you don't need to send awkward reminder emails.
- Your system tracks who has paid and who hasn't.
- It will gently remind clients when their bill is overdue.
- It stops sending reminders the moment they pay or if you cancel the bill.

### 3. Checking Your Money

You need to know your money actually arrived.
- We connect directly to your payment account (like Stripe).
- When the payment system says "paid", we mark your bill as paid.
- We make sure the amount they paid matches the bill exactly.
- We double-check to make sure no one gets charged twice for the same thing.

### What To Do Next

To start getting paid faster, make sure you have connected your payment account in the "My Store" settings.
