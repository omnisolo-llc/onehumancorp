# Marketing & Growing Your Business

Having a great store is only half the battle. You also need to let people know you exist! We have built simple tools to help you reach more customers.

## Creating Discount Codes
Everyone loves a good sale.
1. Go to the **Marketing** tab.
2. Tap **"Create Discount"**.
3. Choose the type: A percentage off (like 15% off) or a fixed amount (like $5 off).
4. Give the code a name, like `SPRING24` or `WELCOME`.
5. You can share this code on your social media or in emails. When customers check out, they enter the code to get the deal.

## Sending Emails to Customers
When someone buys from you, they can choose to sign up for your emails. Over time, you will build a valuable list of fans.

1. In the Marketing tab, tap **"Send Email"**.
2. We have ready-made templates for things like "New Product Announcement" or "Holiday Sale".
3. Add a photo, type a short message, and tap Send.
4. The email will go to everyone who has agreed to receive updates from you.

**Remember:** Don't send emails too often, or people will get annoyed and unsubscribe. Once or twice a month is usually perfect.

## The Viral Referral System
Word of mouth is the best advertising. You can reward your customers for telling their friends about your store.
1. Go to **Marketing > Referrals** and turn it on.
2. When a customer buys something, they get a special link to share.
3. You decide the reward. For example: "If your friend buys something using your link, they get 10% off, and you get a $5 credit for your next purchase!"
4. The system handles all the tracking and credits automatically.

## Social Media Links
Make sure your store connects to your social media pages (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok).
- Go to **Settings > Social Media**.
- Paste the links to your profiles.
- Icons will automatically appear at the bottom of your store, so shoppers can easily follow you.

## Running Abandoned Cart Campaigns
Sometimes a shopper will add an item to their cart but leave without paying. This is called an "Abandoned Cart".
1. Go to **Marketing > Automations**.
2. Turn on the "Abandoned Cart Email".
3. We will automatically send a friendly email to the shopper 24 hours later reminding them about the item.
4. You can even include a small discount code in this email to encourage them to finish the purchase!

## Connecting with Influencers
If you want popular people on social media to promote your products, you can set up an Affiliate Program.
1. Go to **Marketing > Affiliates**.
2. Generate a special link for the influencer.
3. Every time someone buys through that link, the influencer gets a percentage of the sale.
4. We track all the clicks and sales for you.

## Using Analytics to Drive Marketing
Don't just guess what marketing is working. Look at the numbers!
- In your **Marketing** tab, tap on **Reports**.
- You can see exactly how much money each of your emails or discount codes generated.
- If a specific email resulted in a lot of sales, try sending something similar next month!

## Cross-Selling and Upselling
These are fancy terms for getting people to buy more things at once.
- **Cross-selling:** "You bought a camera, would you also like to buy this memory card?"
- **Upselling:** "You are buying the small coffee for $3, would you like to upgrade to the large for just $1 more?"

To set this up:
1. Go to a product's details page.
2. Scroll down to the **Related Products** section.
3. Select 2 or 3 items that go well with it.
4. These items will now be suggested to the customer right before they checkout!

## Local SEO (Search Engine Optimization)
If you have a physical store or only sell to people in your city, you want locals to find you on Google.
1. Make sure your business address and phone number are correct in **Settings**.
2. Write a clear description of your business on your homepage that includes your city name (e.g., "The best homemade cupcakes in Austin, Texas").
3. Ask your happy customers to leave reviews on your Google Business Profile, and put a link to your One Human Corp store there.

## Hosting a Flash Sale
A flash sale is a very short sale (like 24 hours) with a very big discount. It creates excitement and urgency.
1. Create a big discount code (like 40% off).
2. Go to **Store Design** and add a bright "Announcement Bar" to the very top of your site: "FLASH SALE: 40% off everything for the next 24 hours only!"
3. Send an email blast to your customers right when the sale starts, and another one 3 hours before it ends saying "Last chance!"
