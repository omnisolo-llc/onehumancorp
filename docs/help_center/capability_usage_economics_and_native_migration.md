# OmniSolo Business Capabilities

Welcome to OmniSolo! This guide explains how you can accomplish verified business work with our current product, distinguishing between what is supported today and what is coming soon.

## Business Setup
We currently provide onboarding state flows to help you get started. Verified owner-ready operating businesses are currently in development, but you can begin setting up your tenant profile and basic information immediately.

## Customer Proposals
OmniSolo supports creating and managing tenant and customer records, tracking deposits, and obtaining approvals.
*Note:* Currently, the intake process writes fixed scope independent of inquiries. Customizing scope dynamically based on inquiries is planned for a future update.

## Calendars & Tools
You can authenticate and send requests using Google Calendar and Google Workspace.
*Note:* Full secure credential lifecycles and advanced permissions are currently being finalized and require separate proof steps before broad usage.

## Payments
Our invoicing logic allows you to draft and manage invoices effectively.
*Note:* Complete Stripe and MercadoPago workflows with real provider sessions are available but not yet fully reconciled in the final accounting views.

## Agent Coordination
OmniSolo provides robust department and event coordination tools.
*Note:* Currently, feed items are simulated. We are actively working on replacing these with real execution, persistent approval flows, and concrete result evidence for your agents.

## Provider Runtime
The platform currently utilizes a scoped proxy with secret redaction to ensure your API keys and provider credentials are safe.
*Note:* We require reliable accounting and clear payer modes before full usage billing is enabled across all runtime providers.
