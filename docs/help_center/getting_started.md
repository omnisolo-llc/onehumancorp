# Getting Started

Welcome to the One Human Corp family! Let's get your business online and ready for sales in just a few simple steps.

## Step 1: Tell Us About Your Business
When you log in for the first time, you will see a quick setup screen. Just enter your business name and what you sell.

**Pro Tip:** Choose a name that is easy to remember! You can always change your business description later if you add new products.

## Step 2: Add Your First Item
A store needs something to sell! Click the big **"Add Item"** button on your dashboard.
- Take a clear photo of your item (or upload one).
- Give it a simple, catchy name.
- Set a price.
- Write a short description of what it is.

## Step 3: Connect Your Bank
To get paid, you need to tell us where to send your money. Go to **Settings > Payments** and securely connect your bank account. We use bank-level security so your information is safe. Once connected, any money you make will be deposited directly to you.

## Step 4: Share Your Store!
Your store is live! Now you need customers. Click the **"Share Store"** button at the top of your screen to get a link. You can text this link to friends, post it on social media, or email it to your contacts.

## Need Help?
Look for the little "?" icons next to things you don't understand. If you're still stuck, click the floating **Chat** button in the bottom corner of your screen to talk to our friendly AI helper at any time!

## Exploring Your Dashboard
Your Dashboard is the heart of your business. It is the first thing you see when you open the app. From here, you can see how much money you have made today, what tasks you need to do, and messages from your customers.

**Key areas of your Dashboard:**
- **Today's Sales:** A quick look at your daily earnings.
- **To-Do List:** Important reminders, like "Ship 3 orders" or "Reply to 1 customer."
- **Quick Actions:** Big buttons to quickly add an item, create a discount, or hire an AI Agent.

## Getting Comfortable on Mobile
We know you are always on the go. Our app is designed to work perfectly on your phone. You can manage your entire business while waiting in line at the grocery store.

- Use your thumb to easily tap through menus.
- Long-press (hold your finger down) on tricky buttons to read a short explanation of what they do.

## What is a "CUJ"?
You might hear us talk about a "Customer Journey" or "CUJ". This is just a fancy way of saying "the path a shopper takes to buy something from you." We have tested every step to make sure it is as smooth as possible, so your customers have a great experience and keep coming back.

## Next Steps
Now that you know the basics, try adding three more items to your store. Or, head over to the [My Store Guide](my_store.md) to learn how to organize your products into categories!

## Why One Human Corp?
We built One Human Corp specifically for people who are great at their craft but might not be computer experts. You focus on making great products, and we focus on the boring stuff: taxes, shipping calculations, and complicated website coding.

## Understanding the "Grandmother Test"
We have a strict rule for our app: it must be so simple that anyone's grandmother could figure it out in 30 seconds. If you ever find something confusing, please let us know! We probably failed the Grandmother Test and need to fix it.

## Connecting with Other Founders
Running a business can be lonely. We highly encourage you to join the One Human Corp Community Forum.
- It is a safe space to ask questions, get feedback on your products, and share your wins.
- You can find the link in your Settings menu.

## Step 5: Setting Up Taxes
Taxes can be confusing, but we make them easy. Depending on where you live and sell, you might need to collect sales tax.
- Go to **Settings > Taxes**.
- Tell us what country and state/province your business is located in.
- The system will automatically calculate the correct tax rate for your customers at checkout. We keep these rates updated automatically!

## Step 6: Test Your Store
Before you start sharing your link with the world, it is always a good idea to test everything yourself.
1. Open your store link in a new web browser.
2. Add a product to your cart.
3. Go to checkout.
4. Instead of entering your real credit card, go to your **Settings > Payments** and turn on **"Test Mode"**. We will give you a fake credit card number to use.
5. Finish the checkout and make sure the order appears in your Dashboard.
Don't forget to turn **"Test Mode"** back off when you are done!

## Common Mistakes to Avoid
- **Blurry photos:** Your photos are your storefront window. Make sure they are bright and clear!
- **Hidden shipping costs:** If shipping is expensive, people will abandon their carts. Try to offer a simple flat rate, or slightly increase the price of your item so you can offer "Free Shipping".
- **Complicated names:** Name your products exactly what they are. "Cozy Red Winter Scarf" is much better than "Item #42A-Red".

## Exploring Your Store Settings
There are a few important settings you should check before you officially open.
1. **Store Name & Description:** This is what appears in Google search results. Make it descriptive!
2. **Contact Email:** This is the email address your customers will use to contact you. Make sure it is an address you check regularly.
3. **Store Currency:** Make sure this is set to the currency of the country where you live and do business.

## Adding a Custom Domain (Optional)
When you sign up, we give you a free web address like `yourstore.onehumancorp.com`.
If you want to look more professional, you can connect your own domain, like `www.yourstore.com`.
- If you don't own one yet, you can buy one directly through our app in **Settings > Domains**.
- If you already own one from a site like GoDaddy or Namecheap, we have a simple wizard that will help you connect it in just a few clicks.
