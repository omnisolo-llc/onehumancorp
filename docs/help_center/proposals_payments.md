# How to Send Proposals and Collect Payments Securely

**Problem Statement:**
As a small business owner, it is critical to know that the proposals you send to your clients contain the exact details and prices you agreed on, and that payment links are secure.

**Current Capabilities:**
- **No More Placeholder Proposals:** OmniSolo ensures that the quote generated directly reflects your customer's request and your clear business rules. We check the math on all your line items and deposits. If a price is missing, the system marks it as `NEEDS_PRICING` for you to review. We leave out any items you do not select so they do not show up in the total.
- **Secure Checkout Links:** When you create an invoice, OmniSolo generates a real, secure session with your connected payment provider (like Stripe). It will not generate fake checkout links.
- **Drafts vs. Sent Reminders:** The system clearly shows the difference between drafting a reminder and actually sending it. We only save real drafts that come from your true account data.
- **Your Authority:** These actions run under your rules. The system respects your hard spending limits.

**How to Use This Feature:**
1. Open a Lead or Inquiry in the OmniSolo app.
2. Click **Generate Proposal**. Review the line items carefully. If any items are marked `NEEDS_PRICING`, fill in the correct amounts.
3. Approve the proposal to lock in the scope and price.
4. When ready, click **Create Invoice**. OmniSolo will securely connect to your payment provider to create a real checkout link.
5. The email or text draft will go to your outbox for a final check before sending.

**Cost & Expectations:**
You pay for the computer power and AI tools the team uses to generate and send these items. There are no hidden markup fees on your customer's invoice. Standard payment processor fees from your connected provider still apply.

**Exceptions and Recovery:**
If your payment provider disconnects or fails to create a link, the invoice status will stay as "Draft/Pending Provider". You can easily try making the link again later.
