# Setup, Connected Accounts, and Standing Authority

Welcome to OmniSolo! Getting your business up and running involves setting up your connected accounts and establishing the rules for your AI team.

## Setup
To set up your business, you need to configure your connected accounts and set your rules. This ensures that the AI team works safely on your behalf.

## Connected Accounts
You can link your own tools, like Google Workspace for your files and emails, and Stripe for payments. When you link these accounts, your AI team can use them to do work for you. Be sure to check your settings so your team has exactly the right access.

## Standing Authority
Think of standing authority as giving your team a rulebook. You decide what they can do on their own and what they need to ask you about first.
- You set rules for tasks like sending emails or taking payments.
- You set hard spending limits so the team never spends more than you want.
- You can change these rules or stop the team at any time.

## Evidence
You will always know what your AI team is doing. The app keeps a clear, lasting record of all their work. You can check the evidence feed to see the emails they sent, the payments they collected, and the exact steps they took.

## Cost
We want you to understand exactly what you pay for. You pay for the computer power and AI tools the team uses. You will see an estimated cost before a task starts, and a detailed bill when it finishes. If you pay your connected providers directly (like Stripe or Google), we will not charge you twice for those services. There are no hidden fees.

## Exceptions and Recovery
Sometimes things go wrong, like a payment link failing to send. If this happens, your AI team will stop and let you know. The app saves their progress and shows you exactly what went wrong. You can easily fix the issue and try again later, without starting over or paying twice.
