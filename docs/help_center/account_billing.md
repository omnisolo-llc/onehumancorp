# Account & Billing

This section covers how to manage your One Human Corp account settings, keep your information secure, and understand your billing.

## Managing Your Profile
You can update your personal information at any time.
1. Tap the circle with your initials in the top right corner.
2. Select **Account Settings**.
3. Here you can change your email address, update your password, or change your phone number.

## Keeping Your Account Secure
Your business is valuable, so we need to protect it. We highly recommend turning on **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)**.

What is 2FA? It just means that when you log in, we will text a short number code to your phone. You have to enter that code to get in. This means even if someone guesses your password, they can't access your store unless they also have your physical phone.

- Go to **Account Settings > Security** to turn this on.

## Adding Team Members
If you have employees or a business partner, you shouldn't share your password with them. Instead, give them their own login.
1. Go to **Settings > Team**.
2. Tap **"Invite Team Member"** and enter their email address.
3. You can choose what they are allowed to do. For example, you can let them see orders and add products, but stop them from seeing your bank information or issuing large refunds.

## Understanding Your Bill
If you are on a paid plan, or if you use AI Agents, you will receive a simple bill once a month.

- To see your current bill or past invoices, go to **Settings > Billing**.
- We will clearly show you:
  - Your monthly plan fee (if you have one).
  - Any small fees for using AI Agents.
  - A summary of credit card processing fees (though these are usually taken out before the money hits your bank).

## Canceling or Pausing Your Account
We hope you stay forever, but we understand things change.
- If you need to close your store, go to **Settings > Billing**, scroll to the bottom, and tap **"Close Store"**.
- We also offer a "Pause" option. This temporarily takes your store offline and stops your monthly billing, but saves all your products and settings so you can easily come back later.

## Exporting Your Data
Your data belongs to you. If you ever decide to leave, or if you just want to run your own custom reports in Excel, you can export everything.
1. Go to **Settings > Advanced > Data Export**.
2. You can choose to export your Products, your Orders, or your Customer List.
3. Click **"Generate Export"**.
4. We will email you a secure link to download a CSV file. For security reasons, this link expires after 24 hours.

## Deleting Your Account Permanently
If you want to completely erase your business from our servers:
1. First, you must cancel any active subscriptions in the **Billing** tab.
2. Go to **Settings > Advanced > Delete Account**.
3. You will be asked to type your password one more time to confirm.
4. **Warning:** This action cannot be undone. Once deleted, we cannot recover your store, your products, or your sales history.

## Getting Help with Billing Issues
If you see a charge on your credit card that you do not understand:
1. Do not immediately dispute it with your bank! That causes a lot of headaches for everyone.
2. Instead, go to your **Billing** tab and look at the detailed invoice.
3. If it still looks wrong, open the floating Help Chat and type "I need help with my bill."
4. Our human support team will look into it immediately and issue a refund if we made a mistake.

## Upgrading to a Higher Tier
As your business grows, you might need more features (like unlimited AI Agent tasks or advanced custom reports).
- Go to the **Billing** tab to see the available plans.
- Upgrading is instant. You will immediately get access to the new features.
- We will prorate your bill, meaning you only pay the difference for the remaining days in the month.

## Updating Your Business Name
If you rebrand your business, you can easily change your name in the system.
1. Go to **Settings > Business Profile**.
2. Change the "Business Name" field.
3. **Important:** This will immediately update the name on your storefront and on your customers' email receipts. It will *not* automatically change your custom domain name (web address). If you want a new web address, you must purchase a new one.

## Changing Your Store Currency
If you move to a new country and need your base prices to be in a different currency (e.g., changing from USD to EUR):
1. Go to **Settings > Advanced > Currency**.
2. Select your new home currency.
3. **Warning:** This does NOT automatically convert your prices. If a shirt was $20 USD, it will become €20 EUR. You must manually go through your products and update the prices to reflect the exchange rate if necessary.

## The One Human Corp App on Multiple Devices
You can log into your account on your phone, your tablet, and your laptop all at the same time.
- Changes you make on one device will instantly appear on the others.
- If you lose your phone, don't worry! Your business data is saved safely in the cloud. Just log into the website from a computer and change your password to secure your account.
- We recommend downloading the dedicated One Human Corp app from the App Store or Google Play for the fastest mobile experience.
