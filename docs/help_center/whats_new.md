# What's New

We are constantly improving the One Human Corp app to make running your business easier. Here are the latest updates!

## May 2026: The AI Agent Update
This month, we are giving you the ability to hire digital employees!
- **New Feature: AI Support Agents:** You can now go to the Agents tab and hire an AI to answer customer emails for you. It reads your policies and knows what is in stock.
- **Improvement:** The Sales Dashboard now loads twice as fast on older mobile phones.
- **Fix:** We fixed a bug where the "Save" button was sometimes hard to tap on smaller screens when editing a product.

## April 2026: The Marketing Update
- **New Feature: Discount Codes:** You can now create percentage-off or dollar-off discount codes to share on social media.
- **New Feature: Email Blasts:** Send a newsletter to all your past customers right from the app.
- **Improvement:** Product photos now upload much faster and look sharper.

## March 2026: The Spring Cleaning Update
- **Improvement:** We completely redesigned the "Settings" menu so things are easier to find.
- **Fix:** Resolved an issue where some banks took 3 days to connect instead of 1 day.
- **New Feature:** You can now turn on Two-Factor Authentication to make your account hacker-proof.

*(Want the highly technical details? You can read our developer-focused changelog on our main website).*

## February 2026: The "Speed" Update
- **Improvement:** We completely rewrote the code that loads your product list. It is now lightning fast, even if you have 10,000 items in your store.
- **Fix:** Fixed a glitch where the "Save" button would sometimes spin forever if you had a weak internet connection.
- **New Feature:** You can now see exactly how many people are looking at your store right now on the Dashboard.

## January 2026: Happy New Year!
- **New Feature:** The new Year-in-Review report is here. You can download a beautiful PDF showing all your stats for 2025 to give to your accountant.
- **Improvement:** The support chat bubble is now less intrusive and can be dragged around the screen so it doesn't block important buttons.
- **Fix:** Resolved an issue where some international addresses were being rejected by the shipping calculator.

## December 2025: The Holiday Rush Update
- **New Feature:** "Vacation Mode". You can now pause your store with one click if you get too busy during the holidays, without deleting any of your products.
- **Improvement:** We upgraded our servers to handle 5x more traffic, so your store will stay online even if one of your TikTok videos goes viral.
- **New Feature:** Added 5 new festive color themes for your storefront.

*Thank you for growing your business with One Human Corp! We are always listening to your feedback. If you have an idea for a feature, let us know in the Community Forum.*

## November 2025: The Video Update
- **New Feature:** You can now upload a video of your product! Instead of just photos, show your customers how the product works in real life. Video drives significantly higher sales.
- **Improvement:** We completely redesigned the "Orders" page so it is easier to see which orders need to be shipped today.
- **Fix:** Fixed a bug where discount codes with letters and numbers mixed together were sometimes not being recognized at checkout.

## October 2025: Getting Started
- **Welcome!** One Human Corp officially launches in beta. Our mission is to build the easiest e-commerce platform in the world for independent creators and small businesses. We are starting with basic storefronts, secure payments, and a simple mobile app. Stay tuned, we have so much more planned!
