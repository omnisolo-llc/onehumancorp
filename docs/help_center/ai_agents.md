# AI Agents: Your Digital Employees

Running a business alone is hard work. That is why we give you the ability to "hire" AI Agents. Think of them as incredibly fast, very polite digital employees who never need to sleep.

## What is an AI Agent?
An AI Agent is a computer program designed to do one specific job for your business. You talk to them just like you would talk to a real person.

## Hiring Your First Agent
1. Go to the **Agents** tab on your dashboard.
2. Tap the **"Hire Agent"** button.
3. Choose the type of job you need help with. The most popular choice for beginners is the "Customer Support Agent".

## The Customer Support Agent
If you hire a Customer Support Agent, it will read your store's policies, look at your products, and answer common questions from your customers automatically.

- **Example:** A customer asks, "Do you have the red shirt in size Medium?"
- **The Agent does:** It checks your inventory. If you have it, the Agent replies, "Yes we do! Here is the link to buy it."
- **If the Agent doesn't know:** It will politely tell the customer, "Let me check with the owner," and it will send a message to your phone so you can answer it yourself.

## The Marketing Agent
This agent helps you get more sales.
- You can tell it: "I want to run a 20% off sale this weekend for Valentine's Day."
- The Agent will create the discount code, write a nice email to your customers, and prepare a social media post. All you have to do is say "Approve" and it will send everything out.

## Managing Your Agents
Just like real employees, sometimes you need to tell your agents how you want things done.
- If you notice an agent said something you didn't quite like, tap on the conversation and click **"Correct Agent"**.
- You can tell it: "Next time, please be a little more formal." It will learn and remember for next time.

## How Much Do They Cost?
Agents cost a few cents every time they do a task (like answering an email). You can set a strict budget in **Settings > Agent Budget** so they never spend more than you allow. For example, you can say, "Do not spend more than $10 this month."

If they hit the budget limit, they simply pause working until you give them more budget, and the tasks will wait for you to do them manually.

## The Operations Agent
As your business grows, you might find yourself doing the same boring tasks over and over. The Operations Agent can help.
- **Example Task:** "Every time I get a new order, add the customer's email to a Google Sheet."
- **Example Task:** "If an order has a value over $100, send me a text message so I can pack it personally."
You can set up these rules in the **Agents > Operations** tab. The Agent will run them automatically in the background.

## Giving Your Agents Context
AI Agents are smart, but they don't know everything about your specific business unless you tell them.
1. Go to **Settings > Business Profile**.
2. Write a detailed description of your business, your values, and any special rules you have.
3. Your Agents will read this profile and use it to answer questions more accurately.

## Firing an Agent
If an Agent is not working out, you can "fire" them at any time.
1. Go to the **Agents** tab.
2. Tap on the specific Agent.
3. Scroll to the bottom and tap "Deactivate".
4. You will immediately stop being charged for that Agent's tasks.

## Troubleshooting Your Agents
Sometimes an Agent might do something unexpected. Here is how to fix it:
- **Agent is too chatty:** In the Agent's settings, change the "Tone" to "Brief and Professional".
- **Agent gives wrong prices:** Make sure your product catalog is up to date. The Agent only knows what is in the system.
- **Agent is paused:** Check your **Agent Budget** in the Settings menu. You might have hit your monthly limit.

## The Sales Agent
The Sales Agent is perfect for businesses that do custom work or B2B (business-to-business) sales.
- Sometimes a customer doesn't just want to click "Buy". They want to negotiate a price for a bulk order.
- You can activate the Sales Agent and let it negotiate on your behalf!
- You set the rules: "Never give more than a 15% discount, and always require a 50% deposit upfront." The AI will chat with the client, apply these rules, and write up the final invoice.

## Monitoring Your Agents
You should always keep an eye on what your Agents are doing, just like a real manager.
1. Go to the **Agents > History** tab.
2. Here you can read a full transcript of every conversation your Support Agent had with customers.
3. You can see every email the Marketing Agent sent out.
4. If you see an Agent made a mistake, you can use the "Correct Agent" button to teach it the right way for the future.

## Can Agents Talk to Each Other?
Yes! This is one of the most powerful features of One Human Corp.
- If the Support Agent receives a message saying "I love your product, can I buy 500 of them for my company?", the Support Agent knows it shouldn't handle a massive bulk order.
- It will automatically pass the conversation over to the Sales Agent, saying "I am transferring you to our bulk sales team."

## Upgrading Agent Intelligence
By default, all Agents use a fast, standard level of intelligence. If you have a very complex business (like selling specialized medical equipment or legal forms), you can upgrade your Agents to "Premium Intelligence" in the settings. This uses a more advanced AI model that is better at understanding highly technical questions, though it costs slightly more per task.

- **Example Task:** "Remind me to re-order packaging supplies when I drop below 500 sales for the month."
- **Example Task:** "Generate a daily summary report of the number of visitors and conversion rate, and text it to me."
These tasks let you focus on what really matters, without getting bogged down in administration.

## The Design Agent (Advanced)
Available on the Premium Intelligence tier, the Design Agent can help you keep your store looking fresh.
- Tell it: "Update my store to a spooky Halloween theme."
- The Agent will change your background colors, font colors, and even generate a temporary holiday logo for you to approve.
- You can always revert to your standard design with one click.
