# Cost, Exceptions, and Recovery

This guide explains how costs are managed, what happens when things go wrong, and how you stay in control of your AI team.

## Understanding Your Costs
Your AI team uses computing power and provider APIs (like OpenAI or Anthropic) to do work. OmniSolo separates these costs so you always know what you are paying for.

- **Included Usage:** Basic tasks are covered by your standard OmniSolo subscription.
- **Your Own Accounts (BYOK):** If you connect your own provider account (like your own OpenAI API key), you pay that provider directly. OmniSolo never charges you twice for this usage.
- **Spending Limits:** Your AI team respects your hard spending limits. It will never commit to a cost or purchase without checking your budget first.

## Exceptions and Failures
Sometimes, an external provider might be down, or a payment might fail. When this happens:
- **Nothing Breaks Permanently:** The AI team saves a draft of the work. It will securely pause the task until the provider comes back online.
- **No Duplicate Charges:** If a process gets stuck or fails halfway, you will not be charged twice for retrying.
- **You Get an Alert:** If the team needs your help to fix a connection, it will leave a clear message in your inbox.

## Recovery and Continuing Work
If your AI team encounters a problem, you can easily resume work:
- Go to your inbox and review the paused task.
- Click "Retry" or "Resume" when you are ready. The team picks up exactly where it left off.
- If you need to stop a task entirely, you can click "Cancel". This safely stops any further work or charges.
