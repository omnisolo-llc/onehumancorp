# Payments & Getting Paid

Getting paid should be the easiest part of running your business. Here is how it works.

## How Customers Pay You
When a customer buys something from your store, they can pay using major credit cards or Apple Pay/Google Pay. You do not need to do anything special to accept these—we handle all the complicated bank connections behind the scenes.

## Where Does My Money Go?
When a sale is made, the money briefly sits in your One Human Corp "Balance."

To move this money to your actual bank account:
1. Ensure your bank account is connected in **Settings > Bank Connection**.
2. We automatically send the money from your Balance to your bank account every night.
3. It usually takes 1-2 business days for the money to appear in your bank account, depending on your bank.

## Understanding Fees
We only make money when you make money. We charge a small, flat percentage on every sale to cover the costs of processing credit cards and running the secure platform.

You can always see exactly how much fee was taken out of a sale by tapping on the specific order in your **Sales History**. There are no hidden monthly fees just for having an account.

## How to Issue a Refund
Sometimes a customer changes their mind or an item arrives damaged. Giving a refund is simple.
1. Go to your **Orders** tab.
2. Find the order you need to refund and tap on it.
3. Scroll to the bottom and tap the red **"Issue Refund"** button.
4. You can choose to refund the whole amount, or just a partial amount.

The money will be returned to the customer's credit card in a few days.

## Taxes
Nobody likes taxes, but we try to make them less painful.
- If your state requires you to collect sales tax, you can turn this on in **Settings > Taxes**.
- Simply enter your local tax rate (for example, 7%), and we will automatically add it to the final price when customers check out.
- At the end of the year, we provide a simple report showing exactly how much tax you collected, which you can give to your accountant.

## Dealing with Disputes (Chargebacks)
Very rarely, a customer might tell their credit card company they didn't make a purchase, or that they never received the item. This is called a "dispute."
- If this happens, we will email you immediately.
- We will ask you to provide proof that you shipped the item (like a tracking number).
- Our team will handle the complicated communication with the credit card company for you.

## Accepting International Payments
If you want to sell to people in other countries, we make it easy.
1. Go to **Settings > International**.
2. Turn on "Accept International Orders".
3. We will automatically translate the prices into the customer's local currency (like Euros or Pounds).
4. The money will still arrive in your bank account in your home currency. We handle all the complicated exchange rates automatically.

## Managing Subscriptions
Do you sell a product that people need to buy every month? (Like coffee beans or a digital magazine).
1. When adding a product, select the "Subscription" option.
2. Choose how often the customer is billed (e.g., Weekly, Monthly).
3. The customer's credit card will be charged automatically on that schedule.
4. You can see all your active subscribers in the **Orders > Subscriptions** tab.

## Tipping
If you provide a service (like consulting or a local delivery), you can allow customers to leave a tip at checkout.
- Go to **Settings > Checkout**.
- Turn on "Allow Tipping".
- You can set suggested tip amounts (like 10%, 15%, 20%).

## Fraud Protection
We work hard to protect you from scammers.
- Our system automatically checks every credit card transaction for signs of fraud.
- If an order looks highly suspicious (like someone trying to use 10 different credit cards in a row), we will automatically block the transaction.
- If an order looks slightly suspicious, we will flag it in your Dashboard and suggest you contact the customer to verify their identity before shipping the item.

## Selling Gift Cards
Gift cards are a great way to boost sales, especially around the holidays.
1. Go to **Products > Gift Cards**.
2. Turn on the Gift Card feature.
3. Customers can now buy digital gift cards in amounts like $25, $50, or $100.
4. When they buy one, an email with a special code is sent to the recipient. They can use this code at checkout just like a discount code.

## Handling Partial Payments
Currently, One Human Corp does not support partial payments or "layaway" natively for physical goods. However, if you are selling high-ticket items (like custom furniture), you can use our Invoicing tool.
1. Go to **Orders > Invoices**.
2. Create a new invoice and send it to the customer's email.
3. You can request a 50% deposit upfront, and send a second invoice for the remaining balance before you ship.

## What to do if a payment fails
Sometimes a customer's card will be declined. This usually means they typed the number wrong or they don't have enough funds.
- We will automatically show them an error message and ask them to try a different card.
- If you see a lot of failed payments in your logs, don't worry. This is completely normal for e-commerce and you are not charged any fees for declined transactions.
