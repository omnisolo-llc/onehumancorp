# My Store

This guide will help you manage your virtual storefront. Think of this section as arranging the shelves in a physical shop.

## Adding and Editing Products
To add a new product:
1. Tap the **"Store"** icon at the bottom of your screen.
2. Tap the big **"Add New Product"** button.
3. Fill in the details (photo, name, price).

**Oops, made a mistake?** No problem. Just tap on any product in your list and tap **"Edit"**. You can change the price, update the photo, or fix a typo.

## Creating Categories
If you sell different types of things (like shirts and hats), you should put them in categories. This makes it much easier for your customers to find what they want.

1. Go to your Store tab.
2. Tap **"Categories"** at the top.
3. Tap **"Create Category"** and give it a name (like "Winter Hats").
4. Choose which products belong in this category.

## Inventory Management
Inventory just means "how many of this item do I have left to sell?" We help you keep track so you don't accidentally sell something you don't have.

- When you add a product, tell us how many you have in the **"Quantity in Stock"** box.
- Every time someone buys one, we will automatically subtract it.
- When you reach zero, the item will show as "Sold Out" on your store so customers know they can't buy it right now.

## Customizing Your Store's Look
You can make your store match your brand's style.
1. Go to **Settings > Store Design**.
2. Pick your favorite colors from our pre-selected, professional color palettes.
3. Upload a logo if you have one. If you don't, we will display your business name in a nice, clean font.

## Handling Shipping Options
If you are shipping physical goods to people, you need to set up your shipping options.
1. Go to **Settings > Shipping**.
2. You can choose to offer "Flat Rate" shipping (e.g., $5 for every order) or "Free Shipping" (customers love this!).
3. If you only sell locally, you can turn on the "Local Pickup" option.

## Understanding Store Policies
It is a good idea to tell your customers about your rules for returns and shipping. We have written standard, friendly policies for you.
- Go to **Settings > Legal & Policies**.
- Read through the default policies. You can use them exactly as they are, or tap to edit them if you have special rules (like "No returns on custom items").

## Store Design Philosophy
At One Human Corp, we believe your store should reflect your unique personality while remaining highly usable.
1. **Glassmorphism everywhere:** The UI automatically uses a subtle blur effect on backgrounds.
2. **Typography matters:** We use Outfit for big headings and Inter for easy reading.
3. **Mobile-First:** 80% of your shoppers will buy from their phones. Make sure your images look good on a small screen!

## Analyzing Store Performance
Want to know what is selling best?
1. Go to your **Store** tab and tap **Analytics**.
2. Here you can see a breakdown of your most popular items.
3. You can also see what time of day people are buying the most, so you know when to run your sales.

## Organizing Collections
If you have a lot of items, grouping them into Collections helps shoppers.
- A Collection is just a group of items (e.g., "Summer Collection 2026").
- You can feature a specific Collection on your homepage to drive traffic.
- When you add a new product, you can instantly assign it to multiple collections.

## Managing Customer Reviews
Positive reviews are the best way to get new customers to trust you.
1. When a customer receives their item, we automatically send them an email asking for a review.
2. When they leave a review, it will show up on your product page.
3. If you get a bad review, don't panic! You can reply to it publicly. A polite, helpful reply to a bad review often looks better to future customers than a perfect 5-star rating with no replies.

## Adding a "Contact Us" Page
Customers need to know how to reach you if they have a problem.
1. Go to **Store Design > Pages**.
2. Click **Add New Page**.
3. Name it "Contact Us".
4. Add your business email address and phone number (if you want people to call you).
5. This page will automatically appear in the footer at the bottom of your store.

## Creating a Logo
Don't have a logo yet? No problem!
- In the **Store Design** section, you can use our built-in text logo generator.
- Pick a font that matches your style (elegant, playful, modern).
- Choose a color.
- We will generate a clean, professional logo for you instantly. You can always upload a custom picture later when you have one designed.

## Seasonal Updates
Keep your store looking fresh!
- In December, try adding a "Holiday Gifts" category to the top of your homepage.
- In spring, swap out your dark background colors for something lighter.
- Small changes make your store look active and cared for.
