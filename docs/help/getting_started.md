# Getting Started

Welcome to OneHumanCorp! Setting up your business takes less than 10 minutes.

## 1. Choose Your Business Type
Tell us what you do. Do you sell physical products (like custom cakes), digital items (like online courses), or services (like home repair)? Pick the option that fits you best.

## 2. Name Your Business
Give your store a name. Don't worry, you can change this later!

## 3. Meet Your AI Team
As soon as you sign up, your AI agents start working for you in the background. They will help you build your website, reply to customers, and track your money.

## 4. Launch Your Website
Click the "Publish" button to make your store live. You can share your link on Instagram, Facebook, or anywhere else.

Need a tour? Click the question mark "?" button on any page for a quick guide.
