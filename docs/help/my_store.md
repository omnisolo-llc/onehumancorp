# My Store

This is where you manage everything you sell.

## Adding Products or Services
1. Go to the "My Store" tab on your screen.
2. Tap the "Add New Item" button.
3. Type the name, add a photo, and set the price.
4. Tap "Save".

## Managing Bookings
If you offer services, like tutoring or home repair, you can turn on the calendar.
- Customers can pick a date and time that works for them.
- You can block out days when you are busy or on vacation.

## Tracking Inventory
If you sell physical items, we can keep track of how many you have left. When you sell out, your store will automatically show the item as "Sold Out" so you don't get orders you can't fill.
