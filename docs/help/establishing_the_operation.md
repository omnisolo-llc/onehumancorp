# Establishing the Business Operation

Welcome to OmniSolo OneHumanCorp. This guide will walk you through setting up your business profile, understanding supported regions, securely connecting external accounts, publishing your first offer, and establishing the standing authority for your AI team.

## Business Profile
Your business profile is the core identity of your operation.

1. Navigate to **Settings > Business Profile**.
2. Enter your **Business Name**. This is how your business will appear to customers.
3. Select your **Industry**. This helps tailor the AI's understanding of your operations.
4. Provide your **Contact Info**, including email and support phone number.

## Supported Regions
We support businesses operating in various regions. Ensure you review the checklist of supported regions and compliance requirements in the **Settings > Compliance** section. This ensures your operation adheres to local regulations regarding data privacy and commerce.

## Connected Accounts
To fully empower your AI team, you need to securely connect your external tools.

1. Go to **Settings > Integrations**.
2. **Payment Processing (e.g., Stripe)**: Click "Connect" next to your preferred payment processor and follow the secure OAuth flow. We do not store your raw passwords.
3. **Workspace (e.g., Google Workspace)**: Connect your workspace to allow the AI to read free/busy times and manage documents securely.

## Offer Publishing
Publishing an offer makes your services available to clients.

1. Go to the **Services / Offers** section in your dashboard.
2. Click **Create New Offer**.
3. Fill out the offer details, including title, description, and pricing (fixed or variable).
4. Review and click **Publish**. Your offer is now live.

## Standing Authority
Standing authority determines what your AI team can do autonomously without explicitly asking you for approval every single time.

1. Navigate to **Settings > AI Team & Authority**.
2. Set your **Standing Policies**: Determine whether the AI can handle refunds under a certain amount automatically, or if it should escalate to you.
3. Establish **Hard Spend Reservations**: Set a strict monthly budget cap on AI inference and API usage. The system will halt operations before exceeding this cap, requiring your explicit approval to increase it.
4. **Revocation**: You can pause or revoke the AI's authority at any time from the dashboard if you need to take manual control.
