# Marketing

Getting customers to find you is important. Here is how OneHumanCorp helps you get noticed.

## Getting Found on Google
We automatically set up your website so people can find you when they search on Google. You don't have to do any confusing tech work.

## Sharing on Social Media
You can easily link your Instagram, Facebook, or TikTok accounts. Your AI helper can even suggest what pictures to post and what to write in the caption.

## Sending Emails to Customers
When someone buys from you, we can safely save their email. Later, you can send them a message about a new product or a special sale.

## The "Link in Bio"
If you use social media, you can create a simple, beautiful link to put in your profile. When people tap it, they see all your products, booking links, and contact info in one place.
