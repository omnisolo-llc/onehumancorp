# Account & Billing

Manage your personal details and your OneHumanCorp subscription here.

## Your Profile
You can change the email address and phone number you use to log in. Just tap "Edit Profile" in your account settings.

## Choosing a Plan
OneHumanCorp has a free plan that gives you everything you need to start. If your business grows and you need more features, you can upgrade to a paid plan.

## Viewing Your Bills
If you are on a paid plan, you can see all your past bills by tapping "Billing History". You can also update the credit card you use to pay for your plan.

## Getting Help
If you need help with your account, tap the chat button to talk to our support team.
