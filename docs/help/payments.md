# Payments

Getting paid is easy and secure with OneHumanCorp.

## Accepting Online Payments
Customers can pay you using their credit card, Apple Pay, or Google Pay. The money goes safely into your OneHumanCorp account.

## Taking Deposits
Do you do custom work, like big cake orders or home repairs? You can ask customers to pay a deposit upfront before you start the work.

## In-Person Payments
If you sell things face-to-face, you can use your phone to accept payments. Customers just tap their card on your phone to pay.

## Getting Your Money
We will automatically send your earnings to your bank account. You can set this up to happen every day or every week. To check your balance, look at the "Finance" tab on your dashboard.
