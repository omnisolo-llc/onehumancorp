# AI Agents

Think of your AI Agents as your own personal team of helpers. You don't need to know anything about computers to use them!

## Customer Support Agent
This agent helps you answer messages. While you sleep or work, it can reply to simple questions like "What time do you open?" or "Do you have gluten-free options?".

## Website Designer
You don't need a web designer. Your AI designer will create a beautiful website for you. You just pick the colors and upload photos.

## The Accountant
Your AI accountant keeps track of all your money. Every week, it will send you a simple text message telling you how much you earned and which item sold the most.

## Getting Advice
Your AI Advisor watches how your business is doing and gives you tips. For example, it might say, "Your blue shirts are selling fast, you should order more!" or "It's almost Valentine's Day, let's run a special sale."
