# One Human Corp - User Guide

## Introduction
Welcome to One Human Corp (OHC). This guide explains how to use the web application to manage your AI agents, billing, business plans, team features, and more.

## Dashboard
The main dashboard gives you an overview of your AI agent activity, metrics, and required actions.

## Plans & Billing
- **My Plan**: View your active subscription, usage metrics, and remaining balances. Access the billing section to upgrade or change your plan.
- **Cost Dashboard**: Transparency dashboard showing LLM usage, storage costs, and transaction fees.

## Settings
Adjust your account settings, integrations, and preferences.

## Advanced Features
Most advanced features are hidden to maintain simplicity, but can be accessed via the settings toggle or contextual menus.
