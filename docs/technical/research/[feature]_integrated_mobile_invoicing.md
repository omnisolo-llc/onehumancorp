# [feature] Integrated Mobile Invoicing

**Problem Statement:** Solopreneurs need a quick way to invoice clients from their phone immediately after a job.

**Research Report:** Often requires a separate app (like QuickBooks). Needs to be integrated into the core platform to solve the "Scattered Management" pain point.

**Design Doc:**
- **Entities:** Invoice, Line Item, Customer, Payment Status.
- **UX:** 3-tap invoice creation from the mobile dashboard.
- **Mobile Flow (375px):**
  1. Tap "New Invoice"
  2. Select Customer from CRM
  3. Tap "Send".

**Implementation Prompt:** Build a mobile-optimized invoicing flow connected to the CRM and payment gateway.

**Priority:** P1

**Estimated Scope:** Medium
