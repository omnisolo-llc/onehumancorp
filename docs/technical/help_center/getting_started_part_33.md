# Help Center: Getting Started

Welcome to One Human Corp! This guide will help you set up your business quickly and easily.

## 1. Set Up Your Store
Your store is where your customers will find you. To get started:
- Go to the **Business Setup** page from the main menu.
- Fill in your business name and details.
- Choose a design that fits your brand.

## 2. Accept Your First Payment
Getting paid is simple:
- Connect your bank account in the **Payments** section.
- You can accept credit cards and Apple Pay immediately.
- We handle the secure processing.

## 3. Activate Your AI Support Agent
Don't have time to answer every customer question? Let your AI agent help:
- Go to the **Agents** page.
- Click **Hire Agent** and select the Support Agent.
- Your agent will start answering customer questions using your help center content.

## Need More Help?
Click the "?" button anywhere in the app, or use the floating chat button to ask our Support Agent directly.
