# Critical User Journeys (CUJ) - Miser

## 1. The "Plain Language" Bill
**Actor**: Maya (Small Business Owner)
**Scenario**: Maya wants to know why her bill was $42 last month.
**Journey**:
1. Maya clicks on "My Plan" in the OHC app.
2. She sees a clear breakdown: "AI Actions", "Storage", "Processing Fees".
3. Instead of "0.42M Tokens", she sees "Ambassador AI: $2.10 (350 customer chats)".
4. She sees a recommendation: "Switch to ACH for orders over $50 to save $8/mo".

## 2. Automatic Storage Optimization
**Actor**: Carlos (Tradesperson)
**Scenario**: Carlos uploads a high-res photo of his work.
**Journey**:
1. Carlos uploads a 5MB JPEG.
2. OHC automatically converts it to a 200KB WebP.
3. Carlos's storage quota remains healthy, and his customers see faster load times.
