# End-to-End Business Journey CUJ

This document describes the critical user journeys (CUJs) for the End-to-End Business Journey Design feature.

## Persona 1: Maya (The Home Baker)
1. Maya opens the app and enters "I sell custom vegan cakes in Brooklyn".
2. The AI generates a catalog, site, and basic policies.
3. Maya connects Stripe to accept payments (Activation).
4. Maya shares her link to her Instagram Bio.
5. A customer DMs Maya "Vegan options?".
6. AI Auto-replies with her booking link.
7. Customer pays a deposit.
8. Maya receives a "New Order!" notification.

## Persona 2: Carlos (The Freelance Handyman)
1. Carlos enters "I do plumbing and home repairs".
2. The AI generates a service menu & booking calendar.
3. Carlos sets his availability (Retention trigger).
4. A client requests a quote for a leaky faucet.
5. The AI generates a quote based on standard pricing.
6. Client accepts & books a time slot (Activation).
7. Carlos receives a booking confirmed notification.

## Persona 3: Priya (The Boutique Owner)
1. Priya enters "I run a clothing boutique".
2. The AI generates an inventory template (Size/Color).
3. Priya adds products (Activation).
4. A customer buys an item in-store via Tap-to-Pay.
5. The POS processes the payment and syncs inventory across physical/online.
6. AI sends Priya a weekly summary "Red dresses sold out" (Retention).
7. OHC suggests a referral program to friends (Referral).

## Persona 4: Leo (The Music Tutor)
1. Leo enters "I teach guitar online".
2. The AI generates subscription packages & Zoom sync.
3. Leo shares his link on TikTok.
4. A student subscribes to 4 lessons/month (Activation).
5. OHC confirms the subscription is active.
6. Student misses 2 weeks, AI asks "Ready for your next lesson?" (Retention).
7. Student books a lesson.

## Persona 5: Fatima (The Food Cart Operator)
1. Fatima enters "Halal food cart menu".
2. The AI generates a photo menu with a pickup flow (Arabic UI if configured).
3. Fatima sets "Chicken over rice" to active (Activation).
4. A customer pre-orders & pays.
5. Fatima receives an SMS/Push Notification "Order #14".
6. Fatima marks the order fulfilled.
7. AI provides a daily summary report (Retention).
