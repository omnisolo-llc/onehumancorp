# OmniSolo brand identity

**Empowering your solo success.**

OmniSolo expresses one human coordinating an AI-powered business. The selected logo is **A — Human first**, approved on September 15, 2026. Its recognizable human silhouette is surrounded by an O-shaped orbit and three agent dots.

## The name

**Omni** expresses the breadth of connected work: planning, execution, tools, knowledge, and business operations brought together around an owner. **Solo** expresses the single human at the center, supported by a coordinated agent team.

Write the brand as **OmniSolo**, with capital O and S and no space. **OmniSolo OneHumanCorp** is the business operations product. The selected symbol expresses Solo through one person; it does not use a literal numeral 1.

## The symbol

| Element | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Central head and shoulders | The human owner: the source of intent, judgment, and direction. |
| Surrounding O-shaped orbit | Omni: connected possibilities and coordinated work around that person. |
| Three satellite dots | The AI agent team supporting the owner, expressed through Plan, Act, and Learn. |
| Single central figure | Solo: one human directing the operation. |

The person stays visually distinct from the smaller agent dots. The orbit connects the composition while keeping the human at its center.

## Why three dots?

The dots represent a team of AI agents. The brand story describes their work through three complementary ideas:

- **Plan:** research, reason, and organize work.
- **Act:** carry out tasks through tools and integrations.
- **Learn:** retain context and use experience to inform future work.

These are symbolic roles, not exactly three agents, named services, departments, execution stages, or a fixed mapping to the software architecture. They do not promise that every task follows this sequence, that models retrain themselves, or that capabilities are available in every deployment.

All three dots have the same visual treatment. No individual position is assigned permanently to Plan, Act, or Learn.

## The implied triangle

The three dots are spaced approximately 120 degrees apart around the orbit. Connecting their centers would form an equilateral triangle. This spacing was chosen for balance; there is no drawn triangle and no separate triangle symbolism. The intended reading is **one human within an O-shaped network of agents**.

## Color and form

The app tile uses a blue gradient from `#087BFF` to `#0049D8`, with a white human and orbit and mint agent dots (`#93F5E5`). The transparent color mark uses interface blue (`#0066FF`) and darker teal (`#008575`) for light backgrounds. A monochrome mark preserves the same geometry without depending on color.

Keep the rounded geometry, centered person, three dots, and existing proportions. Do not add triangle edges, replace the person with a robot, or assign departments to dots. Keep the existing viewBox padding and scale uniformly. Prefer 32 px or larger; inspect the 16 px rendering when using it as a favicon.

## Approved copy

**Primary slogan:** Empowering your solo success.

**Supporting brand line:** One human. A whole company.

Use the primary slogan in the homepage headline, footer, and logo lockup. The supporting line explains the human-and-agents concept; it does not replace the established slogan.

**Short explanation:** One human at the center, an entire AI-powered operation around them — OmniSolo.

**Full explanation:** The OmniSolo symbol places one human inside an O-shaped orbit. The human represents the solo owner; the orbit represents the breadth of connected work around them. Three dots represent a coordinated AI agent team through the ideas of Plan, Act, and Learn: organizing work, taking action, and retaining context to inform what comes next.
