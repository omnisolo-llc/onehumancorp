## Strategic Reasonings

### Why Buffer?
While Meta has its own suite, it is built for data-hungry advertisers. Buffer is built for time-poor owners. It's the only choice that aligns with our "Anti-Distraction" UX goal.

### Why Brevo?
Mailchimp charges you just for "owning" a customer list. Brevo charges you for "talking" to them. This is much fairer for a growing small business.

### Why TextMagic?
Twilio is a tool for developers. TextMagic is a tool for Grandmothers. You can send your first text in 2 minutes without knowing what a "Webhook" is.
