# Title: Integrate Calendly for Calendar & Scheduling

## Problem Statement
Back-and-forth emails to find a meeting time waste hours each week. Double-bookings cause customer frustration.

## Research Report
**Advantages:** Industry standard, robust timezone handling, minimal setup.
**Risks:** Users might have complex existing calendar setups.
**Pricing:** $12/month Professional tier.
**Cloud/Standalone Support:** Cloud and Standalone supported.

## Design Doc
A 'Booking' page linked directly from the OHC profile, with a simple date/time picker that syncs silently with their personal calendar.

## Implementation Prompt
Implement the integration with Calendly such that the business owner experiences A 'Booking' page linked directly from the OHC profile, with a simple date/time picker that syncs silently with their personal calendar.. Do not focus on technical intricacies but on seamless user experience. Ensure all acceptance criteria related to ease-of-use and reliability are met.

P0

## Estimated Scope
Large
