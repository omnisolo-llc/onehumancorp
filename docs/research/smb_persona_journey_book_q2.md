# OHC SMB Persona Journey Book (Q2 2024-2025)

## Introduction
One Human Corp (OHC) is built for humans. This book documents the experience of our personas as they use our newly researched tool integrations.

---

## Persona 1: Fatima (The High-Speed Food Operator)
**Business**: "Fatima's Flavors" - A food cart in Lagos, Nigeria.
**Primary Tools**: Flutterwave, TextMagic, AfterShip.

### The Problem: The "Cash and Chaos" Trap
Before OHC, Fatima's cart was a mess of cash and shouting. She often lost track of who had paid.

### The OHC Transformation
- **Morning**: Fatima sees pre-orders already paid for via **Flutterwave**.
- **Rush**: A customer scans a QR code. Fatima gets a confirmation "Ding!" immediately.
- **The "Ready" Moment**: Fatima taps a button. **TextMagic** sends an SMS to the customer.
- **Growth**: Fatima uses **AfterShip** to offer delivery, sending branded tracking links to offices.

---

## Persona 2: Maya (The Creative Home Baker)
**Business**: "Maya's Vegan Treats" - Home-based bakery in London.
**Primary Tools**: Buffer, Brevo, AfterShip.

### The Problem: The "Accidental Marketer"
Maya loves baking but hates managing social media comments and newsletters.

### The OHC Transformation
- **Marketing Morning**: Maya spends 15 minutes using **Buffer** to schedule her week.
- **Engagement**: Maya replies to Instagram comments directly from one OHC list.
- **The Newsletter**: Maya uses **Brevo** to send WhatsApp specials to her top customers.
- **Delivery**: **AfterShip** sends her customers beautiful tracking pages.

## Persona 3: Leo (The Professional Music Tutor)
**Business**: "Leo's Guitar Studio"
**Primary Tools**: Outlook, Teams, Brevo.

### The Problem: The "Sticky Note" Schedule
Leo was constantly forgetting appointments or double-booking himself.

### The OHC Transformation
- **The Sync**: Leo connects his **Outlook** calendar. OHC now knows his whole life.
- **Virtual Classroom**: A student books a lesson. OHC creates a **Teams** link automatically.
- **Professionalism**: Leo looks like a high-end conservatory. His students pay upfront and get professional invites.
