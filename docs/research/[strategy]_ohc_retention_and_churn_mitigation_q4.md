# OHC Retention & Churn Mitigation Strategy (Q4 2024)

## 1. The Real Cost of Churn in SMB SaaS

The SMB SaaS market is notorious for high churn rates. The traditional trajectory for platforms like Wix or Squarespace is:
1.  **Month 1 (The Honeymoon):** The user builds a site. Excitement is high.
2.  **Month 2 (The Trough of Sorrow):** The user realizes a website doesn't automatically generate traffic. They struggle to find time to write blog posts or run ads.
3.  **Month 3 (The Cancellation):** Frustrated by the lack of ROI and the ongoing monthly fee ("Cost Creep"), the user churns.

OHC's survival depends on flattening this curve. We must shift the user's perception of OHC from a "sunk cost" (like web hosting) to a "revenue generator" (like a top-performing employee).

## 2. Pillar 1: The First 30 Days (Proving Value)

The first 30 days are critical. OHC must prove its value aggressively before the first billing cycle completes.

### 2.1 The "Quick Win" Onboarding
As detailed in our Market Penetration strategy, the onboarding process must deliver instant value. Setting up the Omnichannel Assistant immediately demonstrates time saved.

### 2.2 Proactive Milestones
The AI teammates should proactively guide the user through "Quick Wins."
*   *Example:* On Day 3, The Generative Promoter says: "Your site is live! Let's get your first customer. I've drafted a Facebook post offering a 10% discount to your local network. Tap to approve and post."
*   *Example:* On Day 7, The Business Advisor says: "You haven't added any photos of your recent work. Want to take a picture of your latest carpentry project now so I can update your portfolio?"

By forcing engagement through low-friction, high-reward tasks, OHC creates a habit of success.

## 3. Pillar 2: The "Sticky" Operational Hub

Churn is dramatically reduced when a platform becomes integral to daily operations, not just a static billboard.

### 3.1 Owning the Transaction
If OHC processes a business's payments (OHC Payments), it becomes significantly harder to leave. The pain of migrating payment gateways, transferring customer payment data, and setting up new banking integrations is a massive deterrent to churn.

### 3.2 The Single Source of Truth
OHC must own the CRM and the inventory. If all customer history, past invoices, and upcoming bookings are housed natively within OHC, the platform becomes the "brain" of the business. Moving away means losing that operational history.

## 4. Pillar 3: The Business Advisor (Preventing the Silent Fade)

Often, users churn simply because they forget they are paying for a service they aren't actively managing.

### The Antidote: The Daily Briefing
The Business Advisor agent is OHC's most powerful anti-churn weapon. By providing a daily or weekly plain-language briefing ("Good morning Carlos! You have 3 bookings today, and your plumbing ad brought in 2 new leads yesterday"), OHC constantly reminds the user of its value, even if the user hasn't actively logged in to make changes.

## 5. Conclusion

Mitigating churn requires moving beyond "software as a service" and towards "software as a partner." By delivering immediate wins, deeply integrating into daily operations and transactions, and proactively communicating success, OHC can achieve best-in-class retention rates in the volatile SMB sector.
