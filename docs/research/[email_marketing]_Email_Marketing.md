# Email Marketing Integration

## Title
Integrate Mailchimp for Email Marketing

## Problem Statement
Business owners struggle to keep their customer lists synchronized between their store and their marketing tools, leading to missed opportunities to drive repeat sales.

## Research Report
**Tool Evaluated:** Mailchimp
**Pricing:** Free tier; $13/mo premium
**Cloud/Standalone Support:** Cloud: Yes. Standalone: Yes (API driven).

**Findings:**
Mailchimp is widely recognized by small businesses. It offers excellent drag-and-drop template builders and robust list management. It handles spam compliance automatically. Pricing scales with contacts (Free up to 500 contacts, then starts at $13/mo).

## Design Doc
A 'Marketing' tab in OHC where users connect Mailchimp. Customer data (opt-ins, purchases) automatically syncs from OHC to Mailchimp audiences. Business owners can view basic campaign stats (open rates, clicks) directly in OHC.

## Implementation Prompt
Build an automatic sync feature between OHC customer contacts and a Mailchimp audience. Provide a simple dashboard view showing recent campaign performance.
