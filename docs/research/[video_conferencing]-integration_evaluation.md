# Issue Brief: Video Conferencing Tools

### Title: Integrate Zoom
**Problem Statement:** Non-technical small business owners need a reliable solution in the video conferencing space. Currently, they spend hours manually performing tasks that Zoom could automate, leading to lost revenue and burnout.

**Research Report:** Zoom is evaluated as a candidate for integration. It offers 'Industry standard video calls.'.
- **Pricing Estimate:** Free tier, $15/month.
- **Cloud/Standalone Compatibility:** Works in both environments via standard authentication flows.
- **Advantages:** Everyone has it installed.
- **Risks:** Requires app download usually.

**Design Doc:**
- **Trigger:** A relevant business event occurs in OHC.
- **Action:** OHC integrates seamlessly with Zoom.
- **User Experience:** The owner simply clicks 'Connect Zoom' and authenticates without encountering complex configurations.

**Implementation Prompt:** Provide a seamless, plain-language integration for Zoom that allows non-technical users to utilize its video conferencing features natively within OHC.

**Priority:** P2
**Estimated Scope:** Medium
