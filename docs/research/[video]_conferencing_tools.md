# Video Conferencing Tools

**Title**: Integrate Automated Video Meeting Links (Zoom, Whereby)

**Problem Statement**:
Users who offer online services (like Leo the Music Tutor) currently have to manually create a Zoom link and email it to the student after they book a slot. This manual step often leads to errors or lost links.

**Research Report**:
Evaluated Zoom API and Whereby Embedded.
- **Zoom**: The ubiquitous standard.
  - *Ease of Use*: Everyone has it. The OAuth flow is well understood.
  - *Pricing*: Requires the business owner to have their own Zoom account (free or paid).
  - *Reputation*: Universally recognized.
- **Whereby Embedded**: Browser-based video calls.
  - *Ease of Use*: No app download required for the customer. We can embed the video call directly into the OHC web portal.
  - *Pricing*: OHC pays for minutes; the business owner doesn't need a separate account.
- **Recommendation**: Integrate Zoom via OAuth as the primary option since it's what users expect. Offer Whereby as a "No Setup Required" OHC-native video option for users who don't want a Zoom account.

**Design Doc**:
- **Trigger**: A customer books an "Online Lesson" service type via the scheduling widget.
- **Action**: OHC backend uses the business owner's connected Zoom account to generate a meeting link, or provisions a Whereby room. The link is automatically added to the calendar invite and the confirmation email.
- **User Experience**: The business owner does nothing. At the time of the meeting, both the owner and the customer simply click the link in their calendar/email to join.

**Implementation Prompt**:
Implement an OAuth flow for users to connect their Zoom accounts. Update the booking backend to call the Zoom API to generate a meeting URL upon successful booking. Store this URL and inject it into the confirmation emails and calendar invites generated by the "Customer Success" and "Operations" agents.

**Priority**: P2
**Estimated Scope**: Medium
