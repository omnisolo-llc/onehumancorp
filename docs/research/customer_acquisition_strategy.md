# Customer Acquisition Strategy for OHC

## Introduction
Building the best product is insufficient if our target personas (non-technical SMBs) do not know it exists. Traditional B2B SaaS acquisition channels (LinkedIn ads, whitepapers) will not reach our core audience. This document outlines a consumer-oriented, viral-first acquisition strategy.

## Phase 1: The "Show, Don't Tell" Strategy (Months 0-6)
### The Premise
Non-technical users do not want to "start a free trial" to see if a product works. They want to see the finished product instantly.
### The Mechanic
1. **The 'Magic Link' Ad:** Run TikTok/Reels ads targeting creators and side-hustlers. The ad says: "Reply to this with your Instagram handle, and our AI will build your store in 10 seconds."
2. **The Autonomous Hook:** The user replies. The OHC onboarding agent scrapes their public IG profile, generates a beautiful storefront with their recent posts as "products," and replies to their DM with the link: "Here is your new store. Claim it for free."
3. **The Aha Moment:** The user clicks the link and experiences zero friction. They are already logged into a fully populated, personalized environment.

## Phase 2: Community & Affiliate Moats (Months 6-12)
### The Premise
Small business owners trust other small business owners infinitely more than they trust software companies.
### The Mechanic
1. **The Transparent Ledger:** OHC will publish open data on the average revenue generated by "OHC Agents" across the platform (anonymized). "OHC users save an average of 14 hours a week using our unified inbox."
2. **The "Powered by OHC" Loop:** Every checkout flow on an OHC free-tier site will include a subtle, elegant badge: "This seamless checkout was built by OneHumanCorp's AI."
3. **The Creator Affiliate Program:** We will heavily incentivize micro-influencers (baking channels, DIY handyman channels) to use OHC for their own merch/services, offering them a percentage of the subscription revenue for anyone they refer.

## Phase 3: Vertical-Specific Partnerships (Months 12+)
### The Premise
To capture entire niches quickly, OHC must partner with the upstream suppliers of our target personas.
### The Mechanic
1. **The Supplier Integration:** Partner with wholesale suppliers (e.g., a major bakery supply distributor or a specialized craft supplier).
2. **The Bundle:** When a new business buys their initial stock from the supplier, they get "1 Year of OHC Premium" included. The OHC setup flow is pre-populated with the exact catalog they just purchased from the supplier.
