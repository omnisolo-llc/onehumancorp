# [Social Media] Buffer Integration for Radical Simplicity

## Title
🔍 Scout: Integrate Buffer for Unified Social Engagement and Scheduling

## Problem Statement
Small business owners like Maya (Home Baker) and Carlos (Handyman) are overwhelmed by the need to be present on Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok. They often miss customer comments because they have to jump between multiple complex apps. Missing a comment on a product post often means a lost sale. They need a single, simplified "Command Center" where they can see what customers are saying across all platforms and schedule updates without being a social media expert.

## Research Report
- **Tool**: Buffer
- **Target Persona**: Maya (Home Baker), Carlos (Handyman), Leo (Music Tutor)
- **Value Proposition**: Buffer is the industry leader in "Human-First" social media management. It prioritizes clarity and focus over complex marketing analytics, making it ideal for the OHC "Grandmother Test."
- **Key Advantages**:
  - **Unified Social Inbox**: Consolidates comments and messages from Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok into one manageable list.
  - **Intuitive Scheduling**: Allows owners to plan their week's posts in one sitting.
  - **AI Post Drafting**: Helps owners find the right words for their captions.
  - **Radical Simplicity**: Uses plain language like "Queue" and "Sent" instead of technical jargon.
- **Risks**: Standalone users may need a simplified way to handle secure connections.
- **Pricing**: Generous Free tier (3 channels) is perfect for OHC Free/Starter tiers.
- **Compatibility**: Works in both Cloud and Standalone modes.

## Design Doc
- **User Experience**:
  - The business owner navigates to the "Marketing" tab and clicks "Connect Social Media."
  - They securely connect their social accounts through a standard login process.
  - Once connected, OHC displays a "Social Feed" that pulls in recent activity from all platforms.
  - Owners can reply to comments directly within OHC.
  - The OHC AI agent drafts suggested replies based on the business's profile, which the owner can approve.
- **Visuals**: The interface looks like a standard chat app, hiding underlying complexity.

## Implementation Prompt
Implement a unified social media engagement feature using the Buffer integration. Create a UI that allows users to securely connect their Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok accounts. The system should fetch recent comments across these platforms and display them in a single feed. Allow users to compose and send replies directly from the OHC dashboard. Ensure the AI agent can suggest reply drafts to assist the owner.
