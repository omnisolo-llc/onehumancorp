# OHC AI Differentiation Manifesto

## Core Philosophy
We will offer **Invisible Autonomous Agents**, not just chat widgets. OHC users don't want to chat with an AI to do work; they want the AI to do the work for them.

## 5 Core AI Automations

1. **The Communicator (Auto-Reply)**
   *   **Value:** Saves 2-3 hours/day. Stops revenue leakage from missed messages.
   *   **Mechanism:** Multi-channel AI agent directly integrated into Meta/Twilio. Queries store memory (inventory, prices) to respond accurately.

2. **The Builder (One-Click Setup)**
   *   **Value:** Eliminates the 5-10 hour setup time. Turns a physical menu into a live site in 3 minutes.
   *   **Mechanism:** AI Vision processes user-uploaded photos to build categories, products, and descriptions automatically.

3. **The Marketer (Auto-Social)**
   *   **Value:** Removes marketing paralysis. Generates sales without the owner needing marketing knowledge.
   *   **Mechanism:** AI drafts weekly social posts and promotional emails. The owner just clicks "Approve".

4. **The Accountant (Receipt Scanner)**
   *   **Value:** Saves 5 hours/month on bookkeeping. Reduces tax season stress.
   *   **Mechanism:** User takes photos of receipts; AI Vision extracts vendor, amount, date, and tax category.

5. **The Analyst (Weekly SMS Briefing)**
   *   **Value:** Makes the owner feel smart and in control without logging into a dashboard.
   *   **Mechanism:** Every Sunday, the AI sends a plain-text SMS: "You made $500 this week. Top item: Vanilla Cake. Suggested action: Run a 10% promo on Chocolate Cake to move inventory. Reply YES to execute."
