# Market Sizing & Strategic Direction

## TAM (Total Addressable Market)
*   **US:** ~33 million small businesses. ~27 million are "non-employer" firms (solo operators).
*   **Global:** Hundreds of millions of micro-businesses.
*   **Opportunity:** A massive percentage of global micro-businesses still operate without a functional e-commerce or booking platform, relying purely on social media and cash/P2P transfers.

## Beachhead Market Strategy
*   **Initial Target:** "Service & Goods Hybrids" (e.g., Bakers, Crafters, Local Tutors).
*   **Why:** They struggle the most with current platforms. Shopify is too product-focused; Calendly is too service-focused. They need both.

## Geographic Expansion
*   **Phase 1:** US/UK/Canada (English).
*   **Phase 2:** LATAM (Spanish) & Brazil (Portuguese). High density of WhatsApp-driven micro-businesses. Need strong WhatsApp integration.
