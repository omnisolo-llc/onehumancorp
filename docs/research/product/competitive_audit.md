# Competitive Audit: SMB Platform Space

## Key Findings & Gaps

### Shopify
*   **Target:** Established E-commerce.
*   **Pros:** Enormous app ecosystem, robust backend.
*   **Cons:** Overwhelming for beginners. Setup is incredibly complex. No meaningful free tier. Mobile app is for management, not creation.
*   **AI:** Shopify Sidekick (chat assistant for the owner).

### Wix
*   **Target:** General website creation.
*   **Pros:** Good drag-and-drop, template variety.
*   **Cons:** Mobile editor is clunky. E-commerce is bolted on.
*   **AI:** Wix ADI (generates site based on Q&A). One-time use mostly.

### Squarespace
*   **Target:** Design-focused, portfolios, restaurants.
*   **Pros:** Beautiful templates.
*   **Cons:** Limited flexibility. Expensive. Weak AI.

### GoDaddy Airo
*   **Target:** Absolute beginners.
*   **Pros:** Very simple. AI branding tools.
*   **Cons:** Aggressive upselling. Shallow feature set. Poor reputation among serious sellers.

### OHC Opportunity
OHC must win on **Mobile-First Zero-Setup**. We cannot compete with Shopify's app store. We must compete on time-to-value: 10 minutes from mobile download to first sale, powered entirely by invisible AI.
