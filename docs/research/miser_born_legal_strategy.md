# Miser: Born Legal & Economic Protection Strategy

## Overview
OHC's "Born Legal" strategy ensures that every business started on the platform is compliant with regional laws and economic guardrails from day one. The Miser system is the economic realization of this strategy, protecting both OHC and the user from unpredictable infrastructure costs.

## Economic Guardrails
1. **Soft Quotas**: Unlike traditional platforms that hard-error when limits are reached, Miser implements soft quotas with "Grandmother Test" compliant messaging. Users are invited to grow, not blocked from working.
2. **Dynamic Steering**: By automatically routing simple tasks to Economy models, we ensure that users' budgets last longer, effectively providing more value per dollar spent.
3. **Lossless Compression**: Storage costs are minimized through automatic WebP conversion and resizing, ensuring that product photos do not bloat the user's storage footprint.

## Compliance and Sovereignty
In Standalone mode, Miser respects the data sovereignty of the user by performing all cost calculations locally and only syncing anonymized telemetry if the user explicitly opts in. This aligns with GDPR and CCPA principles of data minimization.

## ROI-Driven Reporting
The Miser dashboard does not just show "costs"; it shows "Efficiency" and "ROI". By translating technical metrics (tokens) into business metrics (ROI), we empower small business owners to make informed decisions about their AI usage.
