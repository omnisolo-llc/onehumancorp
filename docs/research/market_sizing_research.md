# TAM and Market Direction

## Total Addressable Market
- In the US, there are over 27.1 million non-employer businesses. Globally, it is estimated that there are over 330 million SMBs, with the vast majority being non-employer or micro-businesses.
- More than 25% of these businesses have no formal online presence, relying entirely on physical foot traffic or word-of-mouth.

## Beachhead Market
- **Primary Persona:** The Service-Based Solo-preneur (e.g., Carlos the Handyman, Maya the Baker).
- **Why:** Highest density of underserved users. They have immediate operational pain points (scheduling, payments, repetitive questions) but the lowest technical tolerance. They do not need complex eCommerce (like Shopify); they need a digital storefront that acts as a receptionist.

## Geographic Expansion
- **Phase 1:** US/Canada/UK (English).
- **Phase 2:** LATAM (Spanish/Portuguese). The LATAM market has a massive explosion of micro-entrepreneurship driven by mobile-first connectivity.
- **Phase 3:** MENA (Arabic).

## Vertical Expansion
- After establishing the horizontal "digital business card & receptionist" platform, OHC should deepen verticals starting with **Local Services** (integrated quoting, scheduling, and local SEO), followed by **Food & Beverage** (pickup orders, simple POS).

## Marketplace Opportunity
- OHC can aggregate local services into an "Etsy for Local Services" marketplace, allowing consumers to find trusted, AI-powered independent businesses in their area.
