# Issue Brief: Calendar Tools

### Title: Integrate Google Calendar
**Problem Statement:** Non-technical small business owners need a reliable solution in the calendar space. Currently, they spend hours manually performing tasks that Google Calendar could automate, leading to lost revenue and burnout.

**Research Report:** Google Calendar is evaluated as a candidate for integration. It offers 'Standard scheduling sync.'.
- **Pricing Estimate:** Free.
- **Cloud/Standalone Compatibility:** Works in both environments via standard authentication flows.
- **Advantages:** Ubiquitous, zero friction.
- **Risks:** Privacy concerns for some.

**Design Doc:**
- **Trigger:** A relevant business event occurs in OHC.
- **Action:** OHC integrates seamlessly with Google Calendar.
- **User Experience:** The owner simply clicks 'Connect Google Calendar' and authenticates without encountering complex configurations.

**Implementation Prompt:** Provide a seamless, plain-language integration for Google Calendar that allows non-technical users to utilize its calendar features natively within OHC.

**Priority:** P2
**Estimated Scope:** Medium
