# [RESEARCH] OneHumanCorp - End-to-End Business Journey Architecture

> **Reference**: Full research report and diagrams available at `.agent-task/report/task_output.md`

## Problem Statement
Small business owners (bakers, handymen, boutique owners, tutors, food cart operators) currently face a fragmented, overwhelming landscape of tools when trying to start or run their business online. They need to stitch together website builders, scheduling tools, payment gateways, and CRMs, which requires technical knowledge, time, and manual effort. They need a single, unified platform that allows them to go from "zero to live business in under 10 minutes" entirely from their phone, with AI handling the complexity invisibly.

## Scope
This issue outlines the architectural design for the complete end-to-end user journey across the OneHumanCorp platform, covering Acquisition, Onboarding, Activation, Retention, Revenue, and Referral for our key personas.
