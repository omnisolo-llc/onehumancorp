# Issue Brief: Sms Tools

### Title: Integrate Twilio
**Problem Statement:** Non-technical small business owners need a reliable solution in the sms space. Currently, they spend hours manually performing tasks that Twilio could automate, leading to lost revenue and burnout.

**Research Report:** Twilio is evaluated as a candidate for integration. It offers 'Developer standard SMS.'.
- **Pricing Estimate:** Pay per message.
- **Cloud/Standalone Compatibility:** Works in both environments via standard authentication flows.
- **Advantages:** Global reach, reliable.
- **Risks:** Requires technical setup, A2P 10DLC registration is painful.

**Design Doc:**
- **Trigger:** A relevant business event occurs in OHC.
- **Action:** OHC integrates seamlessly with Twilio.
- **User Experience:** The owner simply clicks 'Connect Twilio' and authenticates without encountering complex configurations.

**Implementation Prompt:** Provide a seamless, plain-language integration for Twilio that allows non-technical users to utilize its sms features natively within OHC.

**Priority:** P2
**Estimated Scope:** Medium
