# AI Weekly Business Insights

## Problem Statement
Business owners are overwhelmed by complex analytics dashboards. They don't want raw data; they want actionable advice.

## Research Report
*   **User Pain Points:** "Google Analytics is too complicated," "I don't know what these numbers mean for my business."
*   **Opportunity:** Replace complex dashboards with a weekly plain-text or audio summary from the AI, highlighting what worked and what to do next.

## Design Doc
*   **Engine:** Data aggregator that pulls weekly sales, traffic, and engagement metrics.
*   **Output:** LLM prompt that translates metrics into a short, encouraging, actionable summary.

## Implementation Prompt
Implement a feature that sends the business owner a weekly summary of their performance. The summary must be written in plain, encouraging language by the AI and provide 1-2 specific recommendations for the upcoming week.

## Priority
P3

## Estimated Scope
Small
