# Title: Integrate Mailchimp for Email Marketing

## Problem Statement
Small businesses struggle to re-engage past customers and announce new promotions without a structured email system.

## Research Report
**Advantages:** Massive template library, great deliverability rates.
**Risks:** Pricing scales steeply as subscriber count grows.
**Pricing:** Free up to 500 contacts, then $13/month.
**Cloud/Standalone Support:** Cloud and Standalone supported.

## Design Doc
A 'Campaigns' tab where business owners can select a pre-made template, select a customer segment, and click 'Send'.

## Implementation Prompt
Implement the integration with Mailchimp such that the business owner experiences A 'Campaigns' tab where business owners can select a pre-made template, select a customer segment, and click 'Send'.. Do not focus on technical intricacies but on seamless user experience. Ensure all acceptance criteria related to ease-of-use and reliability are met.
