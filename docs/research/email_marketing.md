# Title: Integrated Email Marketing Campaigns

## Problem Statement
Small businesses often have a customer list but don't know how to effectively communicate with them. Exporting contacts to a separate tool like Mailchimp is cumbersome and leads to fragmented data. They need a simple way to send newsletters or promotions directly to their customer base.

## Research Report
*   **Competitors:** Mailchimp, ConvertKit, Sendinblue.
*   **Ease of Use:** Needs a drag-and-drop builder with pre-designed templates suitable for various business types. List management should be automatic (e.g., anyone who buys a product is added to the list).
*   **Pricing:** Most offer free tiers up to a certain number of contacts (e.g., 500-1000), then scale up ($15-$50+/month).
*   **Reputation:** Mailchimp is well-known but can become expensive quickly.

## Design Doc
*   **Trigger:** User navigates to the "Marketing" tab and clicks "Create Campaign".
*   **Actions:** User selects a target audience segment, designs the email, and schedules or sends it.
*   **User View:** A dashboard showing campaign performance (open rates, click rates) and a simple email builder interface.

## Implementation Prompt
Build an email marketing module. It should tightly integrate with the existing customer CRM. Provide a basic email template builder and the ability to send bulk emails to segmented customer lists. Include basic analytics tracking (opens/clicks).
