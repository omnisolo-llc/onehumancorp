# Email Marketing Integration

## Problem Statement
Small business owners need an easy way to communicate with their customers about promotions, news, and updates. The solution must be straightforward enough for non-technical users while providing powerful capabilities like API-first embedded experiences.

## Evaluated Tools
We evaluated the following tools:
1. **Resend**: Selected for API-first embedded experiences. It provides an excellent developer experience and allows seamless integration of email sending capabilities directly into OHC without forcing users into complex email builder interfaces.
2. **Brevo**: Selected for all-in-one SMB solutions. It offers a comprehensive suite of marketing tools including email campaigns, SMS, and automation workflows.
3. **Klaviyo**: A strong contender for e-commerce, offering deep integrations with platforms like Shopify. However, its focus is heavily weighted towards advanced e-commerce automation rather than simple embedded SMB tools.
4. **Kit (formerly ConvertKit)**: Geared primarily towards creators and newsletter writers. While powerful for audience building, it may be too specialized and complex for general OHC small businesses.

## Key Recommendation
- **Resend** is the primary choice for deep, API-driven integration where OHC controls the UI and user experience.
- **Brevo** is recommended as an alternative for businesses needing a more comprehensive, all-in-one marketing suite outside of OHC's core offerings.
