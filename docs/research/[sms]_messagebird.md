# Issue Brief: International SMS Focus

## Title
Implement International SMS Focus for Small Business Owners

## Problem Statement
A business operating in Europe finds standard US-based SMS providers too expensive for local messaging.

## Research Report
MessageBird provides competitive international SMS routing.

**Persona Impact:** The European business owner can send crucial appointment reminders to local clients without paying exorbitant cross-border fees, keeping their operating costs low.

**Advantages:** Often better pricing and local carrier relationships outside of North America.

**Risks:** Less brand recognition in the US market.

**Pricing Estimate:** Competitive pay-per-message rates.

**Environment:** Cloud and Standalone supported.

## Design Doc
1.  **Provider Selection:** Seamlessly offer MessageBird as a cheaper alternative for non-US users during onboarding.

## Implementation Prompt
Evaluate MessageBird as an alternative routing option to ensure OHC can offer cost-effective SMS reminders to our international users.

## Priority
P2

## Estimated Scope
Medium

### Unique Considerations
MessageBird's alphanumeric sender ID feature (where the text comes from 'MARIASALON' instead of a phone number) should be heavily promoted to international users as a way to increase brand trust.
