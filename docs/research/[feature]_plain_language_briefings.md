# Plain-Language Business Briefings

## Problem Statement
Small business owners (like Priya the boutique owner) have access to analytics dashboards but don't know how to interpret the data. They are overwhelmed by charts showing "Traffic down 5%" and need actionable advice on what to do next. They need an AI advisor that translates data into simple, actionable insights.

## Research Report
Based on a deep competitor audit and SMB pain point analysis:
- **Pain Point #9**: "Lack of Actionable Insights" is a common frustration. Owners have data but lack the time or expertise to analyze it.
- **Competitor Gap**: Shopify, Wix, and Squarespace offer standard analytics dashboards. None offer proactive, plain-language insights generated by AI.
- **AI Differentiation**: The Business Advisory agent can synthesize complex analytics into simple daily/weekly SMS or push notifications (e.g., "You had 3 sales yesterday. The new cake is popular. You should post about it on Instagram today.").

## Design Doc
- **Core Entity**: `BusinessInsight` (generated by the AI advisor).
- **Key Relationships**: `BusinessInsight` links to `Tenant` and various metrics (e.g., `DailyRevenue`, `TopProducts`).
- **UI Wireframes/Flow (Mobile-First 375px)**:
  - **Dashboard Widget**: A prominent, conversational widget on the main dashboard showing the daily brief (e.g., "Good morning Priya! Yesterday was a great day...").
  - **Push Notification**: An SMS-style notification sent to the owner's phone.
  - **Action Buttons**: Inline buttons within the brief to take immediate action (e.g., a button to "Draft Instagram Post" next to the insight about a popular product).
- **AI Agent Integration**: The Business Advisory agent aggregates data from the Operations and Finance departments overnight and generates a personalized, conversational brief.

## Implementation Prompt
Develop the backend logic for the Business Advisory agent to aggregate daily metrics (revenue, traffic, top products) and use the LLM to generate a plain-language summary. Implement the delivery mechanism (push notification/SMS mock) and the frontend dashboard widget to display the brief. Ensure the brief includes actionable "next steps" that link to other platform features.
