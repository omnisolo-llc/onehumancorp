# Mobile-Only Optimized Redesign

## Title
Mobile-Only Optimized Redesign

## Problem Statement
Current SMB platforms are desktop-first, frustrating owners who try to manage their business on the go from their phone.

## Research Report
*   **Competitor Analysis:** Shopify and Wix have strong desktop experiences but mobile apps often feel like afterthoughts or are missing features.
*   **User Evidence:** Mobile gaps are a top pain point (42%). "Dashboards that require a laptop for basic inventory edits."
*   **OHC Differentiation:** 100% feature parity and optimized UX for 375px screens. No desktop required.

## Design Doc
*   **Architecture:** UI framework strictly prioritizing 375px viewport. Bottom navigation, touch-friendly targets, simplified typography.
*   **Mobile UX Flow (375px focus):** All core actions (inventory, orders, messaging) accessible within 2 taps from the home screen.
*   **AI Integration Points:** Proactive surfacing of relevant actions based on time of day or business events.

## Implementation Prompt
Audit and redesign the core application UI to ensure 100% functionality and an exceptional experience on a 375px mobile viewport. Adhere to the Visual Excellence Mandate (Glassmorphism, etc.).
