# Issue Brief: Social Media Tools

### Title: Integrate WhatsApp Business
**Problem Statement:** Non-technical small business owners need a reliable solution in the social media space. Currently, they spend hours manually performing tasks that WhatsApp Business could automate, leading to lost revenue and burnout.

**Research Report:** WhatsApp Business is evaluated as a candidate for integration. It offers 'Consolidates WhatsApp communication for LATAM/India regions.'.
- **Pricing Estimate:** Free to use, API costs per conversation.
- **Cloud/Standalone Compatibility:** Works in both environments via standard authentication flows.
- **Advantages:** High reach, easy for users.
- **Risks:** Relies on Meta approvals.

**Design Doc:**
- **Trigger:** A relevant business event occurs in OHC.
- **Action:** OHC integrates seamlessly with WhatsApp Business.
- **User Experience:** The owner simply clicks 'Connect WhatsApp Business' and authenticates without encountering complex configurations.

**Implementation Prompt:** Provide a seamless, plain-language integration for WhatsApp Business that allows non-technical users to utilize its social media features natively within OHC.

**Priority:** P2
**Estimated Scope:** Medium
