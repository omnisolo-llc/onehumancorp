# Issue Brief: Payment Tools

### Title: Integrate Stripe
**Problem Statement:** Non-technical small business owners need a reliable solution in the payment space. Currently, they spend hours manually performing tasks that Stripe could automate, leading to lost revenue and burnout.

**Research Report:** Stripe is evaluated as a candidate for integration. It offers 'Global standard for online payments.'.
- **Pricing Estimate:** 2.9% + 30c.
- **Cloud/Standalone Compatibility:** Works in both environments via standard authentication flows.
- **Advantages:** Best developer experience.
- **Risks:** High fees for some regions, strict account closures.

**Design Doc:**
- **Trigger:** A relevant business event occurs in OHC.
- **Action:** OHC integrates seamlessly with Stripe.
- **User Experience:** The owner simply clicks 'Connect Stripe' and authenticates without encountering complex configurations.

**Implementation Prompt:** Provide a seamless, plain-language integration for Stripe that allows non-technical users to utilize its payment features natively within OHC.

**Priority:** P2
**Estimated Scope:** Medium
