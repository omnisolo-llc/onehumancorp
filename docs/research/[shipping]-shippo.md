# Title: Integrate Shippo for Shipping & Logistics

## Problem Statement
Calculating shipping rates manually and buying labels at the post office is incredibly slow and prone to errors.

## Research Report
**Advantages:** Multi-carrier support from a single API, discounted rates.
**Risks:** Label generation errors require clear user feedback.
**Pricing:** $10/month or pay-as-you-go ($0.05/label).
**Cloud/Standalone Support:** Cloud and Standalone supported.

## Design Doc
A 'Fulfill Order' button that instantly shows the best shipping rate, prints a label, and emails the tracking number to the customer.

## Implementation Prompt
Implement the integration with Shippo such that the business owner experiences A 'Fulfill Order' button that instantly shows the best shipping rate, prints a label, and emails the tracking number to the customer.. Do not focus on technical intricacies but on seamless user experience. Ensure all acceptance criteria related to ease-of-use and reliability are met.

## Estimated Scope
