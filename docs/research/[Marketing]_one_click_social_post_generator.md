# One-Click Social Post Generator

## Problem Statement
Small business owners struggle with consistent marketing. Creating social media content is time-consuming and often requires skills they don't possess, leading to inactive profiles and low engagement.

## Research Report
*   **Competitor Analysis:** Many platforms offer basic integrations, but few provide native, AI-driven content generation tailored to the business's specific products.
*   **User Pain Points:** "I don't know what to post on Instagram," "It takes too long to write captions."
*   **Opportunity:** AI that automatically generates weekly social media posts (image + caption) based on new inventory or promotions.

## Design Doc
*   **Integration:** Connection to Instagram/Facebook APIs.
*   **Workflow:**
    1.  AI detects a new product added to the store.
    2.  AI generates 3 alternative posts (image variations + captions).
    3.  User clicks "Approve & Schedule."

## Implementation Prompt
Create a feature where the AI automatically generates social media content whenever a new product is added or a milestone is reached. The user should receive a notification with the generated content, allowing them to publish it with a single tap.
