# Issue Brief: Shipping Tools

### Title: Integrate Shippo
**Problem Statement:** Non-technical small business owners need a reliable solution in the shipping space. Currently, they spend hours manually performing tasks that Shippo could automate, leading to lost revenue and burnout.

**Research Report:** Shippo is evaluated as a candidate for integration. It offers 'Multi-carrier API.'.
- **Pricing Estimate:** Pay as you go.
- **Cloud/Standalone Compatibility:** Works in both environments via standard authentication flows.
- **Advantages:** Great API, easy to use.
- **Risks:** Support can be slow.

**Design Doc:**
- **Trigger:** A relevant business event occurs in OHC.
- **Action:** OHC integrates seamlessly with Shippo.
- **User Experience:** The owner simply clicks 'Connect Shippo' and authenticates without encountering complex configurations.

**Implementation Prompt:** Provide a seamless, plain-language integration for Shippo that allows non-technical users to utilize its shipping features natively within OHC.

**Priority:** P2
**Estimated Scope:** Medium
