# Issue Brief: Omnichannel AI Inbox (The Ambassador)

## Title
[Customer Success] Omnichannel AI Inbox (The Ambassador)

## Problem Statement
Small business owners (like Maya the Baker) experience severe communication overload (Pain Point #1). They manually respond to repetitive DMs (Instagram/WhatsApp) and emails ("Do you have vegan options?", "When are you open?"). This disjointed process costs valuable time and leads to missed sales opportunities.

## Proposed Solution
An Omnichannel AI Inbox that unifies DMs, SMS, and Emails into a single thread. It deploys "The Ambassador" agent—an invisible autonomous assistant that queries the business's embedded memory (`autodream_memories`) to automatically draft and, upon approval, send contextual replies.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Aggregates messages from Instagram DMs, SMS, and Email into a single inbox view.
- "The Ambassador" agent automatically drafts context-aware replies to incoming messages.
- Requires a 1-tap approval from the user before sending the AI-drafted reply.
- Full mobile-first management capabilities (Parity at 375px).
- Secure, multi-tenant isolation ensuring data privacy across businesses.
