# Title: Integrate Zoom for Video Conferencing

## Problem Statement
Manually creating Zoom links and sending them to clients for every online consultation is tedious and unprofessional.

## Research Report
**Advantages:** Universally recognized, highly reliable video quality.
**Risks:** Requires users to authenticate their Zoom accounts via OAuth.
**Pricing:** $15.99/month Pro plan.
**Cloud/Standalone Support:** Cloud and Standalone supported.

## Design Doc
When an online meeting is booked, a Zoom link is automatically generated and added to the calendar invite and confirmation email.

## Implementation Prompt
Implement the integration with Zoom such that the business owner experiences When an online meeting is booked, a Zoom link is automatically generated and added to the calendar invite and confirmation email.. Do not focus on technical intricacies but on seamless user experience. Ensure all acceptance criteria related to ease-of-use and reliability are met.

P2

Medium
