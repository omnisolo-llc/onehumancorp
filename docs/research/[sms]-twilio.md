# Title: Integrate Twilio for SMS & Notifications

## Problem Statement
Customers often miss email notifications, leading to no-shows or delayed order pickups. SMS is more immediate.

## Research Report
**Advantages:** Global reach, highly reliable delivery, programmable routing.
**Risks:** Compliance rules (10DLC) can be complex for small business owners to set up.
**Pricing:** ~$0.0079 per message.
**Cloud/Standalone Support:** Cloud and Standalone supported.

## Design Doc
Automated text messages sent directly to customers for order updates, appearing seamless without the business owner needing to write them manually.

## Implementation Prompt
Implement the integration with Twilio such that the business owner experiences Automated text messages sent directly to customers for order updates, appearing seamless without the business owner needing to write them manually.. Do not focus on technical intricacies but on seamless user experience. Ensure all acceptance criteria related to ease-of-use and reliability are met.

## Priority
P2

## Estimated Scope
Medium
