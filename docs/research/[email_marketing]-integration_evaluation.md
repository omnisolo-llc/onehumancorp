# Issue Brief: Email Marketing Tools

### Title: Integrate Mailchimp
**Problem Statement:** Non-technical small business owners need a reliable solution in the email marketing space. Currently, they spend hours manually performing tasks that Mailchimp could automate, leading to lost revenue and burnout.

**Research Report:** Mailchimp is evaluated as a candidate for integration. It offers 'Standard email marketing.'.
- **Pricing Estimate:** Free tier, $13/month.
- **Cloud/Standalone Compatibility:** Works in both environments via standard authentication flows.
- **Advantages:** Easy to use, great templates.
- **Risks:** Gets very expensive as list grows.

**Design Doc:**
- **Trigger:** A relevant business event occurs in OHC.
- **Action:** OHC integrates seamlessly with Mailchimp.
- **User Experience:** The owner simply clicks 'Connect Mailchimp' and authenticates without encountering complex configurations.

**Implementation Prompt:** Provide a seamless, plain-language integration for Mailchimp that allows non-technical users to utilize its email marketing features natively within OHC.

**Priority:** P2
**Estimated Scope:** Medium
