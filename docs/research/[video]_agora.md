# Scout: Video Conferencing (Agora)

## Title
Integrated In-App Video Consulting 🎥 (Agora SDK Integration)

## Problem Statement
Service providers like Leo (the music tutor) and Priya (the boutique owner offering personal shopping) currently rely on external tools like Zoom or Google Meet. This forces their customers to download separate apps, create accounts, and navigate away from the OHC experience. An integrated video solution is needed to keep the consulting experience native, branded, and frictionless.

## Research Report
- **Goal**: Evaluate Agora Video SDK as the primary real-time engagement engine for OHC.
- **Features evaluated**:
  - **Flutter SDK**: Comprehensive support for native mobile (iOS/Android) and Web video.
  - **White-Labeling**: Complete control over the UI/UX; the video call happens inside the OHC app.
  - **Screen Sharing & Recording**: Essential for tutors (Leo) to share sheet music or record lessons.
  - **Virtual Backgrounds**: Professional look for home-based business owners.
- **Benefits for OHC users (Non-technical)**:
  - No "Zoom Fatigue" or confusion with external links; just a "Join Call" button in the OHC app.
  - Professional, branded experience that enhances their business image.
  - Secure and private; no need for customers to share their personal email/phone with video providers.
- **Integration Risks**:
  - Real-time video is resource-intensive; requires careful optimization for low-end mobile devices.
  - Token-based authentication (dynamic App IDs) requires a secure backend generator.
- **Pricing**: "Freemium" model with 10,000 free minutes per month—perfect for starting SMBs.
- **Cloud vs Standalone**: Native support for Cloud mode. For Standalone, the Agora SDK runs directly within the Flutter desktop/mobile shell, with the OHC local backend managing session tokens.

### Persona Pain Point Summary
| Persona | Pain Point | Solution via Agora Integration |
|---------|------------|--------------------------------|
| **Leo (Tutor)** | Students struggle to find their Zoom links in their email. | Leo's student opens the OHC app and taps "Join Lesson" directly from their dashboard. |
| **Priya (Boutique)**| Wants to show a customer a specific fabric detail via video. | High-definition, low-latency video allows Priya to show product details clearly without stuttering. |

## Design Doc
- **Component**: `IntegratedConsultationService`
- **Responsibilities**:
  - Generate Agora dynamic tokens via the OHC backend.
  - Provide a `VideoCallWidget` in the Flutter app that handles camera/mic permissions and stream lifecycle.
  - Log session duration for the Finance department (for Leo's billing).
- **User Experience**:
  - A "Join Room" button appears on the dashboard 5 minutes before a scheduled booking.
  - Simple in-call controls: Mute, Flip Camera, Share Screen, End Call.

## Implementation Prompt
"Integrate the Agora Video SDK into OHC. Implement a token generation service in `src/server/services/video/` and a set of Flutter widgets in `src/app/lib/screens/consultation/` that provide a full-screen video call experience. Ensure the integration handles network fluctuations gracefully and provides clear visual feedback for connection status. Acceptance criteria: Two users can join a unique 'Room ID' generated by an OHC booking and see/hear each other with low latency."
