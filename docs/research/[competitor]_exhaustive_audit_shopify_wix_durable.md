# Exhaustive Competitor Audit: Shopify vs. Wix vs. Durable (2024-2025)

## 1. Shopify: The Legacy Standard

### 1.1 Onboarding & UX
- **Time to Live**: 45–90 minutes for a "real" store.
- **Friction Points**:
  - Complexity of "Theme Liquid" for custom changes.
  - Mandatory setup of shipping zones, tax rules, and payout accounts before launch.
  - Dashboard is a "Sidebar Nightmare" with 20+ top-level menu items.
- **Mobile Experience**: The Shopify app is strong for order management but frustrating for building. Users often feel "trapped" by small-screen limitations for layout changes.

### 1.2 AI Strategy: Sidekick
- **Interface**: Chat sidebar in the admin panel.
- **Capability**: Can change theme colors, generate reports, and draft blog posts.
- **Weakness**: It is **Reactive**. It waits for the user to ask "How many sales did I have?" It does not proactively say "I noticed sales are down, I've drafted a promotion for you."

---

## 2. Wix: The Design Heavyweight

### 1.1 Onboarding & UX
- **Time to Live**: 20–40 minutes via Wix ADI.
- **Friction Points**:
  - The "Editor" is notorious for being laggy on non-pro laptops.
  - "Pixel-perfect" drag-and-drop leads to broken mobile layouts if the user isn't careful.
  - Upselling is aggressive throughout the onboarding flow.
- **Mobile Experience**: "Wix Owner" app is functional for CRM but the site editor is separate and limited.

### 1.2 AI Strategy: Aria & Wix AI
- **Interface**: Conversational assistant during setup and in-editor.
- **Capability**: Excellent at "vibe coding"—generating sections based on text prompts.
- **Weakness**: It remains a **Design Tool AI**. It helps you build the store, but it doesn't help you *run* the store autonomously.

---

## 3. Durable: The Speed Specialist

### 1.1 Onboarding & UX
- **Time to Live**: **30 seconds.** This is the benchmark OHC must meet.
- **Friction Points**:
  - The generated sites are often "thin"—generic copy and stock images that feel unbranded.
  - Limited deep business logic (e.g., complex multi-variant products).
  - No mobile-native app; browser-only management.
- **Mobile Experience**: Good responsive dashboard but lacks the "native feel" and "lock-screen action" capability of OHC.

### 1.2 AI Strategy: The "Launch" Agent
- **Interface**: Conversational setup.
- **Capability**: Generates name, logo, website, and basic copy in one go.
- **Weakness**: **Operational Decay**. Once the site is live, the AI mostly stops helping. There is no autonomous lead conversion or proactive operations.

## 6. Critical User Journey (CUJ) Comparison: Adding a New Product

### Shopify Journey
1. Open Admin -> Products -> Add Product.
2. Type Title, Description, SKU, Price, Weight, SEO.
3. Upload images.
4. Set inventory levels.
5. Save.
6. (Optional) Go to Marketing -> Create Social Post -> Copy/Paste details.
**Total Time: 8-12 minutes.**

### OHC Journey
1. Open App -> Tap "+" -> Take Photo.
2. AI identifies product, suggests Price and Description.
3. User taps "Confirm".
4. **Agent Action**: Automatically drafts 3 Instagram captions and 1 GEO update.
5. User taps "Approve All" on Lock Screen.
**Total Time: 45 seconds.**

## 7. Strategic Conclusion: The "Empty Room" Problem
Most competitors give the user a "Room" (a website) and a "Manual" (a dashboard).
OHC gives the user a **"Teammate"** who is already working in the room. This shift in the mental model is our greatest competitive advantage.
