# How to Get Paid

Setting up payments is the most important part of your business! We make sure you can accept money from your customers securely and quickly.

## Accepting Online Payments

If you sell through your website, customers will pay using their credit or debit card during checkout. To turn this on:

1. Go to the **Payments** section in your main menu.
2. Click **Connect Bank Account**.
3. Follow the steps to link your checking account. This is where your sales money will be deposited.
4. Once connected, your store will automatically let customers enter their card details when they buy something.

## Accepting In-Person Payments

If you have a physical shop or sell at markets, you can take payments in person using our mobile app.

1. Open the One Human Corp app on your phone.
2. Tap the **Take Payment** button.
3. Enter the amount or select a product.
4. Your customer can tap their card or phone against your phone to pay securely!

## When Do I Get My Money?

After a customer pays, the money will show up as "Pending" in your dashboard. It usually takes 1 to 2 business days for the money to officially arrive in your connected bank account.

If you have any questions about a specific payment, you can always ask your AI Support Agent!
