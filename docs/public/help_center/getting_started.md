# Getting Started with One Human Corp

Welcome to One Human Corp! We are excited to help you start, run, and grow your small business online.

## Setting Up Your Store

Getting your online store up and running is fast and easy. Follow these steps:

1. **Pick a Template**: Choose a look for your website. We have templates for all types of businesses, whether you sell physical items, digital goods, or services.
2. **Name Your Business**: Enter your business name. If you are stuck, our AI assistant can help you brainstorm!
3. **Add Your First Product**: Upload a photo of what you sell, give it a name, and set a price.
4. **Choose a Web Address**: Decide what your customers will type in their browser to find you. You can use our free ".ohc.app" address, or connect one you already own (like yourname.com).

## Taking Your Store Live

When you have added your products and chosen your look, click the **Publish** button. Our system will immediately make your website visible to the world.

## Need More Help?

If you ever get stuck, look for the "?" icon on any screen, or click the **Help** button to chat with our Support Agent. It is available 24/7 and knows everything about running your business on our platform!
