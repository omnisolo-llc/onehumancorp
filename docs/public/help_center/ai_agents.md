# Using Your AI Helpers

Think of AI Agents as your digital employees. They work 24/7 to help you run your business, answer customer questions, and handle repetitive tasks.

## Your Support Agent

Your Support Agent is your right-hand assistant. It lives in the chat bubble on your screen. You can ask it to:

* Explain how a feature works.
* Find a specific order for you.
* Check your sales numbers for the week.

Just type your question in plain English, and it will give you a helpful answer immediately.

## Customer-Facing Agents

You can also turn on AI Agents that talk directly to your customers!

For example, you can set up a **Website Greeter Agent**. When a customer visits your online store, this agent can pop up, say hello, and answer questions about your products, shipping times, or return policy.

To turn this on:
1. Go to the **AI Team** section in your menu.
2. Find the **Customer Support Agent** and click **Hire**.
3. It will automatically learn everything about your store and start helping your shoppers!

## Are AI Agents Safe?

Yes! Your AI Agents only know what you tell them and what is in your store. They will never make up prices, give away your private business information, or do things without your permission.
